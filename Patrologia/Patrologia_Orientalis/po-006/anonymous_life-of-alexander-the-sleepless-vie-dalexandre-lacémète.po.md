For me, however, there is need, as I believe, of the same power, so that I may attempt to express his excellent deeds according to their merit, both by the imitation of his virtue and by words. Therefore, with the help of the most benevolent God, I will explain what kind of man this was, of whom the discourse is, and whence that method and manner of his exercise came; if indeed it is fitting for all who have been heaped with heavenly benefits by the propitious Divinity to recognize His beneficence, to be delighted by His works, to preach His praises, and with the Apostle to burst forth into these voices: O the depth of the riches of the wisdom and knowledge of God! And in a short time he saw how weak and unstable all human things are, and how all worldly glory quickly flows away like the flower of the hay, and therefore, despising these sublunary things as fleeting, he turned the eyes of his mind to the goods of another and far better life. As soon as he heard this and sincerely believed it, he immediately poured out both his patrimony and his military goods—and they were not small, as he was an excellent prefectorian endowed with remarkable temperance—upon the beggars and the poor. For he desired, because of the proposed hope, to withdraw far from his country, relatives, and friends, so that he might be enrolled in the family of Christ alone. For he heard that in Syria there were monasteries of holy men whose conduct was excellent; and when he had generously completed the journey, he was admitted with many prayers into the monastery of the most religious archimandrite Elias, who was famous in those regions for brotherly love, liturgy, and the sacred offices established by him among the brothers.

But the servant of God Alexander, fervent in spirit, constantly hearing that voice of the Lord forbidding one to be anxious even for the morrow, he who provides for many sparrows, was troubled and disturbed in mind, nor could he contain himself any longer. Nor was it right, indeed, that he should speak of these things at the very beginning: for it was necessary that he first be instructed in all the spiritual armor, which is the psalter.

51. And thus the servant of God Alexander is dismissed alone, as they thought. But he was not alone. For he had Christ with him. But as he was dismissed, all the Brothers gathered together, as if called by a trumpet, and on that very day the accustomed rule was kept in the divine ministry, and so all were affected as if they had suffered nothing harsh: nay, rather as if they had found a spiritual treasure, so they exulted: they were even progressing in the Lord, with many other brothers joined to them. For if anyone should wish to travel through the whole world, and not only the Roman empire, but also to go to the provinces of the barbarians, he will find the disciples of this Saint flourishing everywhere. For then they themselves built the illustrious coenobium of the Acoemetae, and many other and greatest ones. For as the sun in the sky, so these are manifest to all.

There, therefore, we will break off the discourse, where he finished his life. This blessed man, having struggled for fifty years, without any intermission suffered straits, persecutions, nakedness, hunger, thirst, but in these he always rejoiced, teaching the word of truth with liberty, and gathering a great multitude of men, and offering them to Christ; which his disciples do even to this very day. When in these things, therefore, his virtue had always shone forth, at length he arrived at a blessed end, free from cares and quiet from labors; and when he had slept holily, he was buried in the borders of Bithynia, in a place which is called Gomon.

54. And we indeed have shown his brotherly charity and beneficence and have related what we ourselves saw, according to our own weakness, following only the truth. We hope, however, by divine providence, that there will be others who, inspired by the holy Spirit, endowed with greater knowledge than we, will explain these things more eloquently, for the edification and utility of those who wish to embrace this way of living. May it happen for us, that we may be made worthy disciples of him, and by his prayers may attain those things which he now enjoys, by the grace and indulgence of our Lord Jesus Christ; to him be glory for ever and ever, Amen.
