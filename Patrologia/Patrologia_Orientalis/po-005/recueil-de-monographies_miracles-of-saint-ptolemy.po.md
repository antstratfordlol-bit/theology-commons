May He accept our prayers and our fasts, may He receive our offerings, and may our supplications be pleasing to Him. May He direct us in good works and keep away from us the evils of trial and adversity. May He feed our orphans and take care of our widows; may He grant continence to our young men, health to men of mature age, and strength to our old men. May He cause our children to grow up in the dispositions that please Him. May He grant rest to the souls of our fathers and our brothers and all those who have died before us in the orthodox faith. May He make us firm on the rock of the faith, confessing His name, and victorious through the divine grace that was conferred upon us by baptism.
