### HISTORY OF THE ROMAN SAINTS (ROMAOS)

### SONS OF VALENTINIAN (VALENTIOS): MAXIMUS AND DOMETIUS (DOMATRIOS)

2. Monastic vocation. - When they had gone to Nicaea and had been blessed by these (holy) Fathers, they departed from there and went to the land of Palestine, near the father Agabus the solitary, who was from Tarsus of Cilicia and dwelt on this mountain which is near the sea. When Saint Agabus saw them, he received them with joy; they revealed their thoughts to him and, when he saw their love for God, he immediately gave them the monastic habit and they dwelt near him.

5. A short time later, Emperor Theodosius and the queen, sister of these holy brothers, heard of their fame. The queen with her mother - who was also the mother of these saints - set out and came to them with their imperial habits (τάξις). When the saints learned of their arrival, they went to meet them, and when the queens saw the change in their beauty (which they had had in the imperial palace), from grief, they fell to the ground as if dead. The blessed ones made the sign of the holy cross over them and they awoke as if from a sleep; they rose * with fear and with many tears; they kissed the feet of the saints, remained near them for a few days, and returned to the imperial city.

As for him, he was full of compassion for the one who was swallowed and defeated; he approached them and said: "See their wickedness against one another." And he took with his hands the one that was swallowed, he pulled it from the belly of the one that was devouring it and he carried it to a certain distance so that they could not find each other again. As for me, when I saw that he was saving the snakes from one another, I was like petrified. The saint returned near me and we worked on the palms while confessing and praising Our Lord Jesus Christ.
