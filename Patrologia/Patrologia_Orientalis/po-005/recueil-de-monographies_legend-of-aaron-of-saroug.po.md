# Legend of Aaron of Saroug

**Author**: Recueil de monographies  
**Series**: PO Tome 5  
**Columns**: pp. 702–750  

[p. 702] {#po.5.p702}
ܬܘܒ ܬܫܟܝܬܐ ܕܩܕܝܫܐ ܘܥܢܘܝܐ ܓܡܝܪܐ ܡܪܝ ܐܗܪܘܢ ܢܨܝܚܐ. ܡܶܟܬܒܐ ܕܝܢ
NEXT, THE HISTORY OF THE SAINT AND PERFECT ASCETIC MAR AARON (AHROUN) THE ILLUSTRIOUS; IT WAS WRITTEN BY HIS DISCIPLE PAUL. MAY HIS PRAYER BE WITH US.
ܠܦܘܠܘܣ ܬܠܡܝܕܗ. ܨܠܘܬܗ ܥܡܢ. ܐܚ̈ܝ. ܒܟܠ ܕܪ̈ܝܢ ܘܒܟܠܙܒܢ ܢܨܚܝܢ ܙܕܝܩ̈ܐ ܪ̈ܚܡܘ̈ܗܝ ܕܐܠܗܐ. ܘܐܟܡܐ ܕܡܝܬܪ ܟܘܟܒܐ ܡܢ ܟܘܟܒܐ ܒܫܘܒܚܐ. ܗܟܢܐ ܡܝܬܪ ܙܕܝܩܐ ܡܢ ܙܕܝܩܐ. ܘܠܐ ܠܢ ܕܢܝܬܐ ܠܡܨܥܬܐ. ܥܘܗܕܢܗ ܕܢܨܝܚܐ ܡܪܝ ܐܗܪܘܢ. ܗܟܢܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܫܘܪܝܐ
1. Exordium. - My brothers, in all generations and in all epochs shine just men, friends * of God, and just as one star excels another in brightness, so one just man excels another. We must produce (here) the memory of the illustrious Mar Aaron (Ahroun). Here is the beginning of his history.
ܕܬܫܥܝܬܗ܀ ܐܝܬ ܗܘܐ ܓܒܪܐ ܚܕ ܒܣܪܘܓ ܡܕܝܢܬܐ ܕܚܠ ܠܐܠܗܐ. ܘܫܡܗ ܝܐܢܝ. ܘܫܡܐ ܕܐܢܬܬܗ ܐܢܐܣܬܛܣܝܐ. ܘܐܝܬܝܗܘܢ ܗܘܘ ܟܪ̈ܝܣܛܝܢܐ ܦܪ̈ܝܪܐ. ܘܐܝܬ ܗܘܐ ܠܗܘܢ ܢܟܣ̈ܐ ܘܥܘܬܪܐ ܣܓܝ. ܘܨܒܐ ܐܠܗܐ ܘܗܘܐ ܠܗܘܢ ܒܪܐ ܗܢܐ. ܕܥܠܘܗܝ ܐܝܬܝܗ̇ ܬܫܥܝܬܢ. ܘܟܕ ܐܬܡܛܝ ܠܡܦܘܚܬܐ ܕܚܡܫ ܫܢ̈ܝܢ. ܐܝܬ ܗܘܐ ܠܐܒܗ̈ܘܗܝ ܩ̈ܝܢܐ ܘܥܢܐ ܣܓܝ ܕܛܒ. ܒܟܦܪܐ ܪܒܐ ܕܣܪܘܓ. ܘܒܚܕ ܡܢ ܝܘ̈ܡܝܢ. ܐܬܐ ܚܕ ܡܢ
2. His birth. - There was in the city of Saroug a pious man named John (Iani); his wife was named Anastasia; they were true Christians; they had goods and much wealth. God permitted that they should have the son whose history we are writing. When he reached the age of five years - his parents had many goods and many flocks of sheep at Kefrà Rabâ of Saroug - one of their shepherds came one day to seek some food;
ܪ̈ܥܘܬܐ ܕܝܠܗܘܢ ܕܢܣܒ ܣܝܒܪܬܐ ܕܡܕܡ. ܘܢܩܦ ܠܗ ܗܘ ܩܕܝܫܐ ܡܪܝ ܐܗܪܘܢ. ܘܐܡܪ ܠܗܘܢ. ܕܐܙܠ ܐܢܐ ܥܡܟ ܨܝܕ ܥܢܐ. ܘܐܡܪܘ ܐܒܗܘ̈ܗܝ. ܠܐ ܒܪܝ ܠܐ ܬܙܠ. ܡܛܠ ܕܚܘܡܐ ܡܘܩܕ ܫܘܦܪܐ ܘܡܚܝܡܐ ܓܘܢܐ ܕܐܦܝ̈ܐ. ܡܛܠ ܕܐܬܪܐ ܗܘ ܚܡܝܡܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܘܝܒܝܫܐ. ܛܠܝܐ ܕܝܢ ܣܓܝ ܥܨܐ ܠܐܒܗܘ̈ܗܝ. ܐܒܘܗܝ ܕܝܢ ܣܓܝ ܟܪܝܬ ܠܗܘܢ. ܘܠܐ ܒܥܐ ܕܢܥܕܨܝܘܗܝ. ܘܐܘܬܒܗ ܥܠ ܟܝܘܕܢܝܐ ܘܝܕܪܗ ܥܡܟ ܪܥܝܐ. ܘܩܘܝ ܠܘܬ ܪ̈ܥܘܬܐ ܬܪ̈ܝܢ ܝܘ̈ܡܝܢ܀
this saint Mar Aaron attached himself to him and said to him: 'I am going with you near the flock.' His parents said to him: 'No, my son, you will not go there, for the heat burns the beauty and blackens the colors of the face, because that region is arid and dried up.' The child was tormenting his parents much, and the father, very pained, but not wanting to make him suffer, sat him on a mule and let him leave with the shepherd. He remained two days with the shepherds.

3. He enters the monastery. - Now, in that region, there was a monastery, called the monastery of the Simotô (treasures?). On the third day, he saw this monastery and asked the (shepherds): "What is this?" The shepherds answered him: "It is the (dwelling) place of the perfect and also of holy men." He said to one of his servants: "Lead me to this place, for I greatly desire to see it." The (servant) took him and led him to the monastery; they arrived (there) at the ninth hour and he was blessed by the solitaries who were there. He then said to his servant: "I greatly desire to dwell near these holy men; go to the flock, and tomorrow, come and fetch me." The servant began to laugh at him * and said to him: "What do you want (to do) here? Is it to pray?" - Finally, his servant obeyed him and went to the flock. This saint remained all that night near the solitaries

ܗܘ ܕܝܢ ܩܕܝܫܐ ܩܘܝ ܒܗܘ ܠܠܝܐ ܠܘܬ ܝܚܝ̈ܕܝܐ. ܘܢܝܚ ܩܠܐ ܕܬܫܡܫ̈ܬܐ ܘܐܦ ܕܨܠܘ̈ܬܐ. ܥܕܡܐ ܕܒܡܛܝ ܥܕܢܐ ܕܠܠܝܐ ܘܕܨܦܪܐ܀
and he heard the chanting of the offices and also the prayers until the end of the night and the dawn.

4. There was in this monastery a pious superior, a perfect and holy man, who greatly feared the Lord. The holy Mar Aaron knelt before the superior and said to him: "I ask, lord, to dwell with you and to be your disciple." The monks laughed at him and said: "What! This one wants to be a monk!" The superior prophesied about him under (the inspiration of) the Holy Spirit and said: "Blessed are the parents who have begotten this child; I tell you: many men will enter the kingdom of heaven thanks to this child."
ܒܝܕ ܗܢܐ ܛܠܝܐ. ܘܥܕ ܗܢܘܢ ܡܡܠܠܝܢ. ܐܬܐ ܥܒܕܗ ܕܩܕܝܫܐ ܘܐܡܪ ܠܗ. ܩܘܡ ܢܐܙܠ ܠܥܢܐ. ܗܘ ܕܝܢ ܩܕܝܫܐ ܦܢܝ ܠܗ. ܕܙܠ ܐܡܪ ܠܡܪ̈ܝܟ. ܕܠܐ ܠܘܬܟܘܢ ܐܬܐ ܐܢܐ. ܘܠܐ ܥܡܟ ܐܙܠ ܐܢܐ. ܘܕܘܡܪܐ ܪܒܐ ܐܚܕܗ ܠܪܥܝܐ ܘܐܡܪ ܠܗ. ܕܡܢܐ ܐܢܝܢ ܡܠܐ ܗܠܝܢ ܕܡܢܟ ܫܡܥ ܐܢܐ. ܩܘܡ ܬܐ ܠܟ ܢܐܙܠ ܠܘܬ ܐܒܘܟ. ܘܡܚܕܐ ܐܝܟ ܕܨܒܐ ܐܢܬ ܥܒܕ. ܐܡܪ ܠܗ ܗܘ ܩܕܝܫܐ. ܠܐ ܬܬܠܐ ܒܣܓܝ̈ܐܬܐ. ܙܠ ܠܘܬ ܡܪ̈ܝܝܟ ܘܐܡܪ ܠܗܘܢ. ܕܐܢܐ ܠܘܬܟܘܢ ܠܐ ܐܬܐ ܐܢܐ. ܡܟܝܠ ܘܠܥܠܡ. ܘܩܡ ܒܟܝܐ ܟܕ
5. While they were speaking, the saint's servant came and said to him: "Come, let us go to the flock." The saint answered him: "Go tell your masters that I am not going to them and that I am not leaving with you." The shepherd was seized with deep astonishment and said to him: "What do these words mean that I hear you say? Rise, come, let us go to your father; then do what you want." This saint said to him: "Do not lament any longer, go to your masters and tell them (on my behalf): I am no longer coming to you, neither now nor ever." The servant, weeping bitterly, went to his masters who

ܒܟܐ ܡܪܝܪܐܝܬ܂. ܘܐܙܠ ܠܩܐ ܡܕ̈ܘܗܝ. ܘܫܰܐܠܘܗܝ ܕܐܝܟܿܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܐܗܪܘܢ. ܗܼܘ ܕܝܢ ܐܫܬܥܝ ܠܗܘܢ ܡܕܡ ܕܐܡܼܪ ܩܕܝܫܐ. ܟܕ ܫܡܥܘ ܕܝܢ ܒܟܝܼܘ ܒܟܬܐ ܪܒܬܐ.
asked him: "Where is Aaron?" He told them what the saint had said; at these words, they wept bitterly.

6. His parents set out; they took a large sum of money and went to this monastery where they were blessed by the saints who inhabited it. * His father said to him: "My son, if it is because of the work and the fatigue (caused by) the flocks that you are angry with us, leave the flocks and come rest at home." Then the saint said to them: "Do not torment yourselves and do not weep, for I no longer wish to return to you." They were seized with great pain on account of the blessed one who said to them: "Give me the inheritance that is due to me from the house, because I have seen the true light and I want to dwell in it; for I have known the true way, that which is proposed to all those who love Christ." Then they made the division and gave him what was due to him from the inheritance of the house. He took this inheritance and gave it to the solitaries who were there. His parents turned back and went away full of sadness. The saint remained for ten years with these solitaries; he learned the chanting of the psalms and the entire rule of the pure conduct of the monks.
ܗܘ̈ܠܠܐ ܕܡܙܡܘܪ̈ܐ. ܘܟܠܗ ܛܟܣܐ ܕܕܘܒܪܐ ܢܟܦܐ ܕܕܝܪ̈ܝܐ. ܘܡܚܕܐ ܒܪܟ ܩܕܡ ܐܚܘܬܐ ܟܠܗ̇ ܘܐܡܪ. ܐܒܗ̈ܝ ܘܐܚܝ: ܒܥܐ ܐܢܐ ܡܢܟܘܢ
7. He chooses the solitary life. - He then knelt before the whole community and said: "My fathers and my brothers, I beseech you,

ܨܠܘ ܥܠܝ. ܘܡܚܕܐ ܗܼܢܘܢ ܝܚܝ̈ܕܝܐ ܡܢ ܚܕ ܦܘܡܐ ܩܥܘ ܘܐܡ̣ܪܘ ܠܘܬܗ ܫܘܝܐܝܬ. ܙܠ ܐܚܘܢ ܒܫܠܡܐ ܡܪܢ ܢܗܘܐ ܥܡܟ ܘܢܨܒܪܟܟ ܐܝܟ ܨܒܝܢܟ. ܘܢܦܩ ܠܗ ܛܘܒܢܐ ܡܢ ܨܝܕܝܗܘܢ. ܘܣܠܩ ܠܗ ܠܪ̈ܝܫܐ ܕܛܘܪܐ. ܘܐܫܟܚ ܬܡܢ ܫܩܝܦܐ ܚܕ. ܕܠܡܪܬܐ ܗܘܬ ܒܗ ܐܪܝܐ. ܘܥܼܠ ܘܥܼܡܪ ܒܗ ܫܢ̈ܝܐ ܬܠܬܝܢ. ܟܕ ܡܕܡ ܠܐ ܐܟܠ ܗܘܐ ܢܨܝܚܐ.. ܐܠܐ ܡܢ ܥܩܪ̈ܐ ܘܦܐܪ̈ܐ ܕܗܽܘ ܛܘܪܐ. ܘܡܢ ܒܬܪ ܗܠܝܢ.. ܢܦܩ ܡܢ ܕܢܐܙܠ ܢܚܙܐ ܐܢ ܩܝܡܝܢ ܝܚܝ̈ܕܝܐ ܗܠܝܢ ܕܐܪܦܝ܂. ܐܘ ܠܐ. ܘܟܕ ܐܙܠ. ܐܫܟܚ ܐܢܘܢ
pray for me." Immediately these solitaries with one voice cried out and said to him together: "Go in peace, O our brother, our Lord will be with you and will bless you as you wish." The saint left them; he climbed to the top of the mountain and found there a cave where a lioness was lodging; he entered it and lived there for thirty years.
