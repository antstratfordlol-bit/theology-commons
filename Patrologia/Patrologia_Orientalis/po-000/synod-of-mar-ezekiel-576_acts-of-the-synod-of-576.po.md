From the acts of the synod held in the year 576 under the presidency of Ezechiel the Catholicos. CHAPTER ONE. CONCERNING THE MESSALIANS. It has been heard in the Synod that there are men who, dressed in the lying habit of monks and ascetics, pervert the minds of the faithful and capture women burdened by sins, boldly asserting that there is no sin in acts committed wickedly. These men remove equity entirely from the midst; they despise the Mass, fasting, prayer, and everything by which God is reconciled to sinners, and they demonstrate by their words that neither will the good inherit reward, nor the impious torments, and they persist in this impudence, to which there is no equal; sometimes they draw to themselves, by flattery, husbands away from their wives, wives away from their husbands, or widows still in the vigor of youth, or virgins, whether already betrothed or not yet, and capture their minds

and lead them around with them to satisfy their great uncleanness and lasciviousness; and they openly resist the Holy Spirit, the legislator, and contend with Him, for which reason many crimes and great offenses are committed.

Concerning these and similar things, the Synod decreed and defined: If they belong to those who have received the priestly rank and were established in the ministry of the Church: let them be utterly rejected and removed from the entire sacred order of the ministry of the holy Church and from all association with the faithful; but a period of one year shall be granted to them for penance, so that they may be admitted to communicate in the sacred mysteries and be permitted to mingle with the faithful. They shall not, however, be able to be promoted again to ecclesiastical ranks. If, however, religious or lay faithful have committed such filthiness: six months shall be granted to them for repentance, and then they shall communicate with the faithful.
