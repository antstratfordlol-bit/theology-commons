Praise to you, Lord, who created us by your own motion in the beginning. / Praise to you, Lord, who called us your living image and likeness. / Praise to you, Lord, who enriched us with the gift of liberty and intellect. / Praise to you, just Father, whose love deigned to create us. / Praise to you, holy Son, who, having put on our flesh, redeemed us. / Praise to you, living Spirit, who enriched us with your charisms. / Praise to you, Lord, who gathered us, snatched from the error of idols. /

O you, who know the thoughts of all men and search the secrets of hearts, you also know our weakness: have mercy on us. Christ, who by your fasting justified our nature, grant us that we may show ourselves strong against the Malignant and his army and may work before you with purity, and have mercy on us. Christ, who through your fasting sanctified our nature and turned our ignorance into the light of your knowledge: illuminate the motions of our thoughts and have mercy on us.

### D
ܓ ܒܥܰܝܢܳܐ ܕܬܰܪܥܝܬܳܐ ܘܰܕ݂ܚܘܽܒܳܐ ܢܶܚܙܝܰܘܗܝ ܟܽܠܰܢ ܠܰܡܫܺܝܚܳܐ ܒܝܰܕ݂ ܐ̱ܪܳܙܶܐ ܘܛܘ̈ܦܣܶܐ ܕܐܰܫܠܶܡ ܠܰܢ ܟܰܕ ܡܶܬܕܰܒܰܪ ܠܚܰܫܶܗ ܕܰܨܠܺܝܒܳܐ ܘܥܰܠ ܡܰܕ݂ܒܚܳܐ ܩܰܕܺܝܫܳܐ ܡܶܬܬܣܺܝܡ ܕܶܒܚܳܐ ܚܰܝܳܐ ܘܟܳܗ̈ܢܶܐ ܐܰܝܟ ܡܰܠܰܐܟ̈ܶܐ ܡܰܥܒܕܺܝܢ ܠܕܽܘܟܪܳܢܳܐ ܕܡܰܘܬܶܗ ܒܩܳܠܳܐ ܕܬܽܘܕܝܺܬܳܐ ܘܐܳܡܪܝܺܢ
With the eyes of the mind and of charity, let us all see Christ through the mysteries and types which he handed down to us when he was led to the punishment of the cross. On the holy altar is placed the living sacrifice, and the priests, in the likeness of angels, celebrate the memory of his death with a voice of thanksgiving, saying: Glory to him for his ineffable gift.

### IV
ܫܘܽܒܚܳܐ ܠܶܗ ܥܰܠ ܡܰܘܗܰܒܬܶܗ ܕܠܳܐ ܡܶܬܡܰܠܠܳܐ ܀ ܕ ܐܳܦܶܢ ܫܳܠܚܺܝܢ ܐܰܢ̱ܬܘܽܢ ܢܰܚ̈ܬܰܝܟܘܽܢ ܗܳܢܘܽܢ ܒܰܪܳܝ̈ܶܐ: ܐܶܠܳܐ ܠܳܐ ܬܶܚܠܨܘܽܢ ܐܶܣܛܰܠܟܘܽܢ ܓܰܘܳܝܬܳܐ ܐܳܘ ܥܡܺܝܕܶܐ. ܐܶܢ ܕܶܝܢ ܠܗܳܢܳܐ ܙܰܝܢܳܐ ܟܰܣܝܳܐ ܠܒܺܝܫܺܝܢ ܐܰܢ̱ܬܘܽܢ: ܡܰܡܛܰܪܬܳܐ ܕܢܶܣܝܘܢܶܐ ܣܰܓܺܝ̈ܐܶܐ ܠܳܐ ܢܶܚܣܰܢܟܘܽܢ. ܝܳܕܥܺܝܢ ܐܰܢ̱ܬܘܽܢ ܗ̱ܘܳ ܐܰܝܠܶܝܢ ܡܶܠܶܐ ܫܡܰܥܬܘܽܢ: ܝܳܕ݂ܥܺܝܢ ܐܰܢ̱ܬܘܽܢ ܕܡܶܢ ܕܶܒܚܳܐ ܚܰܝܳܐ ܐܶܟܰܠܬܘܽܢ. ܐܶܙܕܰܗܪܘ ܡܶܢ ܒܺܝܫܳܐ ܕ݁ܰܠܡܳܐ ܢܰܫܪܰܥܟܘܽܢ ܐܰܝܟ ܕܐܳܦ ܠܳܐܕ݂ܳܡ: ܘܢܘܽܟܪ̈ܳܢܶܐ ܢܶܥܒܶܕ݂ܟܘܽܢ ܠܗܳܝ ܡܰܠܟܘܽܬܳܐ
Even if you put off those outer garments of yours, do not put off the inner habit, O baptized ones. For if you are clothed in these hidden weapons, the storm of many temptations will not prevail against you. You know what you have heard; you know that you have eaten from the sacrifice of life. Beware of the Malignant, lest he seduce you even as he did Adam, and make you strangers to this kingdom of glory. For he made him a stranger

ܘܚܰܡܪܳܐ܀ ܡܶܟ݂ܝܺܠ ܦܫܘܽܛܘ ܐܝܺܕܰܝ̈ܟܘܽܢ ܡܳܝܘ̈ܳܬܳܐ: ܘܣܰܒܘ ܘܐܶܬܚܰܣܰܘ ܘܰܩܢܰܘ ܚܰܝ̈ܶܐ: ܘܐܰܡܠܶܟܘ ܥܰܡ ܡܫܝܼܚܳܐ ܘܰܙܡܪܘ ܘܐܶܡܰܪܘ ܗܰܠܶܠܘܳܝܳܐ. ܗܳܢܰܘ ܐܳܣܝܳܐ ܕܰܢܚܶܬ ܠܰܫܝܘܽܠ: ܘܐܰܣܝܼ ܠܕ݂ܰܒܠܝܼܚܘܽ ܒܰܚܛܳܗܰܝ̈ܗܘܽܢ܀
Extend, therefore, your hands, O mortals, and receive and possess life in remission, and reign with Christ, singing and saying alleluia. He is the physician who descended to the infernal regions and healed those who had been consumed by sins.
