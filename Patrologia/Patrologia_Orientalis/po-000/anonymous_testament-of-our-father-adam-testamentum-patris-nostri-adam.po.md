### TESTAMENT OF OUR FATHER ADAM FRAGMENT I
< ̈ܗ ܧ ܬܰܘܕ݂ܝܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܐܳܕ݂ܳܡ
ܫܳܥܬܳܐ ܩܰܕ݂ܡܳܝܬܳܐ ܕܠܰܠܝܳܐ: ܬܰܘܕ݂ܝܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܫܺܐܕ݂ܶܐ. ܘܰܒ݁ܗܳܝ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܠܳܐ ܣܳܪܚܺܝܢ ܘܠܳܐ ܡܰܟ݁ܠܺܝܢ ܡܶܕ݁ܳܡ ܠܐ̱ܢܳܫܳܐ܀ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܬܰܪܬ݁ܶܝܢ: ܬܰܘܕ݂ܝܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܕ݁ܝܰܘ̈ܢܶܐ܀ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܰܬܠܳܬ: ܬܰܘܕ݂ܝܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܢܘܽܢܶܐ ܘܰܕܢܘܽܪܳܐ ܘܰܕܟܽܠܗܘܽܢ ܬܗܘܳܡܶܐ ܬܰܚܬܳܝܶܐ܀ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܐܰܪܒ݁ܰܥ: ܩܘܽܕ݁ܳܫܳܐ ܕܰܣܪ̈ܳܦ݁ܶܐ. ܗܳܟ݁ܰܢܳܐ ܥܺܝܕ݂ܰܥ ܗ̱ܘܺܝܬ: ܥܰܕ݂ܠܳܐ ܐܶܚܛܶܝܬ: ܩܳܠ ܓܶܦܰܝ̈ܗܘܽܢ ܒܦܰܪܕܰܝܣܳܐ: ܟܰܕ ܢܳܩܫܺܝܢ ܕ݁ܶܝܢ ܣܪ̈ܳܦ݁ܶܐ ܒܩܳܠܳܐ ܕܩܘܽܕ݁ܳܫܰܝ̈ܗܘܽܢ ܘܡܶܢ ܕܥܶܒܰܪܬ ܥܰܠ ܢܳܡܿܘܽܣܳܐ ܠܳܐ ܫܶܡܥܶܬ ܗܰܘ ܩܳܠܳܐ܀ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܚܰܡܶܫ: ܬܰܘܕ݂ܝܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܕ݂ܡܰܝ̈ܳܐ ܕܠܥܶܠ ܡܶܢ ܫܡܰܝ̈ܳܐ. ܗܳܟ݁ܰܢܳܐ ܐ݈ܢܳܐ ܘܡܰܠܰܐܟ݁ܶܐ ܫܡܰܥܢ ܩܳܠ ܓܰܠܺܝ̈ܙܶܐ ܬ݁ܰܩܺܝܦܶܐ ܕܡܰܪܗܶܒ ܗ̱ܘܳܐ ܠܗܘܢ ܪܶܡܙܳܐ ܕܢܶܬܠܘܽܢ ܫܘܽܒܚܳܐ
1. The first hour of the night: The confession of the demons. At which hour they neither inflict damage nor harm anything to man. 2. The second hour: The confession of the doves. 3. The third hour: The confession of the fish and of fire and of all the lower abysses. 4. The fourth hour: The Trisagion of the Seraphim. Thus I heard, before I had sinned, the voice of their wings in Paradise, when the Seraphim were resounding with the voice of their trisagions; and after I transgressed the law, I did not hear this voice. 5. The fifth hour: The confession of the waters which are above the heavens. Thus I and the angels heard the voice of the vehement waves, for a nod moved them, that they might give glory to the Creator. 6. The sixth hour: The gathering (?) of the clouds and the great fear which arises at midnight.

armies after the waters have fallen asleep. At this hour, water is taken into which the priest of God pours holy oil,
ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܢܳܣܶܒ ܟܳܗܢܳܐ ܕܐܰܠܳܗܳܐ ܡܶܫܚܳܐ ܕܩܘܽܕ݂ܫܳܐ ܘܡܳܫܰܚ ܠܰܐܝܠܺܝܢ ܕܐܰܠܝܨܝܺܢ ܘܢܳܝܚܝܺܢ܀ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܰܬܡܳܢܶܐ: ܡܰܦ݁ܰܩܳܢܘܳܬܳܐ ܕܥܶܣܒܳܐ ܕܐܰܪܥܳܐ ܡܳܐ ܕܢܳܚܶܬ ܛܰܠܳܐ ܡܶܢ ܫܡܰܝ̈ܳܐ܀ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܰܬܫܰܥ: ܬܰܘܕ݂ܝܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܰܟܪ̈ܘܽܒܶܐ܀ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܕ݁ܰܥܣܰܪ: ܬܰܘܕ݂ܝܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܰܒܢܰܝ̈ܢܳܫܳܐ. ܘܰܦ݂ܬ݁ܳܚ ܬܰܪ̈ܥܶܐ ܕ݁ܰܫܡܰܝ̈ܳܐ ܕܥܳܐܠܳܢ ܨܠܰܘ̈ܳܬܳܐ ܕܟܽܠ ܕܚܰܝ ܘܣܳܓ݂ܕܳܢ ܘܢܳܦ݂ܩܺܝܢ. ܘܰܒܗܳܝ ܫܳܥܬ݁ܳܐ ܟ݁ܽܠܡܶܕ݁ܶܡ ܕܢܶܫܐܰܠ ܐ݈ܢܳܫ ܡܶܢ
and he anoints the afflicted, and they find rest. 8. The eighth hour: The bringing forth of the herbs of the earth with the dew descending from heaven. 9. The ninth hour: The confession of the Cherubim. 10. The tenth hour: The confession of men. And the doors of heaven being opened, the prayers of every living being enter, and having performed adoration, they depart. At this hour, whatever a man asks of God is given to him, after the wing (?) of the Seraphim and the cocks have sounded.

The twelfth hour: The expectation of incense and the silence falling upon all the orders of fire and spirit, until all the priests have offered incense to His divinity; and then all the armies of the celestials depart.

4. The fourth hour: The confession of the animals. 5. The fifth hour: The supercelestial confession. 6. The sixth hour: The confession of the Cherubim, who intercede for the iniquity of our humanity.

and being healed, they recover. 11. The eleventh hour: The exultation and joy of the righteous. 12. The twelfth hour of the evening: The supplication of men in a will accepted before God, the Lord of all.
