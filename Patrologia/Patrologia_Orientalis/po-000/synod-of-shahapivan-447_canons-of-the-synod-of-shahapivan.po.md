# Canons of the Synod of Shahapivan

**Author**: Synod of Shahapivan (447)  
**Series**: PO Tome PS  
**Columns**: pp. 189–199  

[p. 189] {#po.0.p189}

### VI
CANONS OF THE NATIONAL SYNOD OF THE ARMENIANS A. 447 HELD IN THE CITY OF SAHAPIVAN, ENACTED AGAINST THE MESSALIANS. Canon 14. No bishop or priest, or deacon, or any church servant shall have a so-called housekeeper, as is the custom of the Mzlne. But if one has one and it comes to light through testimonies, he shall be deposed from the rank in which he stands and be regarded as a godless man and a tax-collector; for the holy Church and the Sacrament of the Lord admits only the blameless to salvation. Canon 19. If one is found in Mzinäuthiun, be it a priest
or deacon or one of the monks, the priestly ordination shall be dissolved, the fox-seal shall be pressed upon his forehead, and he shall be brought to a hermitage for penance. But if he is found in it again, one shall cut both his tendons and bring him to the cripple-house, for 'man was in a position of honor and did not value it.' The same punishment shall also be carried out on the monks. But if men are found with women and children in the sect, one shall cut the tendons of the men and women and the understanding children, press the fox-seal upon their forehead, and bring them to the cripple-house for penance. But the children, who do not yet know of the shameful deed, shall be taken away and given into the hand of the holy servants of God, so that they may educate them and instruct them in the true faith and in the fear of God. Canon 20. If Mzine-ers are found in the congregation and the priests know it and do not notify the bishop, but during the investigation it turns out that it is true and the priest knew of their deeds for days and months and a long time and did not accuse the Mzine-ers to the bishop, they shall bear the punishment which these canons have imposed, and the priests may not perform any priestly service for their whole life, so that the priest or the monk who takes their place may see them and learn from their example to lead the congregation in the truth. But if the priest reports it to the bishop and proves it with testimony, but the latter either takes gifts and keeps the matter silent or acts partially, and it comes to light, confirmed by testimonies, that the accusation of the priests has indeed come to his ears and he disregards the commandments of God and has not sought out the lost and has not stood up zealously and vengefully for the law of God, he shall be pushed from the bishop's chair, and the priest shall go out blameless. But if the bishop has made an effort and sought vengeance, so that the priests and other people bear witness to his effort, that he has notified some authority of the evildoers, and the prince in the lands or the chief in the village or the 'Nacharar,' the lord of the district, wants to promote the shameful deed and hide and conceal the fornicators, be it for the sake of vain money or for the sake of partiality and service, and does not prefer to love Christ and his commandments and to avenge the law of God in soul and body, such a one shall be damned


[p. 199] {#po.0.p199}
and shall remain removed from the holy Church until he delivers the shameful ones into the hand of the bishop. But if the shame is found in the house of a Nacharar, be it with his wife, be it with his child, or finally with himself, and he wants neither to hand over his family into the hands of the bishop, nor to come to sanctification himself, but to rely on his own strength, he shall be damned with his whole house, his descendants, and his property; he may not come to public places, and his companions, like the whole land, shall have no communion with him, until he, if he himself is in the shame, comes to sanctification, and if he himself is not, he gives his family and his servants into the hands of the chief bishop for correction. But if the shame is found in the house of a Wostanik, be it with himself, be it with his family, the family shall be corrected according to the law which we have established. But if he also, together with the family, is found in MzInäuthiun, he shall be captured with his shameful family, brought to a public place, and before the chief bishop and before the high princes, before the supreme judges and before the whole people, vengeance shall be taken for the law of God, so that the others may see it and serve the Creator of all in holiness and in fear, so that the order of divine service may remain whole and perfect, the law may be avenged, and the saints may have their honor, so that we may be found as true servants of God in words and deeds and hope for retribution for the good from the good God and inherit his imperishable gifts in eternity. Amen.
