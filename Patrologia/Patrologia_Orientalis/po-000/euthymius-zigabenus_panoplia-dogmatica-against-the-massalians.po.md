FROM THE DOGMATIC PANOPLY OF EUTHYMIUS ZIGABENUS. Title XXVI. Against the Massalians. The heresy of the Massalians sprouted up around the time of Valentinian and Valens. That appellation, translated into the Greek language, signifies Euchites. The name of prayer, however, has a different meaning among them, and they celebrate it far and wide and have it as a password. They call prayer or supplication that which individuals offer to God, whether the "Our Father," which the Lord handed down to the apostles, or some other one known only to the followers, who are more perfect in impiety, and which it is not permitted to betray to all others

They are called Enthusiasts, as those who have undergone the influx of some demon, which they hold to be the Holy Spirit. Those who are deeply infected with this disease shun the labors of the hands as something wicked, but giving themselves to much sleep, they call the visions of dreams an inspiration. Moreover, the leaders of this heresy were many and diverse; especially, however, Adelphius, whom Flavian of Antioch, having summoned him from Edessa, cunningly deceived and, feigning discipleship, enticed him to vomit forth the whole hidden poison of his doctrine. That disease having been made manifest, they were expelled from Syria and withdrew to Pamphylia. We will now continue to investigate some of their dogmas; for they have been taken from their own book and sometimes proposed to everyone to be spat upon.

But holy baptism and any other divine action are powerless to expel him, but only intent and diligent prayer can cast him out with the spittle and phlegm of the one praying, upon whose ejection the Holy Spirit arrives sensibly and visibly, manifesting his own presence and freeing the body from the movements of passions, granting impassibility, so that there is no longer any need for fasting to restrain the body nor for doctrine to curb it and teach it to walk rightly. But not only this; but also that he who has attained such impassibility foresees even future things and sees the divine Trinity with his eyes.
