# Definition of the Council of Ephesus

**Author**: Council of Ephesus (431)  
**Series**: PO Tome PS  
**Columns**: pp. 185–185  

[p. 185] {#po.0.p185}

### VI
Definition of the holy and ecumenical synod of Ephesus against the impious Messalians, who are also called Euchites and Enthusiasts. The most pious and most religious bishops Valerian and Amphilochius, coming to us, proposed for common consideration the matter concerning the Messalians, that is, the Euchites or Enthusiasts, who are staying in Pamphylia, or by whatever name that most contaminated heresy is called. But as we were considering, the most pious and most religious bishop Valerian brought a synodical schedule composed concerning them in great Constantinople under Sisinnius of blessed memory: which, when read, was approved by all, because it was well founded and rightly held. And it pleased us all and the most pious bishops Valerian and Amphilochius and all the most pious bishops of the provinces of Pamphylia and Lycaonia, that all things which are contained in the synodical chart should have force and in no way be passed over; that they should be solid, as well as those things which were done in Alexandria;
so that all who are heretical Messalians or Enthusiasts throughout the entire province, or suspected of the disease of that heresy, whether they be clerics or laymen, should be convicted: and if indeed they shall have anathematized, according to those things which were pronounced in the aforementioned synodical writing, in writing; if they are clerics, let them remain clerics; if laymen, let them be admitted to communion? But if they refuse to anathematize, if they are presbyters or deacons, or in any other rank of the church, let them fall from both the clergy and the rank and the communion: but let laymen be anathematized. 7. Also, those convicted shall not be permitted to have monasteries, so that tares may not spread and grow. That these things may be done so, let the most pious bishops Valerian and Amphilochius, and the other most reverend bishops of the whole province, direct all their strength. Whence it pleased that the polluted book of that heresy, which is called the Asceticon, be anathematized; which the most religious and most pious Valerian had brought, as being composed by heretics. Similarly, if anything savoring of their impiety be found among many, let this also be anathema. Furthermore, while they convene, let those things which are useful and necessary for concord and communion and dispensation be manifestly committed to writing. But if a question has arisen concerning those things which are in this business, and if anything difficult and ambiguous shall be, which is not approved by the most pious bishops Valerian and Amphilochius, and the other bishops throughout the whole province, they ought to examine all things with writings brought forward. And if the most pious bishops either of the Lycians or of the Lycaonians shall have been left, yet the metropolitan of whatever province he may be shall not be left. These things are to be referred to the commentaries, so that if any have need of them, they may find them, by which they may also more diligently explain them to others.
