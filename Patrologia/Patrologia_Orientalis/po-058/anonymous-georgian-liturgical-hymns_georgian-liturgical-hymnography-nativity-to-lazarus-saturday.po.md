### THE IBAKONI OF STEPHEN
Tone 2: After the impious had been confounded by the protomartyr, / They were even more irritated and gnashed their teeth against him. / They seized him, dragged him outside the city, and stoned him. / Therefore, he who prayed for them, for the universe, was massacred by them. / We pray you, O Christ-God, / By his intercession grant to the world forgiveness and great grace. Another, tone 4 plagal: The protomartyr Stephen performed signs and wonders / By the power of your Divinity, O Lord. / When he confounded the impious Jews, / These, in anger against him, shouted and planned the murder of the just one, / He who saw the heavens opened and you who sat at the right hand of the Father. / However he prayed and said: “O Lord, do not impute this sin to them.” / By his intercession grant to our souls great grace. Another, tone 4 plagal: While on his knees Stephen prayed to Christ, / He saw to the east the image of the cross, / But he did not dare to fix his eyes upon it because of its brightness. / The saint protomartyr Stephen prayed and said: / “Receive my spirit and see those who stone me; / Receive my spirit and deliver me from a foreign race. / Lord, have pity on me and save me.”
