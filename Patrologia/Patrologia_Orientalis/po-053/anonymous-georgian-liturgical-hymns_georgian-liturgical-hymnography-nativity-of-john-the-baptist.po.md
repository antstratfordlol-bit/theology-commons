### THE 25TH OF THE MONTH OF JUNE

### NATIVITY OF JOHN
(EVENING OFFICE) At Lord, I have cried... (Ps 140), plagal mode 4: 1. Let us acclaim the Nativity of the Forerunner of Christ, / So that by his intercession, /


[p. 500] {#po.53.p500}
The One, rich in mercy, / May grant us the forgiveness of sins. 2. Vigil and fasting, you manifested them in your body, O Forerunner; / That is why you became worthy to baptize the Savior of the world. 3. By the intercession of the Forerunner, today, in his Nativity, / We pray to you, O our beneficent Savior: / Have mercy on us who acclaim you, we your unworthy servants.


[p. 501] {#po.53.p501}
(MORNING OFFICE) At Sing... (Ode 1; Ex 15:1-19): 1. Let us sing with a new canticle to the Savior and God, / He who, from a Barren woman, manifested to us his Forerunner / From whom with humility he received baptism. / For he has gloriously covered himself with glory. 2. Admirably, O Prophet, by the action of the leaping, / And by your sojourn in the desert you made pagans hear / The call of Christ, our Redeemer, Savior and God. / For he has gloriously covered himself with glory. At Be attentive. (Ode 2; Dt 32:1-41): 1. Be attentive, heaven. / Today let us celebrate and sing to God, / For the birth of his Forerunner. 2. Guard us, we who hope in you, O God, / By the intercession of your glorious Forerunner. 3. Just and holy is the Lord, / He who has made glorious among the believers / The Nativity of the Forerunner.


[p. 502] {#po.53.p502}
At Has been strengthened... (Ode 3; 1 Kgs 2:1-10): 1. You are the one who makes me strong, / You who founded the earth on the waters, / And who gives us to celebrate the memory of the Forerunner. / For none is holy apart from you, Lord. 2. You are the one who makes me strong, / You who made the Nativity of your Forerunner shine. / For none is holy apart from you, Lord. At Lord, I have heard. (Ode 4; Hab 3:2-19): 1. The Forerunner was shining with grace, / He danced in the womb of his mother, / And, by his leaping, / He adored Christ silently. 2. When the Forerunner saw the fulfillment of the law by Christ, / He withdrew to the desert, / And he lived there the way of life of the angels. 3. Forerunner, you who were worthy to baptize Christ, / He who gives life to our souls, / And who announced glory and salvation to the inhabitants of the underworld, / Intercede for us.


[p. 503] {#po.53.p503}
At From the night... (Ode 5; Is 26:9-20): 1. From the night we rise, / We give you glory, Lord our God. / Today, in the Nativity of your Forerunner, give us your peace, / And save our souls. 2. Christ, the light has shone upon us, / In the Nativity of your Forerunner; / From our souls, you have removed the darkness, / And you have made us worthy of your kingdom. 3. You are the Light, Christ, / By your light... At I have cried... (Ode 6; Jon 2:3-10): 1. The Forerunner of the Savior proclaimed in the desert: / "Repent, for the kingdom of Christ / Has arrived for the world." 2. Today, by the intercession of the Forerunner, / In his Nativity, we pray to you: / "Grant us the forgiveness of sins, / We who sing to you without ceasing." / 3. To you only the praise... At Blessed are you... (Ode 7; Dan 3:26-56): 1. You who manifested yourself in the bush, / And who, in the entrails of his mother, blessed John, your Forerunner, /


[p. 504] {#po.53.p504}
Then manifested him as your Baptizer, / Blessed are you, you, for the ages. 2. The Forerunner, raised in the desert, / Preached repentance to the pagans, / And he was saying while giving thanks: / "Blessed are you, you, for the ages." 3. As a sign of repentance, John, herald, prophet, / Conferred upon men a baptism, / And they were proclaiming and saying: / "Blessed are you, you, for the ages." 4. At the intercession of the Forerunner, we pray to you: / Intercede for our salvation, / We who, with all our heart sing to you with faith: / "Blessed are you, you, for the ages." 5. The One of the Trinity. At Bless... (Ode 8; Dan 3:57-88): 1. In the desert, your Forerunner was offering you a hymn, O Savior, / And while giving thanks he was saying without ceasing: / "Bless the Lord, all the works of the Lord."


[p. 505] {#po.53.p505}
2. The way of life of the angels of Christ, / You realized it bodily, O Forerunner, / And you turned the people away from error, / So that we might be able to glorify the consubstantiality of the Trinity, / We who proclaim and say: / "Bless the Lord, all the works of the Lord." 3. Savior of the world, at the intercession of the Forerunner, / Make us worthy of your kingdom, / We who proclaim and say: / "Bless the Lord, all the works of the Lord." 4. Let us sing, we believers, the consubstantial Trinity, / The Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, / By a hymn of glory let us glorify it and say: / "All the works of the Lord, bless the Lord." At Magnify... (Ode 9; Lk 1:46-55): 1. We magnify you, Most holy Virgin, / And we sing to Christ-God, / He who from you became flesh. 2. Figure of the angels and inhabitant of the desert, / John, the Forerunner of Christ, / We all, believers, exalt him. 3. You lived in the desert in the manner of an angel / And, in the expectation of him who would come after you, O Forerunner, / You were rejoicing and glorifying the Lord.


[p. 506] {#po.53.p506}
4. That is why John was declaring and saying to the crowd: / "Prepare the ways of the Lord, behold he comes." / Also I will render him glory and praise. At Praise. (Psalm 148): 1. Let us all praise the Nativity of the Forerunner, proclaim and say: / "Intercede for us, John, illustrious herald of Christ." 2. Forerunner and Baptizer of the Savior, / Today, in your Nativity, intercede without ceasing for our souls. 3. John, Prophet, Forerunner and Baptizer of Christ, / Today, in your Nativity, pray to Christ, / So that he may grant to us, your servants, the forgiveness of sins. 4. Prophet, since you possess assurance, / Intercede without ceasing for us, in order to save our souls, / We who praise you without stopping. Others: 1. Herald of the New Law / And Forerunner of the King of the universe, / He who in the womb of his mother / Was announcing to us the coming of the Lord. 2. John, who in the house of Zechariah as under a tent, / Contemplated the Mother of God, / Danced at the salutation of Elizabeth, / For from the womb, Christ sanctified him, / In view of prophesying and preaching. 3. When Elizabeth, in her old age, was pregnant with the child, / Mary went up to greet her. / Then, John danced with joy in the womb of his mother, / For he saw God incarnate in the womb of the Virgin.


[p. 507] {#po.53.p507}
(LITURGY) The hymn of the entrance, plagal mode 4: Forerunner, herald of Christ, / Like the rising dawn and like the star of the day, / You were sent before him, you, light before his face, / So that, by a Barren woman, was announced to us, / Our Savior, the child born of the Virgin, / And that after your birth, / Light of Light and Voice that cries in the desert, / You might make known to the people the Sun of justice. / You who have assurance before Christ, / Ask him to welcome our souls. Psalm, plagal mode 4: There, I will make rise... (Ps 131:17). Psalm verse: Remember, Lord. (Ps 131:1). Alleluia, mode 2: His throne... (Ps 88:37). (The hymn) of the washing of hands, plagal mode 4: You, the Baptizer, he has exalted you, / He who counts the innumerable multitude of the stars, / And who weighs the invisible breaths of the winds. / While you were in the womb, he sanctified you; / While you were throwing yourself against the wall, /


[p. 508] {#po.53.p508}
An angel of God and good counselor preserved you. / Intercede for us in order to save our souls. (The hymn) of the holy gifts: The multitude of the angels gives you glory, alleluia, / You who save the sons of men, alleluia. / And we, earthlings, saved from error, / As with one mouth we say, alleluia: / "You are holy, you are holy, you are holy, you, Lord, glory (to you)."

### THE IBAK'ONI OF THE NATIVITY OF JOHN
1. Mode... In the desert the rock split / And welcomed Elizabeth with the child, the Baptizer, fleeing Herod. / However, the pursuers, astonished, were saying to one another: / "Although he is neither an angel of God nor his forerunner, / How could the rock have hidden him?" / By his intercession grant to the world great mercy. 2. Mode 4: Herald of the new law, Forerunner of the King of the universe, / He who, in the womb of his mother, / Made us know your advent, O Lord. / By his perseverance he received the crown, / And by his combat he prepared himself for the assembly of the angels. / By his intercession grant to our souls great mercy. 3. Mode 4: John, who in the house of Zechariah as under a tent, / Contemplated the Mother of God / And leaped with gladness at the salutation of Elizabeth, / Hastened here to the service of the Savior. / Now, we too, with him who was born of a Barren woman, / We proclaim: "Christ-God, glory to you."

### OF THE APOSTLES
(EVENING OFFICE) At Lord I have cried... (Ps 140), mode 2: 1. By the intercession of the holy apostles in our favor, / By a hymn to Christ, / May all the imposture of the idols be dissipated, / And may, by faith, the human race be saved. 2. Come, peoples, / Let us honor, all, the apostles of Christ, heralds of the truth, / They who taught us, we pagans, the hymn of the trisagion, / They who, after death, intercede for our souls.


[p. 514] {#po.53.p514}
3. Son of the Father and brothers of the Son, / Apostles of Christ and filled with glory, / Intercede for our souls. 4. Beloved of the Father and lights of the world, / Apostles of Christ and blessed, / Intercede for our souls.


[p. 515] {#po.53.p515}
(MORNING OFFICE) At Sing... (Ode 1; Ex 15:1-19), mode 2: 1. You have become help and protector, / In view of the salvation of your servants, / By the intercession, O God, of your apostles. 2. To the ends of the earth / Spread the words of the apostles, / And in faith were strengthened our souls. 3. The apostles endured the persecution of the impious, / But without ceasing they were singing to you, O God. 4. By the intercession of the apostles we beseech you, O God: / Make us worthy of your kingdom. At Be attentive. (Ode 2; Dt 32:1-43): 1. Be attentive, O heaven, and let us say: / "Let us celebrate and sing to God, / The One, the Friend of men." 2. Let us render glory to the Lord, / He who has made us celebrate the holy apostles. 3. Guard us, we who hope in you, / By the intercession, O God, of your holy apostles.


[p. 516] {#po.53.p516}
4. Just and holy is the Lord, / He who has exalted the memory of the holy apostles. 5. Apostles of the Lord, most worthy of praise, / Intercede without ceasing for the salvation of our souls. At Has been strengthened... (Ode 3; 1 Kgs 2:1-10): 1. Christ, strengthen me, / Strengthen me in you, / And teach me the light of your knowledge. 2. Christ, have mercy on me, / By reason of the intercession of the apostles, / And deliver me from the net of the enemy, / Lord, Friend of men. 3. In persecution, O Christ, / The apostles were confessing you with assurance, / O Savior, rich in mercy. 4. By the intercession of your holy apostles, O Christ, / Grant to our souls the forgiveness of sins. At Lord, I have heard. (Ode 4; Hab 3:2-19): 1. Christ, (your) splendor has covered the heavens, / And of your praise, Lord, the earth is full. 2. Christ, your apostles destroyed the imposture of the idols, / By proclaiming to all the consubstantiality of the Trinity.


[p. 517] {#po.53.p517}
3. Christ, in suffering, the apostles confessed you, / And they received with joy the crown of victory. 4. Strengthened by the torture of your cross, Christ, / The holy apostles have conquered the enemy power. 5. Christ, you have led us, we too, to your light, / And you have chosen us, Lord, to praise you worthily. 6. Christ, make us worthy, we too, of your kingdom, / By the intercession of your holy apostles, O Savior rich in mercy. At From the night... (Ode 5; Is 26:9-20): 1. The apostles dissipated the darkness of the night, O Merciful, / They clothed themselves with the Light; / Thanks to it they trampled underfoot the imposture of the enemy, / And they became worthy of your kingdom. 2. Your holy apostles, Christ, took upon themselves the cross, / They did not tremble with fear before the tyrants, / They taught the world to flee the imposture, / And to adore the true God. 3. For your kingdom, they despised the power and the fear of kings, / And now, they intercede without ceasing for us, / We who sing your goodness, / For you are the One, the Beneficent and the Friend of men. At I have cried... (Ode 6; Jon 2:3-10): 1. From corruption, my life has risen toward you, / Lord, my God, / You who have delivered us from the bonds of death / By your cross and your resurrection. 2. Christ, in persecution, / The apostles were boasting of your assistance.


[p. 518] {#po.53.p518}
3. The holy apostles, made strong by the cross, / Have trampled underfoot the pride of the tyrants. 4. Christ, by the intercession of your holy apostles, / You have given peace to your people. 5. The mode... / At Blessed are you... (Ode 7; Dan 3:26-56) 1. Christ, the holy apostles proclaimed you, / While they were whipping and tearing their bodies, / For they desired you. They were proclaiming and saying: / "Blessed are you, Lord, God of our fathers." 5. In Babylon, the holy children confessed you as the Trinity...

Others: You will find them at Theodore 1. ### IBAK'ONI OF THE APOSTLES
Tone 1: As is fitting, Lord, / You have given joy to your apostles. / To one you entrusted the keys of the kingdom, / While to another you gave to ascend to the third heaven, / But you caused one alone to lean upon your breast. / And now they are glorified in the Churches, / For they preached with faith the hidden and invisible mysteries. / At their prayer, always have mercy on us. Tone 2: Apostles, martyrs, and prophets, / You who have accomplished a magnificent work, / You have kept the faith. / Since you have boldness before Christ the Savior, / Intercede unceasingly for our souls. Tone 2: Apostles, resplendent with divine light, / You have trampled underfoot all the imposture of idols, / You have appeared as the heirs of the kingdom of heaven. / Since you have boldness, / Intercede for our souls before Christ-God. Plagal Tone 1: Heralds of the truth, chosen of God, / Holy apostles, messengers of the sublime Mystery / Which no man has seen, veiled as it was in the flesh. / It was served by you as by the Incorporeals.


[p. 525] {#po.53.p525}
Therefore pray to Christ, / That he may cause a great mercy to descend upon us. Plagal Tone 3: When you lived among your disciples, Savior, / And they saw your miracles, O Lord, / You gave them also the power to heal the sick / And all the infirmities of men. / They, therefore, by the sign of your cross, / Dissipated all the imposture of the enemy, / And they proclaimed the miracle of your resurrection. / Through them, save us, O Christ-God. Tone 3: Apostles and witnesses of the sufferings of Christ, / And participants in his glorious manifestation to come, / You did not follow the wisdom of fables, / For the coming and the power of the Lord Jesus Christ / Had been revealed to you. / But you were the spectators of his glory. / Therefore, even now, do not cease to intercede for us, / Until the day dawns and the morning star shines in our hearts. Another, Tone 2: The works of grace shone throughout the entire world / Through your teaching, / Priests, heralds of the blessed Trinity. / For it was given to you to know the ineffable kingdom of the heavens, / To never forget the beauty of your mission: / The faith, a law for the salvation of the world. / Through you, we ask the God of the universe / For joy and great mercy.

### THE TRANSFIGURATION
(EVENING OFFICE) To the Lord, I cried… (Ps 140), Tone 1: 1. Rejoice, heavens, and sing, foundations of the earth, / Mountains, cry out with gladness, /


[p. 528] {#po.53.p528}
For, today, the Emmanuel was transfigured in the glory he possessed. / He manifested his majesty to his disciples, / For he is God and Friend of men. 2. The apostles rejoiced at the sight of you, Savior, / Because of the splendor of the light of your Divinity, / And, then, they believed in the testimony of the Father's voice: / "You are the Son of God, / You who, having become man in your infinite love for men, / Have saved the human race."


[p. 529] {#po.53.p529}
(MORNING OFFICE) To Sing… (Ode 1; Ex 15:1-19): 1. He who, today, on Mount Tabor, / Shone with light in his Transfiguration / And filled the disciples with joy, / Sing to him, the Lord, for he has gloriously covered himself with glory. 2. Today, with an impalpable light / The apostles shone intensely, O beneficent Savior. / So that we too, thanks to them, may become worthy of it, / We pray to you. To Be attentive. (Ode 2; Dt 32:1-43): 1. Peoples, give glory to Christ, our God, / He who, on the mountain, / Unveiled to the apostles the future manifestation of his Divinity. 2. Before the disciples, Lord, you were transfigured, / And you were recognized by deceased prophets, / So that living and dead might confess you. 3. See, see the Lord, our God, / He who was transfigured on Mount Tabor, / For he is God and Friend of men.


[p. 530] {#po.53.p530}
4. Glory to you, glory to you, King of glory, / You who with the light of your Divinity have filled the apostles / And, through them, with the faith, have adorned the Church. 5. Was chained by fear. / To Is strengthened… (Ode 3; 1 Kgs 2:1-10): 1. Lord, from the beginning, you are strong and glorious, / Have mercy on us. 2. Christ, you have strengthened us, / You who, without seed, were born of the Virgin, / And saved us from the multitude of our sins, / For you are rich in mercy. 3. Christ, when you were transfigured, / You showed the apostles the light of your Divinity, / And you taught the people your humility. To Lord, I have heard. (Ode 4; Hab 3:2-19): 1. Almighty, I have heard what is heard of you, / And it is with fear, Christ-God, that I will glorify you. 2. On your holy mountain, O Savior, / You were transfigured in your Divinity, / As you had decided. 3. When the apostles saw your resplendence of light, / Christ-God, they were frightened / And, seized with fear, they glorified you.


[p. 531] {#po.53.p531}
4. O our God, by the intercession of your holy apostles, / Deliver us from temptation, we who glorify you. To From the night… (Ode 5; Is 26:9-20): 1. From the night we rise, / We sing to you ever more, Christ, our God, / For, on the earth, light are your commandments. 2. Lord, by your power you have illuminated your holy apostles, / And, on the earth, they glorified your Divinity. 3. Lord, by the intercession of the holy apostles, / Give peace to our souls, we who glorify you. To I have cried… (Ode 6; Jon 2:3-10): 1. I have cried to you in the distress of my heart, / To a merciful God; / Do not disdain, O Beneficent One, / Your people who have sinned. 2. Savior, you manifested yourself on your holy mountain, / With the prophets and the apostles, / And you illuminated them, / So that they might sing your Divinity. 3. The apostles rejoiced, / When they heard the testimony of the Father concerning you: / "This is my beloved Son, listen to him."


[p. 532] {#po.53.p532}
To Blessed are you… (Ode 7; Dan 3:26-56): 1. You who by the fire in the bush manifested yourself to the lawgiver, / And who thus prefigured the birth by the Virgin, / Blessed are you, Lord, God of our fathers. 2. You who on Mount Tabor / Manifested yourself in the light, / And who showed your majesty to the apostles and the prophets, / Blessed are you, Lord, God of our fathers. 3. Today, we all believers, / We prostrate ourselves together before your divine manifestation, / And, in thanksgiving, we proclaim and we say: / "Blessed are you, Lord, God of our fathers." 4. By faith. To Bless… (Ode 8; Dan 3:57-88): 1. For you, a hymn 2. Him, Christ-God, who on the holy mountain / Was transfigured in an impalpable light, / Let us sing to him and exalt him above all for ages. 3. He who appeared in glory in the light and the cloud, / Christ-God, before whom Moses and Elijah prostrated themselves, / Let us sing to him and exalt him above all for ages.


[p. 533] {#po.53.p533}
4. Him, the Word, who received from the height of heaven the testimony of the Father, / Christ, our God, before whom the apostles prostrated themselves with fear, / Let us sing to him and exalt him above all for ages. To Magnify… (Ode 9; Lk 1:46-55): 1. We magnify you, we, all generations…, 2. Christ, our ineffable God, / Image and resplendence of the glory of the Father, / He who for the love of men / Put on, from the holy Virgin, the form of the servant, / By a hymn we magnify him. 3. O Christ, you who gave to the holy women / The grace to come to the tomb, receptacle of God, / And to be, for the holy apostles, the messengers of your resurrection; / Through them, Lord, welcome this commemoration of the departed, / And give them rest, / They who await your second coming as God. 7. O Christ, you who because of their courage / Gave victory to the holy martyrs, / They who testified concerning you; / Through them, Lord, welcome the commemoration of the departed, / And give them rest, / They who await your second coming as God. 8. O Christ, by the precious cross, / You made of angels and men a unique flock, / That is why the heavens and the earth rejoice. / Through them, Lord, welcome the commemoration of the departed, / And give them rest, / They who await your second coming as God. 9. The souls that you changed from dwelling, Lord, / Make them worthy to dwell with your saints in your kingdom, / And save them, for you are merciful. 10. Be for us a protector, Lord, / And illuminate our spirits by moving them away from our sins, / For sweet is your mercy which also forgives sins. / Open the doors to our prayers, give us your mercy, / And remember, Lord, the souls that you welcomed. 11. We, chosen flock and inheritance of the holy apostles, / Make us worthy, we unworthy servants, / To enter and to dwell in your kingdom, / And remember, Lord, the souls that you welcomed.
