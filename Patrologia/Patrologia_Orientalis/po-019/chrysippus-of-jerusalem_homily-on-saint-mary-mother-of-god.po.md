It is fitting to proclaim blessed, to admire, and to extol with praises at all times the ever-verdant rod of Jesse, which brought forth life as fruit for the entire human race; yet the present occasion is most opportune for those who celebrate her. For on this day, in many places, this festival is magnificently celebrated, since at this time some mystery hidden from the sense of the common people was accomplished. Come, therefore, let us too—even if we are unable to offer worthy gifts—proclaim our good will to the best of our ability, and let us immediately take our beginning from the words of Gabriel to her: Hail, full of grace; the Lord is with you.
