მიუპყრა სიმესა თვსსა, ზეცისა უკუდავსა მეუფესა. არა თუ პირველისა მის სამოთხისა. რომელი იგი ურჩებისა-გან უქმად დაჰმთა. არამედ რომელი ესე მორჩილთა–მიერ ქრისტნს სახარებისა ქადაგთა შუენიერად აღეშცნა, აღორძნდა და ნაყოფიერ იქმნა
of the heavens, the fresh flower of the new paradise, not of the first paradise which remained uncultivated following disobedience, but of that which was cultivated by the docile preachers of the Gospel of Christ and grew magnificently and became fruitful.

He gave to his church to keep as a treasure, first, the apostle confessors, those fragrant flowers; secondly, their works, the good fruits of their deeds in which the Church always finds its delights and its rejoicing. Beloved, today the apostles acquire power and the confessors appear; today the slain prophets become examples, and the martyrs triumph. Today the Lord reveals his cross and the chosen combatants appear. Today the new foundation of the churches is established and the combatants of Christ discover their beauty at the vaults of the churches like firm stones hewn from the rock of Christ. Today the prophecy of David and that of Isaiah are fulfilled, for around Jerusalem new walls are erected, whose stones are precious stones, sapphires: these are the holy apostles and martyrs.

For the walls of the old law have crumbled and the churches of the new grace, the Gospel of Christ, surround Jerusalem as if with a wall. Today, the believers multiply and the assembly of the apostles is crowned. Today the preachers of the Gospel of Christ are strengthened and the servants of the old law are weakened. The lost lambs of the house of Israel who had fled from the court of their Lord, having become of their own will the prey of wild beasts, decrease in number and the wild flocks of the pagans fill a court inaccessible to beasts. The sons of the law-giving prophets become the debtors of God, the obstinate with hard hearts, uncircumcised and circumcised, rise up against the Holy Spirit as adversaries of heart and soul. The poor in spirit shine, bowing their heads and receiving the light of baptism by water and by the spirit. Christ has today placed upon the head of the churches the crowns of the apostles, those of famous deeds. Stephen is in truth the crown in accordance with his name, the grace of beauty in the hands of the Lord.

Who is worthy of glory to such a high degree, as you are, you, the most praised among the martyrs and the most distinguished among the deacons? We find no one like you.

He saw with his eyes the immutable heavenly inheritance prepared by Christ and he inherited it instantly. It was therefore the order so that the holy and irreproachable servant of the well-known altar should advance in a splendid manner to the spiritual sanctuary of the heavenly altar from above as a spiritual servant of the spiritual [...].
