His arrest. - When the king learned of his son's flight - and none of the servants of the kingdom knew where he had gone - he sent swift messengers and fast horses and dispatched them to all the provinces of his empire; they searched for him and did not find him. * The king had them investigate which way he had gone, what route he had taken, who had received him, and by whom he had been perverted. The adversaries of all justice and especially of the holy Church, who were seeking a favorable place and time to accomplish their evil and cruel will, ran quickly to incite the king with evil words, and they strove not only to accuse and (have) the saint himself put to death, but also to oppress the entire holy Church.
