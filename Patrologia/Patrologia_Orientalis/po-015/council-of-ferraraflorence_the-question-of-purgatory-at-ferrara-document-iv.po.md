Gregory, the most blessed high priest, in the fourth book of the Dialogues, proves with very many examples and many revelations that there is a purgatorial fire, saying thus: 'As each one departs from here, so is he led to judgment.' But indeed, regarding certain faults, one must believe that there is a fire of purification before the judgment, because the Truth says that if anyone blasphemes against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven him, neither in this age nor in the age to come. In this pronouncement, it is given to be understood that some sins can be forgiven in this age, and others in the age to come. For the fact that it denies it for one case makes it clear, from the sequence of thought, that it is permitted for others. But as I said before, one must believe that this can happen regarding small and light sins.
