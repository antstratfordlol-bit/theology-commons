This saint was originally from Nineveh and lived in the time of Kosrau (Kisra) Anosirwan and his son Hormizdad. From his childhood he attached himself to the reading * of the divine Books. Having become an adolescent, he went to find Rabban Job (Ayyoub), disciple of Abraham of Nethpar, who gave him the monastic habit, and with whom he spent fifteen years, serving the brothers, nourishing himself only with bread and water; a sleeveless shirt served as his clothing, he converted to the true faith a crowd of Jacobites who lived in Nineveh (Nainawa), and performed many miracles. It is said that one day, when he was passing by shepherds who were eating meat, they begged and conjured him to eat with them; he consented because of the oath and swallowed three bites. The monks who were with him disapproved of his conduct and conceived contempt for him. It was necessary to cross (the Tigris) to go from Nineveh to the garden. He made the sign of the cross on the water, sat on his garment that he had just spread (on the water),

and after having taken next to him the monks, who had blamed him for having eaten meat, crossed the water with them as if on dry land. The guards of the gate * of the city, having perceived him, took him for a god. Then he built a large temple very close to the city, where two pious monks came to live with him. There were no buildings then opposite the garden. It was Kosrau (Kisra), son of Hormizd, who, at his accession, built many buildings around, where one lodged. During the invasion of the Arabs in the country, from where they led men into captivity, many people took refuge in the vicinity of the saint. The Arabs attacked them and made them prisoners. One of them, who drew his sword to kill the saint, had his hand frozen. Having been healed by the saint, who, at his entreaties, had just prayed over him, he was the cause that all the prisoners and all the booty were delivered to him. He predicted the foundation of Mosul (Mausel) and its glory and the end of the (Persian) domination. When the Arabs reigned, they added numerous buildings to the place where Kosrau had built, and named it Mosul, which later became a city.
قال المتلكة اولمالامتك العرب أداذوا يازا ل إكرى وكنى المرعل كارت اميت ويقال ان هذا القديس لم يكن يأكل فى الصوم المارانى الا النقل فقط. واستناح3 بعد ان شاخ ودفن فى ديرا ويسمى دير ربّن قوسرا وهو كرسى مطارنة الموضل الى وقتنا هذا.
It is said that this saint ate only fruit during Lent. He died at an old age and was buried in his convent, which is known by the name of Rabban Qousré; it is still today the seat of the metropolitans of Mosul.
