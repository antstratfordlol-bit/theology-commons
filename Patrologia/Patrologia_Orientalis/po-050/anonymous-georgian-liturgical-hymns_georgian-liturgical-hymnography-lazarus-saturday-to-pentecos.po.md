### THE SIXTH SATURDAY OF LAZARUS
(EVENING OFFICE) To the Lord, I cried … (Ps 140), plagal mode 4. At Jericho, the Lord said to his disciples: "Let us go to Bethany, for a miracle of God is going to happen today."


[p. 284] {#po.50.p284}
2. The Lord, with his disciples, left for Bethany. He then saw that Martha and Mary, in tears, were weeping for Lazarus. 3. Martha fell at the feet of the Lord and said to him: "Lord, if you had been here, Lazarus, your friend, would not have died." 4. He said to her: "Believe in God and you will see the glory of the Lord." He had already been lying in the tomb for four days. He called him: "Lazarus, rise and come out." 5. The dead man rose, came out (and) declared: "You can do everything, Christ. All praise you, all glorify you, Savior, glory to you."


[p. 285] {#po.50.p285}
(MORNING OFFICE) Blessed are you … (Ode 7; Dn 3, 26-56), mode 3: 1. The three children in the furnace, by the power of the Trinity, extinguished the flame of the fire. And they said while singing: "Blessed are you, Lord, God of our fathers." 2. From the four-day tomb, Lord, You resurrected Lazarus wrapped in a shroud. Therefore by a hymn we acclaim you: "Blessed are you, (Lord, God of our fathers)." 3. Today, you have strengthened Martha in the faith, Christ, by the resurrection of Lazarus from the dead. Therefore by a hymn we acclaim you: "(Blessed are you, Lord, God of our fathers)." 4. Lord, at the intercession of Lazarus, your friend, deliver us from sins.


[p. 286] {#po.50.p286}
So that we all may proclaim and say: "Blessed are you, (Lord, God of our fathers)." 5. Trinity … Bless … (Ode 8; Dn 3, 57-58) 1. The Lord announced his resurrection in advance. Today the Savior came to Bethany in order to resurrect Lazarus, his friend. Priests, (bless him, Peoples, exalt him above all, for all ages). 2. Christ called Lazarus from the tomb and cried to him: "Lazarus, rise and come out." The four-day dead man rose and he praised: "Priests, (bless him, Peoples, exalt him above all, for all ages)." 3. He who resurrected Lazarus from the four-day tomb, Priests, (bless him, Peoples, exalt him above all, for all ages). Magnify … (Ode 9; Lk 1, 46-55), plagal mode 3: 1. We glorify you, spotless Virgin, You who by the word gave birth to the Word of God. Intercede without ceasing for those who pray to you.


[p. 287] {#po.50.p287}
2. We all glorify your love for mankind, God our Savior, Glory of your servants and crown of believers, You who today make the memory of your worthy Lazarus glorified. 3. You announced in advance, (6) Beneficent One, Your resurrection from the tomb by the resurrection of Lazarus; And he said while acclaiming: "You are our God, rich in mercy." 4. Today, you resurrected Lazarus from the four-day tomb, and he said while acclaiming: "You are my life and my resurrection, Savior, rich in mercy." Praise … (Ps 148), plagal mode 3: 1. Today, Savior, from the four-day tomb You resurrected Lazarus. And, by returning him to his sisters, (6) Beneficent One, You strengthened your disciples so that during your suffering in the flesh, they would not become incredulous of your Divinity, For you are the Life and the Resurrection of those who believe in you, Christ.


[p. 288] {#po.50.p288}
2. The quadripartite body of Lazarus already corrupted, the Creator, by the word, strengthened it. For you called the four-day dead man, Christ, and at your voice he came out wrapped. By that very thing we have all been strengthened. Therefore we believe you are God by nature, become man without change for the salvation of our souls. 3. Lord, just as you had said to Martha: "I am the Resurrection," You confirmed (this) word in act, from the tomb you resurrected Lazarus. From the abyss of my faults make me also rise, For you are the Friend of mankind. Other (stanzas), plagal mode 1: 1. The Lord said to his disciples: "Go! Let us leave for Bethany.


[p. 289] {#po.50.p289}
Lazarus, my friend, has fallen asleep, But I am going to go and I will awaken him so that all may believe that I am the God of those who have fallen asleep." 2. The Lord said openly to his disciples: "Lazarus, my friend, is dead. But I am going to go and I will resurrect him so that all may believe that I am God, the Resurrection and the Life of those who rest." 3. The Lord came to Bethany to console Martha and Mary, and he manifested his glory to the whole crowd, and Lazarus, the four-day dead man, he resurrected from the tomb. 4. Mary came to meet the Savior and she fell at the feet of the Lord. With tears she bathed his feet, and with her hair she wiped them.


[p. 290] {#po.50.p290}
5. Mary took an ointment and began to anoint the feet of the Lord; and a good, penetrating odor spread over all the people who had come because of Mary and Martha.


[p. 291] {#po.50.p291}
(LITURGY) The (hymn) of the entrance, plagal mode 4: Blessed are you, Lord, you who came to Bethany. You resurrected Lazarus, the four-day dead man. Those who saw and believed proclaimed and said: "You who have come and who will return, Jesus, our Savior, glory to you." Another, mode 2: Glory, glory to you, glory to your coming. You, Savior, you came to Bethany, you resurrected Lazarus, and the four-day dead man became your companion. You entered the Holy City, Jerusalem, Herald of the resurrection, Savior of our souls, Glory to you. Psalm, mode 2: Lord, you have made (my soul) rise … (Ps 29, 4). Psalm verse: I will exalt you … (Ps 29, 2).


[p. 292] {#po.50.p292}
Alleluia, mode 2: He has brought back my soul … (Ps 22, 3). (The hymn) of the washing of the hands, mode 1: Mary and Martha said to the Savior: "Lord, if you had been here, Lazarus, your friend, would not have died." But Christ, our Savior, Resurrection of those who have fallen asleep, resurrected Lazarus from the four-day tomb. Come all, peoples, let us adore him who came, voluntarily, to suffer for us in the flesh. (The hymn) of the holy gifts, plagal mode 1: The crowd of holy angels stands before (you) and they glorify you, our Savior, for you are holy.

### THE IBAK'ONI OF THE SATURDAY OF LAZARUS
Mode 1: When you came to Bethany, as the dispenser of immortality, for the four-day dead man, you called him as a living one. And, renewed, he who was corrupting heard your voice, when you cried out: "Lazarus, come, out!" And the dead man, revived, but tied, came running toward you. Almighty Lord, glory to you. Mode 2: When Christ called Lazarus and cried to him: "Lazarus, rise and come out," the thickness of the earth did not stop your formidable voice, (6) Savior, and the dead man was not deaf to your voice, (6) Merciful One; But just as friends awaken a sleeper, so you awakened him. Resurrection and Pride of those who rest, Lord, glory to you. Plagal mode 4: The sleep of Lazarus, since you know hearts,


[p. 294] {#po.50.p294}
In advance you announced it, and to Bethany, O Savior, you hastened, in order to resurrect the one you loved. Therefore we acclaim you: "Blessed be he who, according to his will, came to suffer for the salvation of our souls."
