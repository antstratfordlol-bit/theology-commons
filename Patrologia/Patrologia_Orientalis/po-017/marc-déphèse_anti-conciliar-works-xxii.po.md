For, as if the past were not enough to confirm the novelty they have perpetrated, they have chosen for themselves a patron, or rather a hireling, not a shepherd, a wolf, not a herdsman, whom they can drive and carry according to their lust, and by his help they think they will instill the most depraved dogma of the Latins into the minds of all; and perhaps they will stir up persecution against those who fear God, since they can in no way be induced to assent to their communion. 2. When our affairs were in that state, the letter of your holiness was delivered, which brings much consolation by the sincerity of your will, the integrity and candor of your mind, and the communion of sentiment, refreshing our dejected spirit. For it is no longer a contest about words, but about things; nor is it the time to collect sayings and arguments (for to what end, when the judges are so corrupt?); but those who love God must needs resist the works themselves bravely, prepared to face any danger for the right faith, lest they be defiled by the company of the impious. Wherefore I did not think it useful to hand over even the Syntagma of your holiness to the emperor, nor indeed without danger, since it can help in nothing in the present state of affairs except to move to laughter and mockery those who seem wise in their own folly and are rashly driven by an inordinate and dark spirit. For already, because of our sins, that saying has been fulfilled: I will give boys as their princes, and the effeminate shall rule over them. And would that He who alone can do all things might be willing to restore His Church, and to calm the present storm, appeased by your prayers, which may always be with us. Mark of Ephesus and of all Asia.
