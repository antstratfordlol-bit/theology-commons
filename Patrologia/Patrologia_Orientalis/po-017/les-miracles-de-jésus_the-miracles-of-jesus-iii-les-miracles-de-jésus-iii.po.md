### THE MIRACLES OF JESUS
፳፪ ፡ ተኣምር ፡ ዘገብረ ፡ አግዚእ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ ክርስቶስ ፡ በፅፀ ፡ ዘይት ፡፡ "በረከተ ፡ ጸጋሁ ፡ የሀሉ ፡ ምስለ ፡ ገብሩ ፡ ወልደ ፣፡ ዋሕድ ፡ ለዓለመ ፡ ዓለም ፡=፡
TWENTY-FIRST MIRACLE: THE OLIVE BRANCH PLANTED BY ZACCHAEUS Twenty-first miracle that the Lord Jesus Christ performed on an olive tree. * May the blessing of His grace be with His servant Walda-Wahed for ever and ever!
ወእንዘ ፡ የኃልፍ ፡ እግዚእ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ በሀገረ ፡ ዳዊት ፡ ዘይእቲ፣ ምድረ ፡ ኢየሩሳሌ
1 While the Lord Jesus was passing through the city of David [Dawit], that is to say the territory of Jerusalem [Iyarusalem], and with him (were)


[p. 788] {#po.17.p788}
ም ፡ወምስሌሁ ፡ ስምዖን ፡ ወእንድርያስ ፡ ያዕቆብ ፡ ወዮሐንስ ፡፡ ወርእየ ፡በሀየ፣ብእሴ ፡ አረጋዌ ፡ እንዘ ፣ ይበኪ ፡ ወይብል ፡ ሀበኒ ፡ እግዚኦ ፡ ዘአፈዲ ፡ ቦቱ ፡ዕዳየ ፣ ወይቤሎ ፡ እግዚእ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ ምንት ፡ ረከበከ ፡ ወምንት፣ ውእቱ ፡አዳከ ፡።፡ አውሥአ ፡ውእቱ ፡ ብእ ሲ ፡ ወይቤ ፡ ምንት ፡ ይበቀዓኒ ፡ ለአመ ፡ ነገርኩከ ፡= ወይቤሎ ፡ እግዚእ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ ኢትኅብአኒ ፡ ዘኬዎስ ፣ ግብረከ ፡ እስመ ፡ አነ ፡ ውእቱ ፡ ዘኣብዕል ፡ ወዘየኃሥሠኒ ፡ ኢይነዲ ፡ ለግሙራ ፡ ወአነ ፡ ህልው ፡ ለዓለም ፡፡
Simon [Sem'on] and Andrew [Endryas], James [Ya'qob] and John [Yohannes], he saw there an old man who was weeping and saying: "Give me, O Lord, wherewith to pay my debt." The Lord Jesus said to him: "What has happened to you and what is your debt?" This man answered and said: "What will it profit me to explain (it) to you?" The Lord Jesus said to him: "Do not hide from me, Zacchaeus [Zakewos], your affair, for it is I who enrich. He who seeks me will never be poor. I am existing for eternity."
ወሶበ ፡ ሰምዓ ፡ አረጋዊ ፡ እንዘ ፣ ይጹውዖ ፡ በስሙ ፡ እንዘ ፣ ኢያኦምሮ ፡ አፍጠነ፣ ወሰገደ ፡ ሎቱ ፡ ወይቤሎ ፡ ተሣሃለኒ ፡ እግዚእየ ፡ ወአድኅነኒ ፡ እምዕዳየ ፡ እስመ ፡ ሀለ ዉኒ፣፡ አዕዋመ ፡ ዘይት ፡ ውስተ ፡ ገነትየ ፡ ወበዛቲ ፡ ዓመት ፡ የብሱ ፣ ዙሎሙ ፡ ወላዕሌ የ ፡ እዳ ፡ ዘነሣእኩ ፡ ከመ ፡ ”እስልጥ ፡ አመ ፡ ይፈርዩ ፡አዕዋመ › ዘይትየ =፡ ወይቤሎ ፣ እግዚእ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ እስፍንቱ ፡ አፅዋም ፡ እለ ፣ የብሱ ፡ እምዘይትከ ፡፡ ወይቤሎ ፡ ዘኬ ዎስ ፡ ፻ወጓአዕዋም ፡ ሀለዉኒ ፡ በአሐቲ ፡ ገራህት ፡ ወዙሎሙ ፡ የብሱ ፡፡
2 When the old man heard that He called him by his name, without knowing him, he hastened to worship Him. He said to Him: "Be propitious to me, my Lord, and deliver me from my debt. Indeed, I have olive trees in my garden, and, this year, they have all been barren. (There remains) upon me the debt that I contracted, (intending to) * pay it off when my olive trees would bear fruit." The Lord Jesus said to him: "How many trees are there that are barren among your olive trees?" Zacchaeus said to him: "I have one hundred and forty trees in one (single) field: all are barren."


[p. 789] {#po.17.p789}
ወይቤሎ ፡ እግዚእ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ አምጽእ ፡ ኀቤየ ፡ በትረ ፡ አምዘይትከ ።፡ ወአምጽአ ፡ ዘኬዎስ ፡ በትረ ፡ እምአላንቱ ፡ አዕዋም ፡ አለ ፡ የብሱ ፣ ወነሥኣ ፡ እግዚአ ፣ ኢየሱስ ፡ ወባረከ ፡ ላዕሌሃ ፡ ወይቤሎ ፡ ሑር ፡ ኦአረጋይ ፡ ወትክላ ፡ ለዛቲ ፡ በትር ፡ ማእከለ ፡ ገራ ሀትክ ፡ እስመ ፡ እምኔሃ ፡ይከውን ፡፣ ብዕልክ ፡=፡ አእምር ፡ እስመ ፡ ትከውን፣ ዛቲ ፡ በትር ፡ ኦመ ፡ ሸይት ፡፣ ዘ፻አዕፁቂሃ ፡ ወኢትየብስ ፡ ለዝሉፉ ፡ ወትከውን ፣ ምክሆሙ ፡ ለኩሎ ሙ ፡ አሕዛብ ።፡ ወየኃሥሥዋ ፡ እምአጽናፈ ፡ ምድር ፣ ወይትባረኩ ፡ ባቲ ፡ ወትከውኖ ሙ ፡ ፍሥሓ ፡ ወሐሜተ ፡፡ ወኩሉ ፡ ዘይነሥእአ ፡ ፅፀ ፡ እምዛቲ ፡ በትር ፡ አው ፡ እምቄ ጽላ ፡ ወያነብሮ ፡ ውስተ ፡ ቤቱ ፡ ይሄሉ ፡ በረከትየ ፡ ውስቴታ ፣ ወ” ኢይፈርሁ ፡ ሰብአ ፡ ይእቲ ፡ ቤት ፡ እምአጋንንት ፡= ወእመቦ ፡ ዘተሀበለ ፡ ብእሲ ፡ ወቦአ ፡ ቤተ ፣ ኀበ ፡ሀ ሎ ፣ ውስቴታ ፡ እምዛቲ ፡ በትር ፡ ይእኅዞ ፡ ፍርሃት ፡ ወረዓድ ፡፣ እስከ ፡ አመ ፡ ይወጽ እ እምኔሃ =
3 The Lord Jesus said to him: "Bring me a branch from your olive trees." Zacchaeus brought a branch from those trees that were barren. The Lord Jesus took it and blessed it. He said to him: "Go, O old man, and plant this branch in the middle of your field, for from it will come your wealth. Know that this branch will become an olive tree with three boughs, that it will never be barren, and that it will become the pride of all peoples. They will seek it from the ends of the earth and they will be blessed by it. It will become for them joy and gladness. Whoever takes a twig from this branch or its leaves and puts it in his house (will be protected): my blessing will be in (that house) and the people of that house * will not fear demons. If any individual dares to enter (into) the house where (a twig) of this branch is found, fear and trembling will seize him, to the point that he will leave (that house)."


[p. 790] {#po.17.p790}
ወነሥኣ ፡ ለእንታክቲ ፡ በትር ፡ ዝኩ ፡ አረጋዊ ፣ ዘይሰመይ ፡ ዘኬዎስ ፡ እምእዴሁ ፡ ለእግዚአ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ ወተከላ ፡ ማእከለ ፡ ገራሀቱ ፡ ወበጊዜሃ ፡ ሠረፀት ፣ ወአውጽአት ፣ ፋጽለ ፡=
4 The old man, who was called Zacchaeus, took this branch from the hand of the Lord Jesus and planted it in the middle of his field. Immediately it budded and produced leaves.
ን ወሶበ ፡ ርእየ ፡ አረጋይ ፥ዘንተ ፡ አንከረ ፡ ፈድፋደ ፡፡ ወሖረ ፡ ኅበ ፡ እግዚአ ፡ ኢየ ሱስ ፡ ወሰገደ ፡ ቅድሜሁ ፡ ወይቤሎ ፡፣ አንተ ፡ ውእቱ ፡ ክርስቶስ ፡ ወልደ ፡ እግዚአብ ሔር ፡ ፈራቂሁ ፡ ለዓለም ።፡ ወሖረ ፡ ኅበ ፡ ሰብአ ፡ ቤቱ ፡ ወነገሮሙ ፡ ግብሮ ፡ ለእግዚእ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ ዘርእየ ፡ እ ምተአምራቲሁ ፡፡ ወአምኑ ፣ ዙሎሙ ፡ ሰብአ ፣ ቤቱ ፡ ወእምሕዝበ ፡ አይሁድ ፣ በይእ
When the old man saw this, he was extremely astonished. He went (to find) the Lord Jesus, prostrated himself before Him, and said to Him: "You are the Christ, the Son of the Lord, the Redeemer of the world." He went to the people of his house and told them of the work of the Lord Jesus, which he had seen (himself) among His miracles. All the people of his house and of the Jewish people believed that day.
ቲ፡ ዕለት ፡=፡ ወአምዝ ፡ ሖረ ፡ ዘኬዎስ ፡ ኅበ ፡ በትረ ፡ ዘይት ፡ ዘሠረፀት ፡ ወአፍረየት ፡ ወአጾረ ፡
6 Then Zacchaeus went to the olive branch that had grown and had borne fruit.


[p. 791] {#po.17.p791}
እምኔሃ ፡ ማጾረ ፡ ብአሲ ፡ በይእቲ ፡ ፅለት። ወዓዲ ፡ በ፳ወዒኒዕለት ፣ መል” አ ፡ እምኔሃ ፡ ፲ ወ፻ምክያደ ፡ ወዘተርፈ ፡ በልዑ ፡ እምኔሁ ፡ ፍሬሁ ፡= ወምክንያተ ፡ ብዕሉ ፡ ለዘኬዎ ስ ፡ ኮነ ፡ አምፍሬሃ ፡ ለይእቲ ፡ በትር ፡=፡ ወእምድኅረ ፡ አስተጋብአ ፡ ዘኬዎስ ፡ ቅብዓ ፡ ወፍሬ ፡ ዘይት ፡ ዘወፅአ ፡ እምይእቲ፣ በትር ፡ በጽሐ ፡ ኀበ ፡ እግዚእ ፣ ኢየሱስ ፣ ወይቤሎ ፣ ኣአኩተከ ፡ እግዚእየ ፣ ወአም ላኪየ ፡ በእንተ ፡ ኩሉ ፡ዘወሀብከኒ ፡ወኣአኩተከ ፡ በእንተ ፡ ኩሉ ዘገበርከ ፡ ሊተ ፡ ሠና ያተ ወዜነዎ ፡ ኩሎ ፡ ዘረከበ ፣ በረከተ ፡ እምይአቲ ፡ በትረ ፡ ዘይት ፡፡ ወዓዲ ፡ ይቤ ሎ ፡ ኣስተበቀዓከ ፡ አግዚአየ ፡ ወአምላኪየ ፡ ከመ ፡ ተሀበኒ ፡ ለለዓሙ ፡ በከመ ፡ ወሀብ ከኒ ፡ ይእዜ ።፡ ወይቤሎ ፡ እግዚአ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ አእምር ፡ ከመ ፡ ዛቲ ፡ በትር ፡ ትሁበከ፡፣ ለለኩሉ ፡ ዓመት ፡ በከመ ፡ ወሀበተከ ፡ በዛቲ ፡ ዓመት ፡=፡ በረከተ ፡ ጸጋሁ ፡ የሀሉ ፡ ምስሌነ ፡ አሜን ፡=፡
He had forty man-loads brought from its (yield) that day. Furthermore, in twenty-seven days * he filled thirteen (wine-press) containers (with the fruits of this branch). The fruits that remained were eaten. The cause of Zacchaeus's wealth came from the fruits of this branch. 7 After Zacchaeus had gathered the oil and the olives that came from this branch, he came (to find) the Lord Jesus and said to Him: "I give thanks to You, my Lord and my God, for all that You have given me. I give thanks to You for all the benefits that You have accomplished for me." He told Him all the blessings he had found in this olive branch. Furthermore, he said to Him: "I beseech You, my Lord and my God, to give me, each year, as You have given me now." The Lord Jesus said to him: "Know that this branch will give you each year, as it has given you this year." May the blessing of His grace be with us! Amen.

### TWENTY-SECOND MIRACLE

RESURRECTION OF SARAH, REBECCA, AND RACHEL. Twenty-second miracle of Our Lord and Our Savior Jesus Christ at the tomb of Rachel [Rähël]. May the blessing of His grace be with His servant Gabra-Wâhed for ages of ages! Amen.
