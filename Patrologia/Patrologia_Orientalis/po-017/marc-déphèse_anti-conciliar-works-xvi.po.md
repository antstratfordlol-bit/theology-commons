But just as one who is outside the light must be in darkness, so one who has deviated even a little from the truth may rightly be said to be already covered by falsehood; although, to be sure, one may speak of a middle between light and darkness which we call dawn or twilight, yet no one, however much he may labor, could ever invent a middle between truth and falsehood.
