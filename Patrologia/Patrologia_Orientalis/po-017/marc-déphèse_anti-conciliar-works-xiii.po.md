And what need is there to say more? All the doctors of the Church, all the synods, all the divine scriptures exhort us to avoid men of another sect and to flee their communion. Why should I, spurning all these, follow those who contend for a union to be entered into under the guise of a feigned peace, who adulterate the sacrosanct Symbol, and assert the Son to be another cause of the Holy Spirit? For I omit the other absurdities for the present, of which even one would be enough of a reason why we should depart from them. May that never happen, O good Paraclete, nor may I myself ever wander from me and from sound opinion, but continuously and sharply pursuing your doctrine and the blessed men inspired by you, may I finally be gathered to my fathers, bringing back from here, if nothing else, the right faith.
