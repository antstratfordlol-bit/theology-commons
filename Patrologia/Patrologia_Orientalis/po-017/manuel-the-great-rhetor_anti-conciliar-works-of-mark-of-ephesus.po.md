This most holy Mark, therefore, was a child and alumnus of the royal city itself. From his first hair, so to speak, he was addicted by his parents to the learning of common disciplines; which, when he had run through in a short time like some bird, he surpassed all his fellow students and contemporaries. Then, having been admitted to the sacred order of the greater Church, with the blessing and sacred prayer imparted by that most holy and most wise among the patriarchs, Euthymios, he placed all his study in the divinely inspired scriptures; and soon, having taken the monastic habit in the sacred and great monastery of the Mangana, he devoted himself entirely to the solitary life. He contained himself so much in the monastery and his own cell with the study of keeping solitude, that he did not even give the power of visiting to his relatives and friends and those joined to him by birth, but he spent all his effort night and day in the perpetual meditation of divine letters, whence he acquired for himself that sea of opinions which the works published by him testify. Finally, having bowed his neck under the divine yoke of the priesthood, and not long after having been constituted bishop of the Ephesians by the great and most holy Church, unwilling indeed, but with many friends asking, he amplified from then on the greater arena of cultivating virtue and increased it according to his strength.

Τὴν τοῦ αἰτήματος οὖν δεξάμενος πλήρωσιν, εὔχου ἡμῖν τοῖς εὐτελέσι τῷ φωτὶ τῆς ὄντως περιαυγάζεσθαι διὰ παντὸς ἡ ἀληθείας καὶ δαψιλῶς ἐμφορηθῆναι τῆς θείας χάρι-- τος καὶ τῆς ἐλλάμψεως τῆς ἀληθοῦς τυχεῖν ταπεινώ-- σεώς τε καὶ πρᾳότητος, ἵνα τὴν παροῦσαν ζωὴν εἰς δόξαν Χριστοῦ καὶ τῆς αὐτοῦ ἐχκλησίας διαδιθά- σαντες, τύχοιμεν καὶ τῆς μερίδος ἐκεῖσε τῶν σῳζο-- μένων, ἐλέει καὶ χάριτι τοῦ Κυρίου καὶ Θεοῦ καὶ Σωτῆρος ἡμῶν ᾿Ιησοῦ Χριστοῦ, ᾧ ἡ δόξα καὶ ἢ προσκύνησις εἷς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων, ἀμήν.
Truly, for their just condemnation and punishment will be that inextinguishable outer fire, by which they are to be seized there, which is prepared for the devil and his angels. Just as light lacking evening will receive the just; but since the Church occupies the place of God on holy ground, she must entirely carry out what belongs to her office, composing herself in all things after his example, so that in that way she may restrain her children, namely all the faithful, and render some imitators of virtue and impel them to the same zeal for holiness with which those men burned. Therefore, when you have received the solution to your petition, pray for us miserable ones, that, being everywhere suffused with the light of the very truth and abundantly filled with divine grace, we may attain true illumination and humility and meekness, so that, with the present life transferred to the glory of Christ and his Church, we may obtain a part there among those being saved, by the mercy and grace of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ, to whom be glory and adoration for ever and ever, amen.
