1. We who have received the exposition of the mystical liturgy from the holy apostles and their successors, the doctors of the Church, find among none of them that the gift of the Eucharist is sanctified and perfected by the Lord's words alone, and transformed into the very Body and Blood of the Lord; but that the words which are recited first are, by the unanimous consent of all, intended to recall to our memory the event that took place, and to discharge, as it were, the force and power into the proposed gifts for their transformation: but that the prayer and blessing of the priest which follows afterwards, in reality transforms the gifts into that very prototype, the Body and Blood of the Lord. This very series of words also testifies to this, which are found among us in agreement and harmony; and this whole prayer will consist of these.
