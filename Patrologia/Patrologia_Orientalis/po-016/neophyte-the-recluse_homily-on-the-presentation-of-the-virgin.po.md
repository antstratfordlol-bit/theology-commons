Did he not find a remedy that would heal the universal rot? Did the springs of rivers and fountains cover the sea of humanity and the abyss of mercy? By no means. But since it did not befit that infinite mercy to leave its own creature as food for the enemy, and sin demanded a just expiation, namely, justice and holiness, by the command of God, that most immaculate and chaste Virgin Mary is born from Joachim and Anna, so that one of the Trinity, with the other two consenting, taking flesh from her, might destroy sin in his flesh. What kind of virgin, therefore, was it necessary for her to be? Surely purer than the very rays of the sun, so that she might impart most immaculate and pure flesh to the sun of justice. But where was it necessary for such a virgin to be nourished and to dwell? In a common and polluted house? Far from it; but surely in those very Holy of Holies, as one who was holier even than them and was to lend flesh to the most holy Word and God. Whom, at three years old, her parents today introduce into the Holy of Holies with a large choir of virgins and splendid lamps, gloriously fulfilling the promises, just as her grandfather, David, foretold concerning the following virgins: Virgins, he says, shall be brought to the King after her; her neighbors shall be brought to you; they shall be brought in gladness and exultation; they shall be led into the temple of the king.
