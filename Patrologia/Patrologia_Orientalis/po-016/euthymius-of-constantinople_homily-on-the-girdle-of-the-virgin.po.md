Today the pure and whole temple of the glorious daughter of God is dedicated, into which even those who are in the mountains and caves, coming in haste, out of good report, as the Scripture says, with love that fattens the bones, acquire love for themselves, and return home joyful, honoring the highest temple of the ever-virgin Mary.

Today the temple, higher than all temples, of the Most Pure is dedicated, in which many of those who are defiled by the gravest sins are brought back to piety and highest purity and become rivals of the angels. Today the temple of the patroness and protectress of all Christians is dedicated, in which even those who shine with the highest piety are made more beautiful, and receive light from light, adorned with the true humility of Christ. Today the temple of the most praised and immaculate maiden is dedicated, into which whoever enters out of love and faith and confesses his grave deeds, promising that he will cease from harmful things, immediately, O miracle, finds absolution and exultation of heart. For I said, he says: I will declare my iniquity against myself, and you have forgiven the impiety of my heart. Today the temple of the altogether blessed and venerable young girl and crown of the prophets is dedicated, in which the paralyzed are raised, the blind receive sight, and those who suffer from horror and fever are cured. Do not reject our constant prayers, do not reject them, O most immaculate one. Defend us according to your great and abundant mercy, O Lady. Tear up the scroll of our immense evils with your vigilant intercessions, O most benign one. Reconcile us before the end to your merciful and just judge, the Son and God. For we are clearly destitute of all confidence and freedom, because of our negligence and the multitude of sins. For the prayer of the Mother is most powerful, O most praised one, to reconcile the benevolence of the Son, as we believe. Since therefore you have maternal and immense freedom toward him, who was born of you ineffably without flux, make us also partakers of eternal goods, and deliver us from every heavy affliction that is here and there—for all things are possible to you, O altogether blessed and most glorious one—so that, guarded and protected by you and boasting of you, we may offer glory and thanksgiving in this venerable temple and wherever we may be, to the Father and to the holy Son and to the Spirit, now and unto the eternal ages of ages. Amen.
