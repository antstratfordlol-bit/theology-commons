Prayer of the "Trisagion". Compassionate and merciful, long-suffering and of much mercy and truly Lord, look from your prepared dwelling and hear us your suppliants and free us from every diabolical and human temptation and do not turn your help away from us nor impose upon us disciplines heavier than our strength. For we are not fit to overcome what happens against us, but you, Lord, are able to save from all adversities. Save us, God, from the difficulties of this world according to your kindness, that entering with a pure conscience to your holy altar, we may send to you the blessed and thrice-holy hymn with the heavenly virtues without condemnation and, when we have completed the liturgy that pleases you and the divine, we may be deemed worthy of eternal life, exclamation: because you are holy, O Lord our God, and you dwell and rest in the saints and to you we send glory and the thrice-holy hymn to the Father and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of ages, amen.
