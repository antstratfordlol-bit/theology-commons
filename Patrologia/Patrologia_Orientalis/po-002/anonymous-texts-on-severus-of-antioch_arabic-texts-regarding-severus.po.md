by the name of Al-Mu'allaqa (the Suspended) in Cairo. He stopped near the door while the matran (the archbishop) was saying the mass at the altar. When he made the sign of the cross with the cross over the crowd, according to the order of the liturgy, the angels took the paten and the chalice from the altar before him. Turning back to finish the mass, he found neither paten nor chalice before him. || He wept much and remained astonished, not knowing the cause. A voice said to him: "It is because you made the sign of the cross over the lord of the patriarchs, the Patriarch of Antioch." He replied: "O my Lord, I did not know that he was present." Now all the crowd heard him. The matran then came out of the choir, prostrated himself, and said to him: "O our father... enter, bless, and say the mass." He replied: "I am not worthy of it." But the other replied: "You, O our father, Patriarch of Antioch, the city loved by God, come, bless, and say the mass." Now our father the patriarch did not know Coptic. He entered therefore and blessed; and the host returned to its place, as before. The father Anba Severus finished the mass and offered the chalice. The love between the community of the Copts and the Syrians increased until the time of our father Youhana (John) ibn Chouchane, Patriarch of Antioch.
