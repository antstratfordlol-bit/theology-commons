How could the faithful not be moved with reason to cry out these words, when the scepter of the Roman empire is entrusted to emperors so pious and so Christian, that the freedom and the faculty to speak frankly are granted to those who profess the orthodox religion? But in the midst of the general feast of the entire world, alone among all, or at least with very few others, the church of Antioch is filled with sadness and mourning. It suffers because it has received, we do not know how, a wolf instead of a shepherd. What is the tragedy that the crimes perpetrated by Severus have not surpassed? First - not to speak of his life out of regard for your ears - he has manifestly never communicated with any church.
