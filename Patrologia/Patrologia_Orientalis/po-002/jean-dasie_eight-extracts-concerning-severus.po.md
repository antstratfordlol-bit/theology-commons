2 Before this (before the death of Empress Theodora, which occurred in 548), the blessed Severus was called by the emperor to the imperial city for the sake of the peace of the Church. He came, at the pressing insistence of everyone, saying: "I am going up because it is the will of all, but it is not possible that anything will ever be done by those who exercise power." He went up, therefore, and spent two years working and teaching. When he saw that his prediction—"that nothing would be done"—was being fulfilled, he returned to the regions situated south of Alexandria, where he lived constantly, in the midst of the desert, in tranquility, asceticism, and the commerce of the books of the Fathers. He accomplished a great labor and austerity in perfection. After having spent several years in persecution, he fell ill. He was then transported from the desert to the city of Egypt called Xois, and there he reached the end of his struggle and set out toward Our Lord.
