6 When Saint Severus was called by the emperor, for the matter of the union, he also resided in the empress's palace; and these three patriarchs (Theodosius, Anthimus, Severus) found themselves in familiarity with one another. Saint Severus was summoned by order of the emperor and the empress, with a view to the peace of the Churches. He remained a year and a half in Constantinople, demonstrating the truth of the faith, and the transgression of the Synod of Chalcedon. The emperor gathered many controversialists to convince him. Several times he confounded them and showed that they were schismatic and mistaken; but he obtained no advantage, and they did not convert from their error. That is why he took his leave and returned with the monks who accompanied him.
