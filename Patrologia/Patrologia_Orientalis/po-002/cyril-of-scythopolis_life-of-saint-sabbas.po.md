Dominated by an extraordinary boastfulness and strong with the support of the emperor, Severus struck the council of Chalcedon with innumerable anathemas. He strove to strengthen the heterodoxy of Eutyches, and proclaimed that the unique nature (which Our Lord Jesus Christ possesses according to him), after He became flesh from the Virgin, was a corruptible nature. In his love for troubles, he invented many novelties against the dogmas and orthodox laws of the Church. He received, in fact, the second council of Ephesus, that impious council of robbers, and declared it similar to the first council that met at Ephesus. He proclaimed equal, among the doctors, Cyril, the venerable bishop of Alexandria, and Dioscorus, who had admitted to his communion, as having the same opinions as him, Eutyches the heretic, and who had deposed and killed the most holy and most orthodox Flavian, the archbishop of the imperial city. Sinking more and more into impiety, the same Severus sharpened his tongue to blaspheme against God, and he divided, in his discourses, the divinity which is one and indivisible in the Trinity. He said and affirmed that the hypostasis was the nature and the nature the hypostasis. As he

To the emperor most loved by God and most pious, Augustus and autocrat by the grace of God, to the friend of Christ Flavius Anastasius: request and supplication on the part of the archimandrites Theodosius and Sabas and on the part of all the other hegumens and monks who inhabit the holy city of God, all the desert that surrounds it and the banks of the Jordan. The sovereign of all things, our God and Lord Jesus Christ, the only-begotten son of God, etc. . The author and instigator of all these evils was Severus, the patriarch of Antioch who was acephalous and schismatic from the beginning and from the principle, who was named patriarch for his own loss and for that of the entire State, with the consent of God, because of our sins; who anathematized our holy Fathers who confirmed in all things the apostolic faith as it was defined and transmitted to us by the holy Fathers of the council of Nicaea, and who enlightened the whole world by it.
