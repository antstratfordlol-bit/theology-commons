1 Blessed be the glorious Epiphany of Christ our savior who, in his divine splendor, illuminated the creatures who dwelt in darkness and the shadows of death and directed stumbling steps onto the leveled path of peace. Introduction 2 Indeed, by the manifestation of Christ, leveled paths were shown to men so that in them, from the darkness of idolatry, they might return to God his Father who sent him for the salvation of us all. 3 For, by the manifestation of Christ, we have known him, him with his adorable Father and his Holy Spirit. By the manifestation of Christ, we have known ourselves as well as one another. 4 By the manifestation of Christ, the darkened house in which we dwelt appeared to us, and we returned in search of our place of origin. Thanks to the manifestation of Christ, we were able to see the obstacles placed before our steps, and through the knowledge we received of them, we were able to avoid their dangers. 5 In the manifestation of Christ, this obscure dwelling of our race was illuminated and everything in it appeared in its place and according to its nature. By the light that comes from it, he illuminated the dwellings of our souls, and the closed eyes of intimate thoughts were opened. 6 By it, he illuminated the obscure dwellings over which darkness was spread, and we were able * to walk, with clear sight, without stumbling over obstacles.
