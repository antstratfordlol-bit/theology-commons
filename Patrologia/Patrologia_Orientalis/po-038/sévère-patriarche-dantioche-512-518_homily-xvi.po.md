### HOMILY XVI

1 Is there anyone among you, of those gathered here, who, loving to be instructed and to apply himself to hearing the divine words, has searched in private, or inquired of his neighbor while seeking with him, why, in these venerable and holy days of the fast, we read the creation of the world, which was written for us by Moses? Or do you wish that we ourselves now give you a discourse on this subject? Will you truly say that you wish it? Do you pretend not to be informed, and will you choose to say and endure everything, only to launch us into the race of the word and bring us to the stadium of teaching? It is thus that you hear our modest words with pleasure. That is why I, too, present myself before the richness of your affection, while I am poor, and I voluntarily forget the proverb which says: Do not present yourself, if you are poor, before the rich, and I come to speak, while I have absolutely no preparation for (this) instruction.

2 But, do me this favor of listening to me in silence and of conducting yourselves properly, without bumping into one another while trying to jostle those who are standing nearby, as if to gather loot. Otherwise, you do yourselves harm as well, by losing the hearing because of the noise you make; and we, for our part, are disconcerted and troubled, being forced to repeat the same things several times and to return again to the same subjects. Now we must hear the divine book saying: The words of the wise are heard in calm.

32 For I would pray to be myself anathema, (separated) from Christ for my brothers and my kinsmen according to the flesh, they who are Israelites, to whom belong the filial adoption, the glory, the covenants, the legislation. But because it is for the Jews that he wants to be anathema, then especially it was shown that it is for Christ only that he was asking this, because it is to them that belong the filial adoption, the glory, the service, and the promises, for the law also (comes) from there. What he was saying regarding the Messiah and all the covenants with them, and the Messiah coming from them, and their ancestors coming from them who had received the promises of all this; but it is there precisely that was the obstacle: they fell (deprived) of all goods, it is for that reason that he says: I am in sadness, but, even if I am separated and excommunicated from the troop of Christ, I will not be from His love.

33 No, may that not be! because, even that, it is out of love for Him that I would do it, but I would have received from these delights and from this glory, because my Lord is not blasphemed, since I hear people say: it is for the sake of form that these things were accomplished: He promised to some and He gave to others; He was born of some and He saved others from them; He promised to the ancestors of the Jews and He let their sons fall; those who never knew Him, He took away their goods. As therefore He does not say these things nor others similar, would they be said regarding my master; and even if they were said iniquitously, behold I will throw myself out of the kingdom and out of the ineffable glory and I will endure all evils, but I thought that there would be a great consolation in my suffering, namely that the one whom I love, I will no longer hear from now on being the object of blasphemy.
