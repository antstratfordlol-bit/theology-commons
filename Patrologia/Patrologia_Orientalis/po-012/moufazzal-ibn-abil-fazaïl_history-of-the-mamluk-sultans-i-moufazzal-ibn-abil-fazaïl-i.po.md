Praise to God who is the Primordial, and who had no beginning; who will be the Last, and who will have no end; who knows the absolute truths of all things, and whose holy name is invoked in all languages by the universality of men. I praise him with the praise of a being who gives thanks to him, and who recognizes his own impotence; I proclaim that he has need of neither counselor, nor general, nor minister; I address to him the thanksgivings of a servant who loves him with a sincere love, because he is the most worthy of it and deserves it the most. When these ambassadors had obtained an audience with the sultan, they informed him of what had happened to them (and what had been taken from them). The sultan then sent an officer to Alexandria to forbid the idolatrous merchants who were there from engaging in financial operations and undertaking (commercial) voyages, as long as they had not reimbursed the value of the objects that their coreligionists (the Franks of Saint-Jean * d'Acre) had seized. The letter that was in the hands of the ambassadors of the house of Berké contained the detail of all the countries that had been under the power of the Muslims and of which the house of Hulagu had taken possession. The ambassadors asked the sultan of Egypt to assist them in their struggle against the house of Hulagu, and to help them break its power.
