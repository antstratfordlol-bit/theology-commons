The difference of the natures does not cause any damage to the unity of the Son, but just as the corruptible body is one thing and the immortal soul is another, and from the two, a single man is constituted; so from the mortal and the immortal, from the corruptible and the incorruptible, from that which is subject to a beginning and that which has no * beginning - that is to say from God the Word - I confess a single person (prosopon) of the Son.
