1. The sun, when it shines on the earth, my dear friends, powerfully shows its strength. When the darkness ceases, when it is dissipated before it and disappears, the thieves leave the roads and hide, the wicked animals take refuge in their dens and fall silent, creation is rejoiced by its desired ray and man goes out to his work and his occupation until the evening, as David also said. 2. Even when the fear of God shines in the soul of the faithful, the darkness of error is dissipated before it and ceases, the evil demons, like thieves, flee and hide; the passions of sin rest in their dens, like animals, and the soul delights and rejoices in the light of the truth; it occupies itself with the work of justice and it obeys the salutary law of its master. 3. Such was at the beginning the occupation of the head of our race, Adam, in Paradise; the Creator gave him a formidable dominion and great power over everything that appears;

When he had listened to the word of the serpent, who spoke to him because he was jealous of his prosperity, he fell from the honor he had and he was subjected to the sufferings of death; he who was formidable and powerful fell and became weak, and he who had been created master of everything, became the servant of the creatures; he who had worn a garment (στολή) of glory needed the leaves of the fig tree. 5. But our good God did not let his creation perish and did not permit his image to be despised; he did not deprive of his help the son of the free chained by the tyrant; what did he do? Because the adversary, who had humiliated our race, was hidden, he secretly clothed our race and he engaged in combat with our adversary in order to defeat him with the help of the body that he defeated. The principle of our salvation went out into the desert, as you have heard, to strike down the army commander of the demons before all the cohorts of the just.
