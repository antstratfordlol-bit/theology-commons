After that (Peter) exhorts (them) as is fitting, to acquire piety vis-à-vis the One who called them. He also advises to honor kings and he prescribes to women to be in agreement with their husbands; he invites them a little also to cultivate, as is fitting, modesty. He declares that the preaching of salvation was done by the Lord, even in the underworld from His resurrection; (he announces) also the resurrection to come and the judgment in the flesh of those who, already dead, wait, they too, for the grace of the resurrection. He indicates that the end of all things is near; that is why one must be ready, to know how one will answer before the King. And it is on this that he finishes his letter.

In beginning, he proclaims the faith that the prophets had announced in advance, for the prophecies concerning the Savior were not like human words said by chance, but they were inspired by God. After that, (Peter) commands (them) not to follow the impostors; their company would lead them into the fall that the guilty angels knew. He also foresees days when there will be seducers who will say to deceive men: "False is the story of the return of Christ, for it is always promised that he will come and he never appears." (Peter) recommends to all to keep away from such people and not to be discouraged because of the time (that passes). Indeed, all (time is) a small thing and disappears before the Lord, for whom a day is like a thousand years and a thousand years like a day. He insists firmly that all prepare themselves through good works, in view of the day of the Lord which will occur suddenly and unexpectedly. He also commands to receive with pleasure the letters of the apostle Paul and he reproves those who disdain them. He further prescribes to scrutinize all the inspired Scriptures, and it is on this that he concludes his letter.

Յորժամ ցանկն արկ շուրջանակի, Հրաման տայ աճել, զի ոչ է բաւական առնուլ միայն զսերմն, այլ բուսուցանել եւ աճեցուցանելով տալ պտուղ եւ զաւրդեութիւն ընդունել, ապա Թէ ոչ դատ առնու ընդ նոսա սերմանողն. Ըստ որում է ընդ վրիպեալ Իսրայելի, մանաւանդ թէ եւս առաւել. Զի սոքա յաներեկ աւուրն ունին տալ քննութիւն գործոց, յորժամ արդարութեամբ փառատրի, այժմ փառատրեալն ի շնորհաց: Եւ ի դատողութեան նստի աթոռ, որ այժմ է յողորմութեան. Եւ ի ժխտողացն ոչ զամպսն արգելու, այլ ի Հուր յաւիտենից արկանէ. Որդի լիցուք փրկեալք մեք Քրիստոսի Յիսուսի որում փառք յաւիտեանս. Ամէն:
When one surrounds (a field) with a hedge, it is to allow growth. It is not enough, in fact, that it only receives seed, but (it is necessary) to make it grow, and, when it has grown, to make it bear fruit and prosper, otherwise the sower passes judgment against it. This is how the sinful house of Israel (will be judged), and even more severely; for, during the day without decline, they will have to account for their works, when he whom grace already makes glorious will be glorified in all justice. And at the tribunal will sit he who, now, exercises mercy, and it is not the clouds of heaven that he will forbid to the renegades, but he will cast them into eternal fire. We, we will be saved from it by Christ Jesus, to whom be glory for the ages. Amen.
