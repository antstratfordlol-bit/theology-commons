### PREAMBLE
ثم اني اثبت تحقيق ذلك من اثار المسيح كما نطقت عنه الانبيا انه ياتي الى العالم ويصنع بينهم الايات والمعجزات وما يصنع به بني اسراييل من الهوان والصلب كما نطقت عنهم الانبيا كذلك اتى وصنع بيتهم المعجزات وابرا اسقامهم واشفا مرضاهم واظهر ذلك وائبته وابينه. اذ بين كل شي صنعه من الايات والمعجزات فى المكان الذي صنعه فيه. اذ ذلك المكان نفسه يشهد لنا بذلك المعجز الذي ينعة فيه بالاثارات والاشاير الظاهرة فيه. اذ اعطانا ذلك الاماكن جميعها التي صنع فيها تلك الايات والعجايب. اعطها لنا ملكا مويد للابد لتشهد لنا بصحة ما صنع من الايات والمعجزات كما نطقت عنه الانبيا الاولين السابقين له لمجيه انه ياتي ويفعلهم وقد انا وفعلهم جميعهم كما يشهد لنا اماكنهم الباقية الى اليوم والى الابد شهادة ثابتة وانه هو المسيح المتنبى عنه ولا بقي مسيح اخر ياتى بعده وخاب ١ انتظار اليهود لمجيه وليس بقي يجى الا فى مجيه الثانى لمداييتهم ومحكماة شعبه وخاصته التي لم تومن به اما امن منهم من جميع الامم فهم نحن التي جعلنا امته الجديدة واعطانا ارضه وسماه مورنًا دايمًا للابد فمن تلك الاماكن التي صنع فيها اياته واعطاها لنا ذكرًا مويدًا وشهادة ثابتة كما يشهد ذلك المكان لنفسه من الاثارات والاشاير ليلا يقول المعترض منهم والجاحد ان هذا الذي اتى ليس هو المنتظر كما يزعمون فقد اظهرنا لهم كذيهم وظلالتهم من كتبهم كما نطقت عنه الانبيا في كتبهم انه ياتي بينهم ويفعل هذه الرسوم ويفعلوا هم به ما قد تقدمت الانبيا وذكروه عنهم انهم يفعلوا به هكدى

Thus He came and performed miracles among them, He treated their infirmities and healed their sick, and I illustrate this, confirm it, and demonstrate it. Behold, He manifested all that He performed of signs and miracles in the place where He performed them, and such a place itself bears witness for us to such a miracle that He performed in that place, with the vestiges and signs that are visible there. Behold, He gave us all these places in which He performed these signs and wonders. He gave them to us as an eternal possession forever, so that they might bear witness for us to the truth of what He performed through signs and miracles, as the prophets—the first precursors of Him and His advent—had spoken concerning Him, namely that He would come and would perform these signs. Indeed, He came and performed them all, as these places bear witness for us, which remain until today and forever as a perpetual testimony to the fact that He is indeed the Christ who was prophesied and that there will be no other Christ who will come after Him. Vain is the hope of the Jews concerning His advent, for He will no longer come except in His second advent to condemn and judge His people and His own who did not believe in Him. But as for those who believed in Him among them and among all the nations, it is us, whom He has constituted as His new nation and to whom He has given as an inheritance His earth and His heaven always and forever, and this thanks to these places, in which He performed His signs and which He gave us as a perpetual memory and imperishable testimony. This place bears witness for itself thanks to the vestiges and signs, so that an opponent, an infidel among them, cannot say: “The one who came is not the one we were expecting, as they claim.” We showed them their lie and their perdition from their books, as the prophets spoke concerning Him in their books: that He would come among them, that He would perform such signs, and that they would do to Him what the prophets predicted and mentioned concerning them, namely that they would treat Him in such a way by humiliating, crucifying, and killing Him. Thus, Christ gave us the vestiges and the holy places of this world as an inheritance and a pledge of the goods He promised us concerning the Kingdom of Heaven and the benefits of the life to come. There remains no place, among those in which He manifested His divinity through signs and wonders—neither in a plain, nor on a mountain—, nor a place where God once spoke to one of His prophets, that He did not put into the hands of those who believed in Christ, as an inheritance from the twelve fathers, the Apostles, who saw Him, touched Him, who heard from Him what He prescribed to them, and who announced to us what He said to them. We believed in what they told us about Him and we received this from Him as an inheritance, namely these places that they gave us, in which He performed these wonders before their eyes, so that they might bear witness for us to the truth of what they said about Him. This place became a truthful testimony and an imperishable inheritance forever, until we join them on the day of Judgment and inherit with them the Kingdom of Heaven, which has neither corruption nor alteration, but which is eternal life, perpetual rest, and unfailing goods: what eye of man has not seen, what his ear has not heard, and what has not entered into his heart (1Co 2:9), what his tongue could not describe nor give an image of, what He created, fashioned, and prepared well before having founded the world and what is in it. He preordained all this, formed it, and made it in view of those who believed in Him thanks to the fathers, that is to say the pure Apostles, and those who received their rank and acted according to the commandments of Christ: all, one by one, according to their actions, as He preordained it in His wisdom, exalted be He! He has arranged for each man his rewards in accordance with his actions, according to the works of each. To Him always the glory and to us His mercy!

When He completed His growth and reached the age of thirty years, He attained the fullness of His human form and the completion of it concerning strength and reason, to fulfill all that pertains to the human condition and to take upon Himself all the sanctions of Adam, which were incumbent upon Him according to a just judgment. The reason for this is that Adam, when God created him, did not create him small nor lacking anything—neither in body, nor in strength, nor in reason—, but created him perfect in all things and thirty years old. That is why the Lord, until He was thirty years old, did not publicly manifest His works, in order to resemble the first Adam at the moment of his creation and his transgression, and to save the like by the like. In the instant that preceded the baptism of John, the Spirit of holiness rested upon Him in the form of a dove (Mt 3:16), as when He breathed upon the face of Adam (Gn 2:7) and completed him by (the gift of) the rational and speech-capable spirit, so that he might do what he wanted until he transgressed by his desire and deserved expulsion from Paradise, death, and punishment, that he went out onto the earth and did there what he wanted for himself until the moment of dying and going to Hell. Thus, when our Lord received baptism from John and the Spirit of holiness rested upon Him, He manifested Himself, by means of extraordinary and visible signs and miracles, namely that He was the Christ who was expected for the redemption of Adam. That is why they omitted from the account of the life of our Lord what He did from the beginning of His incarnation until He was thirty years old, so that He might resemble Adam until the moment He received baptism by John. These first signs were called the Gospel of the Infancy and there is no difference between them regarding divinity and power; but it was so that He might resemble the first Adam at the moment He created him. He manifested therefore by this means what He performed, for in the first Adam there was contradiction, death, and suffering, while in the second Adam there is redemption and eternal life thanks to His good news, His preaching, His life, His works, His sufferings, His death for us, His resurrection from the dead, and His redemption of Adam and his descendants.

Then, they uttered all kinds of divine hymns and celestial doxologies in honor of his divine substance, as a sign of praise and gratitude for what he did with them through the abundance of his benefits and the great number of his favors. Here then, he took them as his portion, when he made them his personal property (Dt 32:9), his beloved ones, and the heirs of what he had promised them concerning eternal life and imperishable beatitude, having made them immortal for eternity, them and those who would come after them, who would follow their faith and who would walk in their footsteps forever. Let us ask God to grant us this universal benefit of pure and orthodox faith, to confirm us in this faith without error until the last breath, and to help us in it toward knowledge and toward a way of acting that is in conformity with it, until the end of time. Thus, we will stand with them at his right hand and we will hear his tender voice say: "Come, you blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world, what the eye has not seen, what the ear has not heard, what has not entered into the heart of man, what God has prepared for his faithful, for his sincere friends, and for those who keep his prescriptions" (Mt 25:34; 1 Cor 2:9). To him be glory, majesty, adoration, and reverence for the eternity to come and for ages of ages. Amen.
