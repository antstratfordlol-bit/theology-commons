ἢ τὸ ἐκείνων ἀνέλωμεν λόγοις καὶ ἐπιχειρήμασι καὶ ἀγωγῆς, ἃ δὴ καὶ πρὸς τὰς λύσεις ἥρμοττεν ἂν τῶν ἀποριῶν, ἃς ὡς ἐξ ἡμῶν τίθησιν· ἀλλὰ πρὸς τὰς ὑποθέσεις δὲ ὧν ἄλλως ἥλπισε κατασκευάσειν ὁδῷ προβαίνων, τὴν δόξαν Λατίνων τὸ δοκοῦντα μετὰ τῆς ἀληθείας προθέμεθα πρὸς τὴν τῶν ἁγίων θεολογίαν ταύτας προβαλλόμενοι. EXTREMA SENTENTIA PATRIARCHÆ. Joseph, miseratione divina archiepiscopus Constantiopolis novæ Romæ et œcumenicus patriarcha. Quoniam ad finem vitæ meæ perveni, soluturus jam commune debitum, Dei gratia scribo et subscribo sententiam meam aperte universitati meorum filiorum. Omnia igitur, quæ sentit et quæ dogmatizat catholica et apostolica Ecclesia Domini nostri Jesu Christi senioris Romæ, ipse quoque sentio et iis me acquiescentem do ac dico. Profiteor quoque beatissimum Patrem patrum, et maximum pontificem, et vicarium Domini nostri Jesu Christi, antiquæ Romæ papam ad certam omnium fidem: necnon purgatorium animarum. In horum quippe confirmationem scriptum est, die mensis Junii nono, anno millesimo quadringentesimo tricesimo nono, indictione secunda.

CONFESSION OF FAITH OF JOSEPH, PATRIARCH OF CONSTANTINOPLE.
(Council of Florence, session 25.—Mansi, vol. XXXI, col. 1007.)
THE PATRIARCH’S FINAL DECLARATION. Joseph, by divine mercy archbishop of Constantinople, New Rome, and ecumenical patriarch. Since I have reached the end of my life and am now about to pay the common debt, by God’s grace I openly write and subscribe my declaration for all my children. Everything, therefore, that the catholic and apostolic Church of our Lord Jesus Christ in Old Rome believes and teaches, I too believe, and I declare and affirm my assent to it. I also acknowledge the pope of Old Rome as the most blessed Father of fathers, the supreme pontiff, and the vicar of our Lord Jesus Christ, so that all may be assured of my faith; I also acknowledge the purgatory of souls. In confirmation of these things, this was written on the ninth day of June, in the year one thousand four hundred and thirty-nine, in the second indiction.
THE PATRIARCH’S FINAL DECLARATION. Joseph, by the mercy of God archbishop of Constantinople, New Rome, and ecumenical patriarch. Since (1) I have reached the end of my life and wish now to discharge the common debt, by God’s grace I openly write and subscribe my belief for my spiritual children. Everything, therefore, that the catholic and apostolic Church of our Lord Jesus Christ in Old Rome believes and teaches, I too believe, and I pledge my assent to these things. Furthermore, I acknowledge the pope of Old Rome as the most blessed Father of fathers, the supreme high priest, and the vicar of our Lord Jesus Christ, for the assurance of all; I also acknowledge the purgatory of souls. For assurance concerning these things, this was signed on the ninth day of the month of June, in the year one thousand four hundred and thirty-nine, in the second indiction.
