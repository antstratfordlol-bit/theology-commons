From which I was brought to birth and knew the light,
Having obtained this as a myriad-fold gift of God,
And named as well Theodora,

Born of the race of the John Cantacuzenos,
Of the Komnenian line, named after the Angels,
He whom Ioannikios was called [as one] of the monastics,
Having adapted all [things] to the habit.
He was the famous grandson of that John,
Who traced his tribe back to the Angels,

The purple-born daughter, rightfully, of Alexios,
The Great Komnenian sovereign.

Was the Komnenian and Palaiologina,
An Irenic [Peace-making] one, Irene by name,
Being of the same blood as the king
Michael Palaiologos the Komnenos, save that
 That one was seen as a greater ornament in power,
Than the entire power of her kinsman.
I called this woman, from the monastic habit,
Eulogia, as one worthy of the blessing.
She was the grand-daughter of the despot Alexios
Of the Palaiologan line descending from the race,

Bloomed from the father, the genuine Alexios,
Wielding the scepter from the lineage of the Angels—

This man was the Komnenos Raoul John,
