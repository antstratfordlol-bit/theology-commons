Κατῆλθεν ἐπὶ πόκον Τοῦ Γεδεὼν ἡ δρόσος, Τὸν τόκον ζωγραφοῦσα Τῆς μόνης Θεοτόκου. Λέγεται Ἠσαΐας Ἠὼς τίκτει ἡ Παρθένος, Φυγοῦσα τὰς ὠδῖνας Καὶ πόνους τῶν μητέρων. Ῥαγέντος δὲ τοῦ ᾅδου Ἀνέστη ὡς ἐξ ὕπνου, Ἀνέβη τε πρὸς θρόνον Πατρὸς ἐν τοῖς ὑψίστοις. Σὺ οὖν, Ἰσραηλῖτα, Μνήσθητι ἀπιστήσης, Σπεῦσον τοῦ βαπτισθῆναι Χριστὸς γὰρ ἐπεφάνη.
Disce studiose cognitionem Trinitatis, edocere divinam Christi incarnationem. In primis temporibus primum verbum protulit Pater: Faciamus Adam ad meam imaginem. Vitæ perfectioris in Christo prævidens Enos, vocandum esse Salvatorem prænuntiavit. Quereus patriarchæ tuam fidem illuminet, quæ Triadem sanctissimam olim hospitio recepit. Cum Deo quondam luctatur in hominis specie magnus ille Jacob, futurum edocens. En tibi præcipit Moyses legislator ut audias legislatorem qui nasci debet. Descendit in vellus Gedeonis ros adumbrans partum solius Deiparæ. Dicat Isaias quomodo pariat virgo, dolores et labores maternitatis fugiens. Sine decore apparuit Dominus gloriæ : propter prævaricationes populi mei immolatus sum. Vincit patientia blasphematorum contumelias, terga flagellantur, facies conspuitur. Stupefacit omnem mentem Jeremias dicens : Agite, mittamus lignum in panem agni. Dicat magnus David divinus ille Pater, quomodo transfixisti manus et pedes Domini ? Quomodo dedisti in potum fel et acetum, et sortem misisti in tunicam Creatoris. Inferno autem dehiscente, surrexit velut e somno, ascendit ad thronum Patris in altissimis. Tu ergo, Israelita, memento incredulitatis, baptizari festina, Christus enim apparuit.

CRISTOPHORI PROTOASECRETIS
AD EOSDEM ISRAELITAS EXHORTATIO. CANTUS I.
—
Διδάχθητι τῶν θείων Λογίων τὰς ἐμφάσεις, Τῶν προφητῶν τὰς ῥήσεις Σωματικῶς μὴ νόει. Μὴ νόμιζε τὴν πόλιν Σιὼν οἰκοδομεῖσθαι Ἐκ λίθων θινομένων Ἐντίμων ἐν τῷ κόσμῳ. Μηδὲ τοῦτο ἐκδέχου Ὀφθαλμοῖς τοῖς σαρκίνοις Θεάσασθαι ἐν βίῳ. . . Ὁ λόγος τοῦ προφήτου Πεπλήρωται προδήλως, Καὶ Σιὼν ἐδομήθη Ἐκ λίθων ἀσυγκρίτων.
Σιὼν τὴν Ἐκκλησίαν Προεῖπον οἱ προφῆται Τὴν πόλιν τὴν μεγάλην Τοῦ πάντων βασιλέως. Ἐν ταύτῃ λίθος ἔντιμος Θεμέλιος ἐτέθη Χριστὸς, ὥσπερ προεῖπεν Τῶν προφητῶν ἡ δόξα. Ἐπὶ τῷ θεμελίῳ Χριστοῦ τοῦ μαργαρίτου. Τοὺς δώδεκα εὑρήσεις Ὡραιοτάτους λίθους. Λίθους δώδεκα νόει Τοὺς θείους ἀποστό/ους, Ἀστράπτοντας ἐν μέσῳ Σιὼν τῆς Ἐκκλησίας.
Τὸν σμάραγδόν μοι νόει Πέτρον τὸν κορυφαῖον. Πρωτεύοντα ἐν μέσῳ Χοροῦ τῶν ἀποστόλων. Τὸν ἄνθρακα λογίζου Παῦλον τὸν θεηγόρον, Ἡμέρας καὶ νυκτὸς γὰρ Τὰς ἀστραπὰς ἐκπέμπει. Ὑάκινθος δέ ἐστιν, Ὁ μέγας Ἰωάννης, Οὐράνια μυστήρια Βροντῶν τῇ Ἐκκλησίᾳ. Καὶ τοὺς λοιποὺς δὲ λίθους Τοὺς ἀποστόλους νόει, Ἀφ᾿ ὧν ᾠκοδομήθη Σιὼν ἡ θεία πόλις.
Docere divinorum oraculorum significationes. Prophetarum voces carnaliter ne intelligas. Ne arbitreris Sion urbem ædificari e lapidibus qui visi fuerint conspicui in hoc mundo. Ne illud excipias oculis carneis in vita contemplari... Verbum prophetæ impletum est manifeste, et Sion exstructa est e lapidibus dispersis. Sion Ecclesiam prædixerunt prophetæ, civitatem magnam universorum regis. In illa lapis eximius fundamentum positum est Christus, sicut prædixit prophetarum famosa vox.
In fundamento quod Christus margarita est duodecim invenies splendidissimos lapides. Lapides duodecim intellige divinos apostolos, fulgentes in medio Sion Ecclesiæ. Smaragdum reputa Petrum, coryphæum, primas tenentem in medio apostolorum chori. Anthracem intellige Paulum Deiloquum; nam die et nocte splendores emittit. Hyacinthus est magnus Joannes, cœlestia mysteria tonans in Ecclesia. Cæteros autem lapides intellige apostolos, a quibus ædificata est Sion civitas divina.
It is more pleasant to be silent than to speak of things that are not fitting. A One must receive as given by God things that come from God.
Follow the actions of all wise men,
Better not to live than to live ignominiously.
Grief brings death to men.
Imitate refined things; do not imitate an evil way of life.
Consider your parents to be gods.
Providing for guests, then you shall receive in kind.
Flee from swearing an oath, even if you have sworn justly.
Deem this life a public spectacle.
Live in the best way; you shall receive the heavenly inheritance.
From a wise man it is necessary to learn something wise.
You will complete your skill if you look to God.
Raise your hands perpetually toward God.
 Endeavor to love the glory of labors.
Hasten to obtain the place above, if it is right.
Stammer wisely rather than play the philosopher unreasonably.
For since these things provide what is good, they must be practiced.

EXHORTATION TO ISRAEL. CANTUS I.
(From Matranga, *Anecdota Graeca*, vol. II, p. 667.)
Cast aside blasphemy,
Cast aside unbelief,
Cast aside the veil
Of your soul, O Israelite.
Learn the outcomes
Of deep mysteries,
Of hidden things,
Which the prophets spoke.
Become an Israelite
In practice and in theory,
Raising up your mind
Like Jacob of old.
Abjure blasphemy, abjure unbelief, abjure the veil of the mind, O Israelite. Learn the outcomes of deep mysteries, of hidden things, which the prophets foretold. Become an Israelite in practice and theory, raising up your mind like Jacob of old.
EXHORTATION TO THE JEWS
Learn studiously the knowledge of the Trinity, and be instructed in the divine incarnation of Christ. A In the first times the Father first spoke the word: "Let us make Adam in my image." Enos, foreseeing a more perfect life in Christ, proclaimed that the Saviour was to be called upon. May the oak of the patriarch illuminate your faith, which once received the most holy Trinity in hospitality. B Great Jacob once wrestled with God in the form of a man, teaching what was to come. Behold, Moses the lawgiver commands you to hear the Lawgiver who is to be born. The dew descended upon the fleece of Gideon, prefiguring the birth of the only Theotokos. Let Isaiah say how the Virgin gives birth, fleeing the pains and labors of motherhood. The Lord of glory appeared without comeliness: "For the transgressions of my people I was sacrificed." His patience overcomes the insults of the blasphemers; His back is scourged, His face is spit upon. Jeremiah astonishes every mind, saying: "Come, let us cast wood into the bread of the lamb." Let the great David, the ancestor of God, declare: How did you pierce the hands and feet of the Lord? How did you give gall and vinegar for drink, and cast lots for the tunic of the Creator? But with hell gaping open, He rose as if from sleep, and ascended to the throne of the Father in the highest.
You, therefore, Israelite, remember your unbelief, make haste to be baptized, for Christ has appeared. The sign given of old is now fulfilled; for the ruler has ceased to reign over my people to the end. The prophet Daniel revealed the time, saying there would be seventy weeks. I am enlightened, brother, I receive baptism, having learned what I did not know of the mysteries of the Law. O choir of the prophets, receive me as a brother; for having obeyed you, I am clothed in grace. I offer you a purified mind, my Saviour. Basil the emperor cries out now to the Saviour: As you once accepted the sacrifice of Abraham, so accept also my gift from the newly-chosen flock. For I have consecrated to you my children with my wife, the kingdom itself, and my whole life.
CHRISTOPHORUS THE PROTOASECRETIS: EXHORTATION TO THE SAME ISRAELITES. CANTO I.
Learn the meanings of the divine oracles,
Do not understand the utterances
Of the prophets in a carnal sense.
Do not think that the city
Of Sion is built
Of stones that are seen
And honored in this world.
Nor expect this
To be beheld by carnal eyes
In this life. . .
The word of the prophet
Has been clearly fulfilled,
And Sion has been built
Of incomparable stones.
Sion, the Church,
The prophets foretold
As the great city
Of the King of all.
In it, a precious stone
Was laid as a foundation,
Christ, as foretold
By the glory of the prophets.
Upon the foundation
Of Christ the pearl,
You shall find the twelve
Most beautiful stones.
Understand the twelve stones
To be the divine apostles,
Shining in the midst
Of Sion the Church.
Consider the emerald for me
To be Peter the chief,
Holding the first place in the midst
Of the chorus of the apostles.
Regard as the carbuncle
Paul the god-inspired,
For day and night
He sends forth lightnings.
And the hyacinth
Is the great John,
Thundering heavenly mysteries
To the Church.
And understand the remaining stones
To be the apostles,
By whom was built
Sion the divine city.
