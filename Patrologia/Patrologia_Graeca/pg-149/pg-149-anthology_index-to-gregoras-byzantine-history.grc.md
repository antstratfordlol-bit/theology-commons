ADDENDA TO THE WORKS OF NICEPHORUS GREGORAS
OF THE MOST WISE AND LEARNED LORD NICEPHORUS GREGORAS: AN ADDRESS TO THE EMPEROR
How pleasant and radiant is your return to us from your victories, most divine Emperor, and today more than before, and you more than all the emperors who preceded you, toward those who were previously your subjects. For what those others had the misfortune to lose, and what they had not the strength to recover, this you have taken back with much greater power and have restored to the broken chain of the Roman hegemony.
