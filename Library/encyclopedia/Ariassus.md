# Ariassus {#cathen.ariassus}

A titular see of Pamphylia in Asia Minor, whose episcopal list (381-458) is given in Gams (p. 450).

Lequien, Oriens Christ. (1740), I, 162; Smith, Dict. of Greek and Roman Geogr., I, 211.
