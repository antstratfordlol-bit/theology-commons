# Arcadiopolis {#cathen.arcadiopolis}

A titular see of Asia Minor. Its episcopal list (431-879) is given in Gams (p. 444); there is also in Gams (p. 427) the episcopal list of another see of the same name (431-879).

LEQUIEN, *Oriens Christ. (1740), I, 1711-12.*
