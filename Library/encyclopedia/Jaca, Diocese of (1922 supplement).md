# Jaca, Diocese of {#cathen.jaca-diocese-of-supp}

**Jaca** (or Xaca), Diocese of (Jaccensis; cf. [[Jaca, Diocese of|C. E., VIII—260a]]), in the province of Huesca, Spain, suffragan of Saragossa. Rt. Rev. Emmanuel de Castro y Alonso, appointed to this see 28 October, 1913, was transferred to Segovia, 9 July, 1920. He was succeeded by Rt. Rev. Francisco Frutos y Valiente, canon and chaplain major of the king at the cathedral of Toledo, appointed 21 September, 1920. The diocese embraces a territory of about 1081 sq. miles and by 1920 statistics comprises a Catholic population of 71,659, 8 archpresbyteries, 251 parishes, 100 filial parishes, 1206 priests, 137 chapels and 9 convents with 82 nuns and 60 Sisters.
