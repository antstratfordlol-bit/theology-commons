# John Allen (II) {#cathen.john-allen-ii}

**Allen,** John, priest and martyr. He was executed at Tyburn in the beginning of the year 1538, because he refused to subscribe to the ecclesiastical supremacy of Henry VIII.

Stow, *Chronicles;* *Cath. Magazine* (1832.; Gillow.

John J. a' Becket.
