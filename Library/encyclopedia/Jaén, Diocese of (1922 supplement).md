# Jaén, Diocese of {#cathen.jaen-diocese-of-supp}

**Jaén,** Diocese of (Giennensis; cf. [[Jaén, Diocese of|C. E., VIII—267b]]), in the province of Andalusia, Spain, suffragan of Granada. Rt. Rev. John Emmanuel Sanz y Saravia, appointed to this see 29 April, 1909, died 22 June, 1919, and was succeeded by Rt. Rev. Emmanuel Basulto y Jiménez, born in Adanero, Spain, 1867, studied at the Seminary of Avila, served as vice-rector and canon of Léon, canon of the Cathedral of Madrid in 1905, appointed Bishop of Lugo, 4 September, 1909, transferred 18 December, 1919. This diocese embraces a territory of some 873 sq. miles and a Catholic population of 437,783, with only 190 Protestants. By 1920 statistics it comprises 12 archpresbyteries, 159 parishes, 8 filial parishes, 475 priests, 16 chapels, and 33 convents with 67 religious and 659 Sisters.
