# Amorios {#cathen.amorios}

**Amorios** (also Amorium), a titular see of Phrygia in Asia Minor, now known as Hergan Kaleh. It was a see as early as 431.

Lequien, *Oriens Christ.* (1740), I, 853 sq.; Gams, I, 447.
