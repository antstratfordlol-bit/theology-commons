# Pope St. Anastasius I {#cathen.pope-st-anastasius-i}

**Anastasius I,** Saint, pope, a pontiff who is remembered chiefly for his condemnation of Origenism. A Roman by birth, he became pope in 399. and died within a little less than four years. Among his friends were Augustine, and Jerome, and Paulinus. Jerome speaks of him as a man of great holiness who was rich in his poverty. It was during the time of the barbarian invasions.

*Acta SS.*, III, September; Butler, *Lives of the Saints*, 27 September.

T. J. Campbell.
