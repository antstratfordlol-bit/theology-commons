# Prefecture Apostolic of Araucania {#cathen.prefecture-apostolic-of-araucania}

Located in Chile, established by Leo XIII in 1901, and confided to the Capuchins, It has twenty-eight missionaries.

BATTANDIER, *Ann. Pont. Cath.* (Paris, 1906), 343.
