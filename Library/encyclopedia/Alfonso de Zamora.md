# Alfonso de Zamora {#cathen.alfonso-de-zamora}

**Alfonso de Zamora,** a converted Spanish Rabbi, baptized 1506; d. 1531. He revised the Hebrew text for Ximenes's Polyglot Bible, translated the Chaldee paraphrase in it, and added the sixth volume. He published also a work called "Introductiones Hebraicæ" (Alcala, 1526).

A. J. Maas.
