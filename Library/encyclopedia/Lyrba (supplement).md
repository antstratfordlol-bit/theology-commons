# Lyrba (supplement) {#cathen.lyrba-supplement}

**Lyrba**, titular see in Pamphylia Prima; the ruins are southeast of Kiesme, vilayet of Koniah; there have been found some inscriptions, tombs, and the remains of a Byzantine church.

Radet in *Revue des etudes anciennes*, XII (Bordeaux, 1910), 365-72.

S. Petrides.
