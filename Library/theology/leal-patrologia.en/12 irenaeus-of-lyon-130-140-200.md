### IRENAEUS OF LYON (130/140-200) {#leal-patrologia.introduction-to-patristi.irenaeus-of-lyon-130-140}
A native of Asia Minor and born about 130-140, he became bishop of Lyon after the death of his predecessor Potinus, during the pontificate of Victor (189-198). He wrote a five-book treatise *Against Heresies* (which has come down to us in the Latin translation) to refute the various Gnostic schools, and a kind of catechism, *Demonstration of Apostolic Preaching*. With the first of the two works, Irenaeus consecrates himself as the first Christian writer to make a complete and systematic treatment of the faith.

Gnosticism is a religious movement of redemption of late antiquity in which the possibility of a negative interpretation of the world and existence is taken up in particular and unmistakable ways, which has crystallised into a consistent conception of rejection of the world and which finds its characteristic expression in the coinage of terms, figurative language, and a highly contrived mythology. Among the most recurrent characteristics of Gnosticism we can enumerate the following: cosmological dualism with an irreducible opposition between matter and spirit, body and soul; recourse to divine genealogies, where aeons proceed by pairs and generate other aeons; a trichotomous anthropology, in which men are divided into three categories: material, animal, and spiritual, and only the latter possess the divine spark in the soul; other reductionisms leading to a halved theology, where knowledge is preferred almost exclusively over morality, allegorical exegesis over literal exegesis, a salvation is defended only for some (thus there is no freedom), the spirit is corroborated up to the denial of the reality of the body of Christ (docetism), and only a few books of the Bible are chosen, even not completely. Its main exponents were Carpocrates, Basilides, Menander, Cerdon, and Valentinus. A separate consideration deserves Marcion, because of the characteristics of his movement, which differ substantially from the other exponents.

Mention should also be made here of apocryphal literature. The term "apocryphal" (secret) initially alluded to a revelation transmitted only to some (not to all Christians) and which could be communicated only to a few chosen persons. Then, "apocryphal" took on the meaning of "false," "spurious," and extended to all texts that claimed to be revealed, even though they did not have the characteristics of revelation. Sometimes these writings are also called pseudo-epigraphs. Although there are apocrypha of the Old Testament, some retouched by Christians, the greatest number of apocrypha concern the New Testament (gospels, acts, letters, apocryphal apocalypses) and a good number of them contain gnostic doctrines. Irenaeus refuted these writings, which were transmitters of the doctrines he fought.

Irenaeus' exegesis is characterised by a Christological (*typological*) reading of the Old Testament, in which the doctrine of recapitulation occupies a predominant place, and by an essentially literal reading of the New Testament. Irenaeus' two main concerns are the continuity of the two Testaments and the progressiveness of Revelation; and this is accomplished through typology, which he calls allegory, as opposed to that of the Gnostics. The Christological reading of the Old Testament is developed on two levels: the prophecies have an immediate explanation; the facts impose a historical and an allegorical meaning. Thus, the writings of Irenaeus have a very suggestive richness. He is the first to consider the New Testament on the same level as the Old, that is, as a Bible. The most salient elements of his exegesis are: the use of the *regula veritatis* as the norm of biblical interpretation; tradition as the norm of exegesis; the consonance of the whole Bible. Two very useful rules can be deduced from his pages: the most obscure passages are to be interpreted starting from the clearest ones; one should not look for useless questions.

We present two texts of Irenaeus. The first one, certainly very obscure, is useful to understand the proliferation of apocryphal writings, as well as the essence of Gnosticism, especially the exegesis, with its arbitrary allegory that starts from the New Testament (just the opposite of what Catholic exegesis does) and serves only to justify the Gnostic theories. The second, on the creation of man, shows the typological treatment of Scripture and hints at the recapitulation doctrine.

#### Against Heresies I, 1.20-22
*The system of Ptolemy*

*Origen’s Pleroma and structure of the Pleroma*

1,1. They maintain, then, that in the invisible and ineffable heights above there exists a certain perfect, pre-existent [Æon](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm), whom they call Proarche, Propator, and Bythus, and describe as being invisible and incomprehensible. Eternal and unbegotten, he remained throughout innumerable cycles of ages in profound serenity and quiescence. There [existed](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm) along with him Ennœa, whom they also call Charis and Sige. At last this Bythus determined to send forth from himself the beginning of all things, and deposited this production (which he had resolved to bring forth) in his contemporary Sige, even as seed is deposited in the womb. She then, having received this seed, and becoming pregnant, gave birth to Nous, who was both similar and equal to him who had produced him, and was alone capable of comprehending his father's greatness. This Nous they call also Monogenes, and Father, and the Beginning of all Things. Along with him was also produced Aletheia; and these four constituted the first and first-begotten [Pythagorean](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12587b.htm) Tetrad, which they also denominate the root of all things. For there are first Bythus and Sige, and then Nous and Aletheia.

And Monogenes, perceiving for what purpose he had been produced, also himself sent forth [Logos](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm) and Zoe, being the father of all those who were to come after him, and the beginning and fashioning of the entire Pleroma. By the conjunction of [Logos](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm) and Zoe were brought forth Anthropos and Ecclesia; and thus was formed the first-begotten Ogdoad, the root and substance of all things, called among them by four names, viz., Bythus, and Nous, and [Logos](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm), and Anthropos. For each of these is masculo-feminine, as follows: Propator was united by a conjunction with his Ennœa; then Monogenes, that is Nous, with Aletheia; [Logos](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm) with Zoe, and Anthropos with Ecclesia.

1,2. These [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm) having been produced for the [glory](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm) of the [Father](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), and wishing, by their own efforts, to effect this object, sent forth emanations by means of conjunction. [Logos](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm) and Zoe, after producing Anthropos and Ecclesia, sent forth other ten [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm), whose names are the following: Bythius and Mixis, Ageratos and Henosis, Autophyes and Hedone, Acinetos and Syncrasis, Monogenes and Macaria. These are the ten [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm) whom they declare to have been produced by [Logos](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm) and Zoe. They then add that Anthropos himself, along with Ecclesia, produced twelve [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm), to whom they give the following names: Paracletus and Pistis, Patricos and Elpis, Metricos and Agape, Ainos and Synesis, Ecclesiasticus and Macariotes, Theletos and Sophia.

*Biblical proof*

1,3. Such are the thirty [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm) in the erroneous system of these men; and they are described as being wrapped up, so to speak, in silence, and [known](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) to none [except these professing teachers]. Moreover, they declare that this invisible and spiritual Pleroma of theirs is tripartite, being divided into an Ogdoad, a Decad, and a Duodecad. And for this reason they affirm it was that the Savior— for they do not please to call Him Lord— did no work in public during the space of thirty years, [Luke 3:23](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk003.htm#verse23) thus setting forth the [mystery](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm) of these [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm). They maintain also, that these thirty [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm) are most plainly indicated in the [parable](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11460a.htm) [Matthew 20:1-16](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat020.htm#verse1) of the labourers sent into the vineyard. For some are sent about the first hour, others about the [third hour](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14514c.htm), others about the sixth hour, others about the ninth hour, and others about the eleventh hour. Now, if we add up the numbers of the hours here mentioned, the sum total will be thirty: for one, three, six, nine, and eleven, when added together, form thirty. And by the hours, they hold that the [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm) were pointed out; while they maintain that these are great, and wonderful, and hitherto unspeakable [mysteries](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm) which it is their special function to develop; and so they proceed when they find anything in the multitude of things contained in the [Scriptures](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html) which they can adopt and accommodate to their baseless speculations.

*Recourse to the Apocryphal writings*

20,1. Besides the above [misrepresentations], they adduce an unspeakable number of [apocryphal](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01601a.htm) and spurious writings, which they themselves have forged, to bewilder the minds of foolish men, and of such as are [ignorant](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm) of the [Scriptures](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html) of [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm). Among other things, they bring forward that false and [wicked](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm) story which relates that our Lord, when He was a boy learning His letters, on the teacher saying to Him, as is usual, Pronounce Alpha, replied [as He was bid], Alpha. But when, again, the teacher bade Him say, Beta, the Lord replied, First tell me what Alpha is, and then I will tell you what Beta is. This they expound as meaning that He alone [knew](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) the Unknown, which He revealed under its type Alpha.

*False interpretation of some evangelical texts*

20,2. Some passages, also, which occur in the [Gospels](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm), receive from them a colouring of the same kind, such as the answer which He gave His mother when He was twelve years of age: Did you not know that I must be about My Father's business? [Luke 2:49](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk002.htm#verse49) Thus, they say, He announced to them the Father of whom they were [ignorant](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm). On this account, also, He sent forth the [disciples](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm) to the twelve tribes, that they might proclaim to them the unknown God. And to the person who said to Him, Good Master, [Mark 10:17](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mar010.htm#verse17) He confessed that God who is [truly](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm) good, saying, Why do you call Me good: there is One who is [good](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm), the Father in the heavens; [Luke 18:18](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk018.htm#verse18) and they assert that in this passage the [Æons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm) receive the name of heavens. Moreover, by His not replying to those who said to Him, By what power do You this? [Matthew 21:23](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat021.htm#verse23) but by a question on His own side, put them to utter confusion; by His thus not replying, according to their interpretation, He showed the unutterable nature of the Father. Moreover, when He said, I have often desired to hear one of these words, and I had no one who could utter it, they maintain, that by this expression one He set forth the one [true](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm) God whom they [knew](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) not. Further, when, as He drew near to Jerusalem, He wept over it and said, If you had [known](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm), even you, in this your day, the things that belong unto your peace, but they are hidden from you, by this word hidden He showed the abstruse nature of Bythus. And again, when He said, Come unto Me all you that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest, and learn of Me, [Matthew 11:28](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat011.htm#verse28) He announced the Father of [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm). For what they [knew](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) not, these men say that He promised to teach them.

20,3. But they adduce the following passage as the highest testimony, and, as it were, the very crown of their system:— I thank You, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because You have hid these things from the wise and [prudent](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12517b.htm), and have revealed them to babes. Even so, my Father; for so it seemed [good](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm) in Your sight. All things have been delivered to Me by My Father; and no one [knows](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) the Father but the [Son](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm), or the Son but the [Father](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), and he to whom the Son will reveal Him. [Matthew 11:25-27](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat011.htm#verse25) In these words they affirm that He clearly showed that the Father of [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm), conjured into [existence](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm) by them, was [known](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) to no one before His advent. And they desire to construe the passage as if teaching that the Maker and Framer [of the world] was always [known](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) by all, while the Lord spoke these words concerning the Father unknown to all, whom they now proclaim.

*Redemption according to the heretics: errors and contradictions*

21,1. It happens that their tradition respecting *redemption* is invisible and incomprehensible, as being the mother of things which are incomprehensible and invisible; and on this account, since it is fluctuating, it is impossible simply and all at once to make [known](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) its nature, for every one of them hands it down just as his own inclination prompts. Thus there are as many schemes of redemption as there are teachers of these mystical opinions. And when we come to refute them, we shall show in its fitting-place, that this class of men have been instigated by [Satan](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm) to a denial of that [baptism](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) which is regeneration to [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), and thus to a renunciation of the whole [[Christian](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm)] [faith](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm).

21,2. They maintain that those who have attained to perfect [knowledge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) must of necessity be regenerated into that power which is above all. For it is otherwise impossible to find admittance within the Pleroma, since this [regeneration] it is which leads them down into the depths of Bythus. For the [baptism](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) instituted by the visible Jesus was for the remission of [sins](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm), but the redemption brought in by that Christ who descended upon Him, was for perfection; and they allege that the former is animal, but the latter spiritual. And the [baptism](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) of John was proclaimed with a view to repentance, but the redemption by Jesus was brought in for the sake of perfection. And to this He refers when He says, And I have another [baptism](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) to be [baptised](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) with, and I hasten eagerly towards it. Moreover, they affirm that the Lord added this redemption to the sons of Zebedee, when their mother asked that they might sit, the one on His right hand, and the other on His left, in His kingdom, saying, Can you be [baptised](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) with the [baptism](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) which I shall be [baptised](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) with? [Mark 10:38](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mar010.htm#verse38) [Paul](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm), too, they declare, has often set forth, in express terms, the redemption which is in Christ Jesus; and this was the same which is handed down by them in so varied and discordant forms.

21,3. For some of them prepare a nuptial couch, and perform a sort of mystic rite (pronouncing certain expressions) with those who are being initiated, and affirm that it is a spiritual marriage which is celebrated by them, after the likeness of the conjunctions above. Others, again, lead them to a place where water is, and [baptise](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm) them, with the utterance of these words, Into the name of the unknown Father of the [universe](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm)— into [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm), the mother of all things — into Him who descended on Jesus — into union, and redemption, and communion with the powers.

Others still repeat certain Hebrew words, in order the more thoroughly to bewilder those who are being initiated, as follows: Basema, Chamosse, Baœnaora, Mistadia, Ruada, Kousta, Babaphor, Kalachthei. The interpretation of these terms runs thus: I invoke that which is above every power of the [Father](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), which is called light, and good Spirit, and life, because You have reigned in the body. Others, again, set forth the redemption thus: The name which is hidden from every deity, and dominion, and [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm) which [Jesus of Nazareth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm) was clothed with in the lives of the light of Christ — of [Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm), who lives by the [Holy Ghost](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm), for the angelic redemption. The name of restitution stands thus: Messia, Uphareg, Namempsœman, Chaldœaur, Mosomedœa, Acphranœ, Psaua, Jesus Nazaria. The interpretation of these words is as follows: I do not divide the Spirit of [Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm), neither the heart nor the supercelestial power which is merciful; may I enjoy Your name, O Savior of [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm)! Such are words of the initiators; but he who is initiated, replies, I am established, and I am redeemed; I redeem my [soul](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm) from this age (world), and from all things connected with it in the name of Iao, who redeemed his own [soul](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm) into redemption in Christ who lives. Then the bystanders add these words, Peace be to all on whom this name rests. After this they anoint the initiated person with balsam; for they assert that this ointment is a type of that sweet odour which is above all things.

21,4. But there are some of them who assert that it is superfluous to bring [persons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm) to the water, but mixing oil and water together, they place this mixture on the heads of those who are to be initiated, with the use of some such expressions as we have already mentioned. And this they maintain to be the redemption. They, too, are accustomed to anoint with balsam. Others, however, reject all these practices, and maintain that the [mystery](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm) of the unspeakable and invisible power ought not to be performed by visible and corruptible creatures, nor should that of those [beings] who are inconceivable, and incorporeal, and beyond the reach of sense, [be performed] by such as are the objects of sense, and possessed of a body. These hold that the [knowledge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) of the unspeakable Greatness is itself perfect redemption. For since both defect and passion flowed from [ignorance](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm), the whole substance of what was thus formed is destroyed by [knowledge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm); and therefore [knowledge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) is the redemption of the inner man. This, however, is not of a corporeal nature, for the body is corruptible; nor is it animal, since the animal [soul](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm) is the fruit of a defect, and is, as it were, the abode of the spirit. The redemption must therefore be of a spiritual nature; for they affirm that the inner and spiritual man is redeemed by means of [knowledge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm), and that they, having acquired the [knowledge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) of all things, stand thenceforth in need of nothing else. This, then, is the [true](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm) redemption.

21,5. Others still there are who continue to redeem [persons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm) even up to the moment of death, by placing on their heads oil and water, or the pre-mentioned ointment with water, using at the same time the above-named invocations, that the [persons](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm) referred to may become incapable of being seized or seen by the principalities and powers, and that their inner man may ascend on high in an invisible manner, as if their body were left among [created](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04470a.htm) things in this world, while their [soul](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm) is sent forward to the [Demiurge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04707b.htm). And they instruct them, on their reaching the principalities and powers, to make use of these words: I am a son from the Father — the Father who had a pre-[existence](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm), and a son in Him who is pre-existent. I have come to behold all things, both those which belong to myself and others, although, strictly speaking, they do not belong to others, but to Achamoth, who is female in nature, and made these things for herself. For I derive being from Him who is pre-existent, and I come again to my own place whence I went forth. And they affirm that, by saying these things, he escapes from the powers. He then advances to the companions of the [Demiurge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04707b.htm), and thus addresses them:— I am a vessel more precious than the female who formed you. If your mother is [ignorant](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm) of her own descent, I [know](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) myself, and am aware whence I am, and I call upon the incorruptible Sophia, who is in the [Father](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), and is the mother of your mother, who has no father, nor any male consort; but a female springing from a female formed you, while [ignorant](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm) of her own mother, and imagining that she alone [existed](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm); but I call upon her mother. And they declare, that when the companions of the [Demiurge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04707b.htm) hear these words, they are greatly agitated, and upbraid their origin and the race of their mother. But he goes into his own place, having thrown [off] his chain, that is, his animal nature. These, then, are the particulars which have reached us respecting redemption. But since they differ so widely among themselves both as respects doctrine and tradition, and since those of them who are recognized as being most modern make it their effort daily to invent some new opinion, and to bring out what no one ever before thought of, it is a difficult matter to describe all their opinions.

*The rule of Truth*

22,1. The rule of [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm) which we hold, is, that there is one God Almighty, who made all things by His Word, and fashioned and formed, out of that which had no [existence](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm), all things which exist. Thus says the [Scripture](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html), to that effect By the Word of the Lord were the heavens established, and all the might of them, by the spirit of His mouth. And again, All things were made by Him, and without Him was nothing made. [John 1:3](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse3) There is no exception or deduction stated; but the Father made all things by Him, whether visible or invisible, objects of sense or of intelligence, temporal, on account of a certain character given them, or [eternal](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm); and these [eternal](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm) things He did not make by [angels](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm), or by any powers separated from His Ennœa. For God needs none of all these things, but is He who, by His Word and Spirit, makes, and disposes, and governs all things, and commands all things into [existence](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm) — He who formed the world (for the world is of all) — He who fashioned man — He [who] is the [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm) of [Abraham](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm), and the [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm) of Isaac, and the [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm) of Jacob, above whom there is no other [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), nor initial principle, nor power, nor pleroma — He is the Father of [our Lord Jesus Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm), as we shall prove. Holding, therefore, this rule, we shall easily show, notwithstanding the great variety and multitude of their opinions, that these men have deviated from the [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm); for almost all the different [sects](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13674a.htm) of [heretics](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm) admit that there is [one God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10499a.htm); but then, by their pernicious doctrines, they change [this [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm) into [error](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm)], even as the [Gentiles](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm) do through [idolatry](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm) — thus proving themselves ungrateful to Him that created them. Moreover, they despise the workmanship of [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), speaking against their own [salvation](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm), becoming their own bitterest accusers, and being false witnesses [against themselves]. Yet, reluctant as they may be, these men shall one day rise again in the flesh, to confess the power of Him who raises them from the dead; but they shall not be numbered among the righteous on account of their unbelief.

22,2. Since, therefore, it is a complex and multiform task to detect and convict all the [heretics](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm), and since our design is to reply to them all according to their special characters, we have judged it necessary, first of all, to give an account of their source and root, in order that, by getting a [knowledge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) of their most exalted Bythus, you may understand the [nature](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm) of the tree which has produced such fruits.

#### Against Heresies IV, 20,1; IV, 25,2-3; IV, 26,1-2; V, 15,4-16,2
*The creation of man*

IV. 20,1. As regards His greatness, therefore, it is not possible to [know](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), for it is impossible that the Father can be measured; but as regards His [love](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm) (for this it is which leads us to God by His Word), when we [obey](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm) Him, we do always learn that there is so great a [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), and that it is He who by Himself has established, and selected, and adorned, and contains all things; and among the all things, both ourselves and this our world. We also then were made, along with those things which are contained by Him. And this is He of whom the [Scripture](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html) says, And God formed man, taking clay of the earth, and breathed into his face the breath of life. [Genesis 2:7](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen002.htm#verse7) It was not [angels](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm), therefore, who made us, nor who formed us, nor had [angels](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm) power to make an image of [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), nor anyone else, except the Word of the Lord, nor any Power remotely distant from the Father of all things. For God did not stand in need of these [beings], in order to the accomplishing of what He had Himself determined with Himself beforehand should be done, as if He did not possess His own hands. For with Him were always present the Word and Wisdom, the Son and the [Spirit](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm), by whom and in whom, freely and spontaneously, He made all things, to whom also He speaks, saying, Let *Us* make man after Our image and likeness; [Genesis 1:26](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse26) He taking from Himself the substance of the creatures [formed], and the pattern of things made, and the type of all the adornments in the world.

*The scarlet token*

25,2. This fact is indeed set forth by many other [occurrences], but typically by [the history of] Thamar, Judah's daughter-in-law. [Genesis 38:28](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen038.htm#verse28), etc. For when she had conceived twins, one of them put forth his hand first; and as the midwife supposed that he was the [first-born](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm), she bound a scarlet token on his hand. But after this had been done, and he had drawn back his hand, his brother Phares came forth the first; then, after him, Zara, upon whom was the scarlet line, [was born] the second: the [Scripture](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html) clearly pointing out that people which possessed the scarlet sign, that is, [faith](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm) in a state of [circumcision](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm), which was shown beforehand, indeed, in the patriarchs first; but after that withdrawn, that his brother might be born; and also, in like manner, him who was the elder, as being born in the second place, [him] who was distinguished by the scarlet token which was [fastened] on him, that is, the passion of the Just One, which was prefigured from the beginning in Abel, and described by the [prophets](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm), but perfected in the last times in the [Son of God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm).

*Three types of foreshadowing*

25,3. For it was requisite that certain facts should be announced beforehand by the fathers in a paternal manner, and others prefigured by the [prophets](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm) in a legal one, but others, described after the form of [Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm), by those who have received the adoption; while in one God are all things shown forth. For although [Abraham](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm) was one, he did in himself prefigure the two covenants, in which some indeed have sown, while others have reaped; for it is said, In this is the saying [true](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm), that it is one 'people' who sows, but another who shall reap; [John 4:37](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh004.htm#verse37) but it is one God who bestows things suitable upon both — seed to the sower, but bread for the reaper to eat. Just as it is one that plants, and another who waters, but one God who gives the increase. [1 Corinthians 3:7](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co003.htm#verse7) For the patriarchs and [prophets](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm) sowed the word [concerning] Christ, but the [Church](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm) reaped, that is, received the fruit. For this reason, too, do these very men (the [prophets](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm)) also [pray](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm) to have a dwelling-place in it, as Jeremiah says, Who will give me in the [desert](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04749a.htm) the last dwelling-place? in order that both the sower and the reaper may [rejoice](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm) together in the kingdom of [Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm), who is present with all those who were from the beginning approved by [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), who granted them His Word to be present with them.

26,1. If any one, therefore, reads the [Scriptures](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html) with attention, he will find in them an account of [Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm), and a foreshadowing of the new calling (*vocationis*). For Christ is the treasure which was hid in the field, [Matthew 13:44](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat013.htm#verse44) that is, in this world (for the field is the world [Matthew 13:38](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat013.htm#verse38)); but the treasure hid in the [Scriptures](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html) is Christ, since He was pointed out by means of types and [parables](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11460a.htm). Hence His [human](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm) [nature](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm) could not be understood, prior to the consummation of those things which had been predicted, that is, the advent of [Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm). And therefore it was said to Daniel the [prophet](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm): Shut up the words, and seal the book even to the time of consummation, until many learn, and [knowledge](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) be completed. For at that time, when the dispersion shall be accomplished, they shall [know](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm) all these things. [Daniel 12:4, 7](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/dan012.htm#verse4) But Jeremiah also says, In the last days they shall understand these things. [Jeremiah 23:20](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/jer023.htm#verse20) For every [prophecy](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm), before its fulfilment, is to men [full of] enigmas and ambiguities. But when the time has arrived, and the prediction has come to pass, then the prophecies have a clear and certain exposition. And for this reason, indeed, when at this present time the law is read to the [Jews](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm), it is like a fable; for they do not possess the explanation of all things pertaining to the advent of the [Son of God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm), which took place in [human](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm) [nature](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm); but when it is read by the [Christians](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm), it is a treasure, hid indeed in a field, but brought to light by the cross of [Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm), and explained, both enriching the understanding of [men](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm), and showing forth the wisdom of [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm) and declaring His dispensations with regard to [man](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm), and forming the kingdom of Christ beforehand, and preaching by anticipation the inheritance of the [holy](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm) Jerusalem, and proclaiming beforehand that the man who loves God shall arrive at such excellency as even to see [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), and hear His word, and from the hearing of His discourse be [glorified](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm) to such an extent, that others cannot behold the [glory](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm) of his countenance, as was said by Daniel: Those who do understand, shall shine as the brightness of the firmament, and many of the righteous as the stars for ever and ever. [Daniel 12:3](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/dan012.htm#verse3) Thus, then, I have shown it to be, if any one read the [Scriptures](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html). For thus it was that the Lord discoursed with the [disciples](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm) after His [resurrection](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm) from the dead, proving to them from the [Scriptures](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html) themselves that [Christ](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10212c.htm) must suffer, and enter into His [glory](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm), and that remission of [sins](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm) should be preached in His name throughout all the world. [Luke 24:26, 47](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk024.htm#26) And the [disciple](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm) will be [perfected](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11665b.htm), and [rendered] like the householder, who brings forth from his treasure things new and old. [Matthew 13:52](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat013.htm#52)

*How to discover the truth*

26,2. Wherefore it is incumbent to [obey](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm) the [presbyters](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm) who are in the [Church](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm) — those who, as I have shown, possess the succession from the [apostles](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm); those who, together with the succession of the [episcopate](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm), have received the certain gift of [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm), according to the good pleasure of the Father. But [it is also incumbent] to hold in suspicion others who depart from the primitive succession, and assemble themselves together in any place whatsoever, [looking upon them] either as [heretics](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm) of perverse minds, or as schismatics puffed up and self-pleasing, or again as hypocrites, acting thus for the sake of lucre and vainglory. For all these have fallen from the [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm). And the [heretics](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm), indeed, who bring strange fire to the altar of God — namely, [strange doctrines](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm)— shall be burned up by the fire from heaven, as were Nadab and Abiud. [Leviticus 10:1-2](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/lev010.htm#verse1) But such as rise up in opposition to the [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm), and exhort others against the [Church of God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm), [shall] remain among those in [hell](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07207a.htm) (*apud inferos*), being swallowed up by an earthquake, even as those who were with Chore, Dathan, and Abiron. [Numbers 16:33](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/num016.htm#verse33) But those who cleave asunder, and separate the unity of the [Church](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm), [shall] receive from God the same punishment as [Jeroboam](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08340a.htm) did. [1 Kings 14:10](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ki014.htm#verse10)

V. 15,4. All the followers of [Valentinus](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm), therefore, lose their case, when they say that man was not fashioned out of this earth, but from a fluid and diffused substance. For, from the earth out of which the Lord formed eyes for that man, from the same earth it is evident that man was also fashioned at the beginning. For it was incompatible that the eyes should indeed be formed from one source and the rest of the body from another; as neither would it be compatible that one [being] fashioned the body, and another the eyes. But He, the very same who formed [Adam](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm) at the beginning, with whom also the Father spoke, [saying], Let Us make man after Our image and likeness, [Genesis 1:25](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse25) revealing Himself in these last times to [men](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm), formed visual organs (*visionem*) for him who had been blind [in that body which he had derived] from Adam. Wherefore also the [Scripture](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html), pointing out what should come to pass, says, that when [Adam](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm) had hid himself because of his disobedience, the Lord came to him at eventide, called him forth, and said, Where are you? [Genesis 3:9](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen003.htm#verse9) That means that in the last times the very same [Word of God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm) came to call man, reminding him of his doings, living in which he had been hidden from the Lord. For just as at that time God spoke to [Adam](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm) at eventide, searching him out; so in the last times, by means of the same voice, searching out his posterity, He has visited them.

*We go forth from the earth*

16,1. And since [Adam](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm) was moulded from this earth to which we belong, the [Scripture](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html) tells us that God said to him, In the sweat of your face shall you eat your bread, until you turn again to the dust from whence you were taken. [Genesis 3:19](https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen003.htm#verse19) If then, after death, our bodies return to any other substance, it follows that from it also they have their substance. But if it be into this very [earth], it is manifest that it was also from it that man's frame was created; as also the Lord clearly showed, when from this very substance He formed eyes for the man [to whom He gave sight]. And thus was the hand of God plainly shown forth, by which [Adam](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm) was fashioned, and we too have been formed; and since there is one and the same Father, whose voice from the beginning even to the end is present with His handiwork, and the substance from which we were formed is plainly declared through the [Gospel](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm), we should therefore not seek after another Father besides Him, nor [look for] another substance from which we have been formed, besides what was mentioned beforehand, and shown forth by the Lord; nor another hand of God besides that which, from the beginning even to the end, forms us and prepares us for life, and is present with His handiwork, and perfects it after the image and likeness of [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm).

*Where the imagination of God is in Man*

16,2. And then, again, this Word was manifested when the [Word of God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm) was made man, assimilating Himself to [man](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm), and man to Himself, so that by means of his resemblance to the [Son](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm), man might become precious to the Father. For in times long past, it was *said* that man was created after the image of [God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm), but it was not [actually] *shown*; for the Word was as yet invisible, after whose image man was created, Wherefore also he did easily lose the similitude. When, however, the [Word of God](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm) became flesh, He confirmed both these: for He both showed forth the image [truly](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm), since He became Himself what was His image; and He re-established the similitude after a sure manner, by assimilating man to the invisible Father through means of the visible Word.
