### On the Cover

**Beginning of Creation**  
12th-century ivory plaque  
© Museo Diocesano di Salerno  

The cover image depicts the early stages of creation. On the left, one notes the Spirit of God, represented by the image of a dove hovering over the waters, as well as the separation of light from darkness. On the right side of the plaque, the Creator is adored by the angels. As often happens in medieval iconography, He is depicted with the face of the future Christ, author and perfecter of all things. The image aptly describes the theological approach of this manual, which presents protology and eschatology in the light of Trinitarian Christocentrism.
