## In Switzerland {#pioppi-storia-teologia.22}
Hans Urs VON BALTHASAR (1905-1988): the great Church historian Roger Aubert speaks of the "work, relevant and unclassifiable, of the Swiss theologian H.U. von Balthasar, elaborated on the boundaries between theology, religious philosophy and spirituality and which, increasingly rooted in the symbolic world of Greek patristics, strives, in continuous comparison with the orientations of contemporary thought, to constantly keep under the eyes the fullness of ecclesial tradition and the transcendence of the Christian message in the face of all that is human ".
