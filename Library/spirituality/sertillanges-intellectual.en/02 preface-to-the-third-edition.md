## Preface to the Third Edition {#sertillanges-intellectual.preface-to-the-third-edi}
Would now be a good time to reissue a work like this? When the universe is in flames, would it be opportune to throw pages into the furnace to be consumed, instead of forming a team and helping to pump water?

Poor us! Anyway, we feel an overwhelming powerlessness. But if the present is painful and bewildering, mustn’t we, amidst all this, worry about the future?

The future depends on God and on us; but in a certain order. It does not depend mainly on strength, but on thought. After atrocious demolitions, it will be necessary to rebuild. All elements of civilization must be resumed. Adventurous architects will propose projects to us. Some are already being announced with fanfare. Will the plans of our master builders have the necessary breadth, harmony, and solidity? God willing! In any case, there will be much work for reflection. There is a future for knowledge in all the forms it takes in our complex civilizations, whether ancient or more recent. Catholic thought will not have the right to abstain, nor will others. For all men of goodwill, the task will be immense. Persuaded that he holds the essential truth entrusted to him by Christ, the Catholic has more responsibility than any other, and to assume it, he must gather all his means, verify his methods, and exercise his heart through meditation on his possibilities and duties.

This book has no other purpose than to help you in this task. As in calmer times, yet also lacking, it will easily be enlivened by the reader with a new flame coming from their own consciousness. By itself, a text is nothing, as a journey is nothing in itself. It takes a soul that gathers the values of this one and the sentences of that one, making them shine in the contact of that mysterious light called truth or bearing the name of beauty.

The effect of a book depends on each one of us. Its final stage is not the printed object that leaves the publisher, but the mental verb that the reader himself produces. Faced with the challenges of events and in the current affliction, more than ever, after a peace that has cost us so dearly and that extends over so many ruins, we trust that the ideas exposed here about intellectual life will find in our young people a renewed understanding and superior effectiveness.

Here’s why we reissue this work. We know that it will be spread to other places, far from where it was generated, and it pleases us to think that, becoming tomorrow as universal a necessity as chaos is today, our humble effort may join that of the best, in the renewed common atmosphere and in the two worlds.

A. D. Sertillanges, O. P.,

1944.
