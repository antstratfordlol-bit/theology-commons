# Early Church Fathers (Philip Schaff Edition & Vernacular Translations)

A structured digital library of patristic literature in clean UTF-8 Markdown, providing the complete 38-volume English translation by Philip Schaff alongside Spanish patristic translations and selected critical Latin/Greek texts.

---

## 1. Scope & Corpora Held

* **Ante-Nicene Fathers (ANF 1–9):**
  * `ANF01` to `ANF09`: Apostolic Fathers, Justin Martyr, Irenaeus, Clement of Alexandria, Tertullian, Origen, Hippolytus, Cyprian, and early liturgies/apocrypha.
* **Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, First Series (NPNF1 1–14):**
  * `NPNF1-01` to `NPNF1-08`: Saint Augustine of Hippo (*Confessions*, *City of God*, *On the Trinity*, Anti-Pelagian, Anti-Donatist, homilies, and Psalms exposition).
  * `NPNF1-09` to `NPNF1-14`: Saint John Chrysostom (*On the Priesthood*, Pauline and Gospel homilies).
* **Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, Second Series (NPNF2 1–14):**
  * `NPNF2-01` to `NPNF2-14`: Eusebius, Socrates, Sozomen, Theodoret, Athanasius, Gregory of Nyssa, Gregory Nazianzen, Jerome, Basil, Hilary of Poitiers, John of Damascus, Ambrose, Sulpitius Severus, Vincent of Lérins, John Cassian, Leo the Great, Gregory the Great, Ephraim the Syrian, Aphrahat, and the Seven Ecumenical Councils.
* **Padres de la Iglesia:**
  * `padres-de-la-iglesia/`: Canonical patristic treatises and letters in Spanish translation (*Biblioteca de Autores Cristianos*).
* **Critical Selections:**
  * `critical/Latin` and `critical/Greek`: 41 curated landmark texts in original Latin and Greek with modern chapter/section divisions for rapid reading without Migne column pagination.

## 2. Citation Schemes & Sigla

Every work on this shelf carries invariant division anchors in the sigla grammar documented in `sigla.md`:
* Format: `{#<author>-<work>.<part>.<chapter>}`
