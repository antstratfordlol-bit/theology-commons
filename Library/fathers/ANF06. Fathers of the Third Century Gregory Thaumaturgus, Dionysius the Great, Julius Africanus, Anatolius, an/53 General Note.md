# General Note. {#methodius-general-note-2}

————————————

(*Vexillas*,—as they are called, p. 399.)

It is very curious to note how certain ideas are inherited from the earliest Fathers, and travel down, as here, to find a new expression in a distant age. Here our author reflects Justin Martyr,[^3190] and the *Labarum*[^3191] itself is the outcrop of what Justin wrote to Antoninus Pius.

[^3190]: See vol. i. p. 181, this series.
[^3191]: See p. 285, *supra*, under *the Emperors*.
