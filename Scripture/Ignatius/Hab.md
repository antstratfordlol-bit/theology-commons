# Habakkuk

#### The Prophet’s Complaint

## Habakkuk 1 {#Hab.1}

[1]{#Hab.1.1} 
The oracle of God which Habak'kuk the prophet saw.

[2]{#Hab.1.2} O LORD, how long shall I cry for help,

and you will not hear?

Or cry to you “Violence!”

and you will not save?

[3]{#Hab.1.3} Why do you make me see wrongs

and look upon trouble?

Destruction and violence are before me;

strife and contention arise.

[4]{#Hab.1.4} So the law is slacked

and justice never goes forth.

For the wicked surround the righteous,

so justice goes forth perverted.

[5]{#Hab.1.5} Look among the nations, and see;

wonder and be astounded.

For I am doing a work in your days

that you would not believe if told.

[6]{#Hab.1.6} For behold, I am rousing the Chalde'ans,

that bitter and hasty nation,

who march through the breadth of the earth,

to seize habitations not their own.

[7]{#Hab.1.7} Dread and fearsome are they;

their justice and dignity proceed from themselves.

[8]{#Hab.1.8} Their horses are swifter than leopards,

more fierce than the evening wolves;

their horsemen press proudly on.

Yes, their horsemen come from afar;

they fly like an eagle swift to devour.

[9]{#Hab.1.9} They all come for violence;

terror [^Hab-1-9a] of them goes before them.

They gather captives like sand.

[10]{#Hab.1.10} At kings they scoff,

and of rulers they make sport.

They laugh at every fortress,

for they heap up earth and take it.

[11]{#Hab.1.11} Then they sweep by like the wind and go on,

guilty men, whose own might is their god!

[12]{#Hab.1.12} Are you not from everlasting,

O LORD my God, my Holy One?

We shall not die.

O LORD, you have ordained them as a judgment;

and you, O Rock, have established them for chastisement.

[13]{#Hab.1.13} You who are of purer eyes than to behold evil

and cannot look on wrong,

why do you look on faithless men,

and are silent when the wicked swallows up

the man more righteous than he?

[14]{#Hab.1.14} For you make men like the fish of the sea,

like crawling things that have no ruler.

[15]{#Hab.1.15} He brings all of them up with a hook,

he drags them out with his net,

he gathers them in his seine;

so he rejoices and exults.

[16]{#Hab.1.16} Therefore he sacrifices to his net

and burns incense to his seine;

for by them he lives in luxury, [^Hab-1-16b]

and his food is rich.

[17]{#Hab.1.17} Is he then to keep on emptying his net,

and mercilessly slaying nations for ever?

#### God’s Reply

## Habakkuk 2 {#Hab.2}

[1]{#Hab.2.1} 
I will take my stand to watch,

and station myself on the tower,

and look forth to see what he will say to me,

and what I will answer concerning my complaint.

[2]{#Hab.2.2} And the LORD answered me:

“Write the vision;

make it plain upon tablets,

so he may run who reads it.

[3]{#Hab.2.3} For still the vision awaits its time;

it hastens to the end—it will not lie.

If it seem slow, wait for it;

it will surely come, it will not delay.

[4]{#Hab.2.4} Behold, he whose soul is not upright in him shall fail, [^Hab-2-4c]

but the righteous shall live by his faith. [^Hab-2-4d]

[5]{#Hab.2.5} Moreover, wine is treacherous;

the arrogant man shall not abide. [^Hab-2-5e]

His greed is as wide as Sheol;

like death he has never enough.

He gathers for himself all nations,

and collects as his own all peoples.”

[6]{#Hab.2.6} Shall not all these take up their taunt against him, in scoffing derision of him, and say,

“Woe to him who heaps up what is not his own—

for how long?—

and loads himself with pledges!”

[7]{#Hab.2.7} Will not your debtors suddenly arise,

and those awake who will make you tremble?

Then you will be booty for them.

[8]{#Hab.2.8} Because you have plundered many nations,

all the remnant of the peoples shall plunder you,

for the blood of men and violence to the earth,

to cities and all who dwell therein.

[9]{#Hab.2.9} Woe to him who gets evil gain for his house,

to set his nest on high,

to be safe from the reach of harm!

[10]{#Hab.2.10} You have devised shame to your house

by cutting off many peoples;

you have forfeited your life.

[11]{#Hab.2.11} For the stone will cry out from the wall,

and the beam from the woodwork respond.

[12]{#Hab.2.12} Woe to him who builds a town with blood,

and founds a city on iniquity!

[13]{#Hab.2.13} Behold, is it not from the LORD of hosts

that peoples labor only for fire,

and nations weary themselves for nothing?

[14]{#Hab.2.14} For the earth will be filled

with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD,

as the waters cover the sea.

[15]{#Hab.2.15} Woe to him who makes his neighbors drink

of the cup of his wrath, [^Hab-2-15f] and makes them drunk,

to gaze on their shame!

[16]{#Hab.2.16} You will be sated with contempt instead of glory.

Drink, yourself, and stagger! [^Hab-2-16g]

The cup in the LORD’s right hand

will come around to you,

and shame will come upon your glory!

[17]{#Hab.2.17} The violence done to Lebanon will overwhelm you;

the destruction of the beasts will terrify you, [^Hab-2-17h]

for the blood of men and violence to the earth,

to cities and all who dwell therein.

[18]{#Hab.2.18} What profit is an idol

when its maker has shaped it,

a metal image, a teacher of lies?

For the workman trusts in his own creation

when he makes dumb idols!

[19]{#Hab.2.19} Woe to him who says to a wooden thing, Awake;

to a mute stone, Arise!

Can this give revelation?

Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver,

and there is no breath at all in it.

[20]{#Hab.2.20} But the LORD is in his holy temple;

let all the earth keep silence before him.

#### Habakkuk’s Prayer

## Habakkuk 3 {#Hab.3}

[1]{#Hab.3.1} 
A prayer of Habak'kuk the prophet, accord- ing to Shigion'oth.

[2]{#Hab.3.2} O LORD, I have heard the report of you,

and your work, O LORD, I fear.

In the midst of the years renew it;

in the midst of the years make it known;

in wrath remember mercy.

[3]{#Hab.3.3} God came from Te'man,

and the Holy One from Mount Par'an.

His glory covered the heavens,

and the earth was full of his praise. *Selah*

[4]{#Hab.3.4} His brightness was like the light,

rays flashed from his hand;

and there he veiled his power.

[5]{#Hab.3.5} Before him went pestilence,

and plague followed close behind.

[6]{#Hab.3.6} He stood and measured the earth;

he looked and shook the nations;

Then the eternal mountains were scattered,

the everlasting hills sank low.

His ways were as of old.

[7]{#Hab.3.7} I saw the tents of Cush'an in affliction;

the curtains of the land of Mid'ian trembled.

[8]{#Hab.3.8} Was your wrath against the rivers, O LORD?

Was your anger against the rivers,

or your indignation against the sea,

when you rode upon your horses,

upon your chariot of victory?

[9]{#Hab.3.9} You stripped the sheath from your bow,

and put the arrows to the string. [^Hab-3-9i] *Selah*

You split the earth with rivers.

[10]{#Hab.3.10} The mountains saw you, and writhed;

the raging waters swept on;

the deep gave forth its voice,

it lifted its hands on high.

[11]{#Hab.3.11} The sun and moon stood still in their habitation [^Hab-3-11j]

at the light of your arrows as they sped,

at the flash of your glittering spear.

[12]{#Hab.3.12} You bestrode the earth in fury,

you trampled the nations in anger.

[13]{#Hab.3.13} You went forth for the salvation of your people,

for the salvation of your anointed.

You crushed the head of the wicked, [^Hab-3-13k]

laying him bare from thigh to neck. [^Hab-3-13l] *Selah*

[14]{#Hab.3.14} You pierced with your [^Hab-3-14m] shafts the head of his warriors, [^Hab-3-14n]

who came like a whirlwind to scatter me,

rejoicing as if to devour the poor in secret.

[15]{#Hab.3.15} You trampled the sea with your horses,

the surging of mighty waters.

[16]{#Hab.3.16} I hear, and my body trembles,

my lips quiver at the sound;

rottenness enters into my bones,

my steps totter [^Hab-3-16o] beneath me.

I will quietly wait for the day of trouble

to come upon people who invade us.

[17]{#Hab.3.17} Though the fig tree does not blossom,

nor fruit be on the vines,

the produce of the olive fail

and the fields yield no food,

the flock be cut off from the fold

and there be no herd in the stalls,

[18]{#Hab.3.18} yet I will rejoice in the LORD,

I will joy in the God of my salvation.

[19]{#Hab.3.19} GOD, the Lord, is my strength;

he makes my feet like deer’s feet,

he makes me tread upon my high places.

To the choirmaster: with stringed [^Hab-3-19p] instruments.
