# 1 Peter — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### 1 Pet 1:1 {#nt.1Pet.1.1}

See note on Jas 1:1. Baptism is the main theme of this letter which, in fact, may have been a baptismal address.

### 1 Pet 1:11 {#nt.1Pet.1.11}

*Spirit of Christ*: Christ, as the eternally existing Word, is envisaged as inspiring the prophets of old.

### 1 Pet 3:1–6 {#nt.1Pet.3.1}

Peter’s teaching on the behavior and status of women corresponds to that of Paul, though without Paul’s forthrightness.

### 1 Pet 4:1 {#nt.1Pet.4.1}

*ceased from sin*: Peter means that a continual acceptance of suffering is incompatible with a proneness to sin.

### 1 Pet 5:13 {#nt.1Pet.5.13}

*Babylon*: Rome was as full of iniquity as ancient Babylon; cf. Rev 17:9.
