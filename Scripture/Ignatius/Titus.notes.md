# Titus — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### Tit 1:5 {#nt.Titus.1.5}

*elders*: Each Christian community was ruled by a body of elders.

### Tit 2:13 {#nt.Titus.2.13}

*God and Savior*: Both terms appear to refer to Jesus Christ.

### Tit 3:5–7 {#nt.Titus.3.7}

A brief and clear statement of the doctrine of justification.
