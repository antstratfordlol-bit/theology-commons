# Haggai — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### 2:9 {#nt.Hag.1.1}

Haggai (Aggeus) is the first of the postexilic prophets and differs considerably from the earlier. No longer do we read threats of punishment for sin or words of consolation. The people need to be helped in their work of restoration and encouraged to persevere. Haggai first insists that the temple must be built before they think of anything else. This is to be the focal point of their life, as it was in the past, and they cannot hope for any prosperity without it. In spite of its humble appearance, the Spirit of God will rest upon it and the “latter splendor of this house shall be greater than the former, says the LORD of hosts” (2:9).
