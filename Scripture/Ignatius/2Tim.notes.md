# 2 Timothy — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### 2 Tim 1:15 {#nt.2Tim.1.15}

*Asia*: The Roman province of that name, now in western Turkey.

### 2 Tim 2:18 {#nt.2Tim.2.18}

They explained the resurrection by saying it was the rising to newness of life in baptism, thus ignoring a bodily resurrection, a doctrine the Greeks found very hard to accept; cf. Acts 17:32.

### 2 Tim 3:16 {#nt.2Tim.3.16}

Paul refers to the Old Testament Scriptures.

### 2 Tim 4:6 {#nt.2Tim.4.6}

*on the point of being sacrificed*: Literally, “poured out in sacrifice” as a drink-offering or libation.

### 2 Tim 4:21 {#nt.2Tim.4.21}

*Linus*: According to tradition, the successor of Peter in the see of Rome.
