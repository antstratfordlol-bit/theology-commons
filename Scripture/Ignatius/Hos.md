# Hosea

## Hosea 1 {#Hos.1}

[1]{#Hos.1.1} 
The word of the LORD that came to Hose'a the son of Bee'ri, in the days of Uzzi'ah, Jo'tham, A'haz, and Hezeki'ah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jerobo'am the son of Jo'ash, king of Israel.

#### Hosea Marries a Harlot and Has Children

[2]{#Hos.1.2} When the LORD first spoke through Hose'a, the LORD said to Hosea, “Go, take to yourself a wife of harlotry and have children of harlotry, for the land commits great harlotry by forsaking the LORD.” [3]{#Hos.1.3} So he went and took Gomer the daughter of Dibla'im, and she conceived and bore him a son.

[4]{#Hos.1.4} And the LORD said to him, “Call his name Jezre'el; for yet a little while, and I will punish the house of Je'hu for the blood of Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel. [5]{#Hos.1.5} And on that day, I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezre'el.”

[6]{#Hos.1.6} She conceived again and bore a daughter. And the LORD said to him, “Call her name Not pitied, for I will no more have pity on the house of Israel, to forgive them at all. [7]{#Hos.1.7} But I will have pity on the house of Judah, and I will deliver them by the LORD their God; I will not deliver them by bow, nor by sword, nor by war, nor by horses, nor by horsemen.”

[8]{#Hos.1.8} When she had weaned Not pitied, she conceived and bore a son. [9]{#Hos.1.9} And the LORD said, “Call his name Not my people, for you are not my people and I am not your God.”[^Hos-1-9a]

#### Israel’s Restoration

[10]{#Hos.1.10} [^Hos-1-10b] Yet the number of the people of Israel shall be like the sand of the sea, which can be neither measured nor numbered; and in the place where it was said to them, “You are not my people,” it shall be said to them, “Sons of the living God.” [11]{#Hos.1.11} And the people of Judah and the people of Israel shall be gathered together, and they shall appoint for themselves one head; and they shall go up from the land, for great shall be the day of Jezre'el.

## Hosea 2 {#Hos.2}

[1]{#Hos.2.1} 
[^Hos-2-1c]Say to your brother,[^Hos-2-1d] “My people,” and to your sister,[^Hos-2-1e] “She has obtained pity.”

[2]{#Hos.2.2} “Plead with your mother, plead—

for she is not my wife,

and I am not her husband—

that she put away her harlotry from her face,

and her adultery from between her breasts;

[3]{#Hos.2.3} lest I strip her naked

and make her as in the day she was born,

and make her like a wilderness,

and set her like a parched land,

and slay her with thirst.

[4]{#Hos.2.4} Upon her children also I will have no pity,

because they are children of harlotry.

[5]{#Hos.2.5} For their mother has played the harlot;

she that conceived them has acted shamefully.

For she said, ‘I will go after my lovers,

who give me my bread and my water,

my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink.’

[6]{#Hos.2.6} Therefore I will hedge up her[^Hos-2-6f] way with thorns;

and I will build a wall against her,

so that she cannot find her paths.

[7]{#Hos.2.7} She shall pursue her lovers,

but not overtake them;

and she shall seek them,

but shall not find them.

Then she shall say, ‘I will go

and return to my first husband,

for it was better with me then than now.’

[8]{#Hos.2.8} And she did not know

that it was I who gave her

the grain, the wine, and the oil,

and who lavished upon her silver

and gold which they used for Ba'al.

[9]{#Hos.2.9} Therefore I will take back

my grain in its time,

and my wine in its season;

and I will take away my wool and my flax,

which were to cover her nakedness.

[10]{#Hos.2.10} Now I will uncover her lewdness

in the sight of her lovers,

and no one shall rescue her out of my hand.

[11]{#Hos.2.11} And I will put an end to all her mirth,

her feasts, her new moons, her sabbaths,

and all her appointed feasts.

[12]{#Hos.2.12} And I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees,

of which she said,

‘These are my hire,

which my lovers have given me.’

I will make them a forest,

and the beasts of the field shall devour them.

[13]{#Hos.2.13} And I will punish her for the feast days of the Ba'als

when she burned incense to them

and decked herself with her ring and jewelry,

and went after her lovers,

and forgot me, says the LORD.

[14]{#Hos.2.14} “Therefore, behold, I will allure her,

and bring her into the wilderness,

and speak tenderly to her.

[15]{#Hos.2.15} And there I will give her her vineyards,

and make the Valley of A'chor a door of hope.

And there she shall answer as in the days of her youth,

as at the time when she came out of the land of Egypt.

[16]{#Hos.2.16} “And in that day, says the LORD, you will call me, ‘My husband,’ and no longer will you call me, ‘My Ba'al.’ [17]{#Hos.2.17} For I will remove the names of the Ba'als from her mouth, and they shall be mentioned by name no more. [18]{#Hos.2.18} And I will make for you[^Hos-2-18g] a covenant on that day with the beasts of the field, the birds of the air, and the creeping things of the ground; and I will abolish[^Hos-2-18h] the bow, the sword, and war from the land; and I will make you lie down in safety. [19]{#Hos.2.19} And I will espouse you for ever; I will espouse you in righteousness and in justice, in steadfast love, and in mercy. [20]{#Hos.2.20} I will espouse you in faithfulness; and you shall know the LORD.

[21]{#Hos.2.21} “And in that day, says the LORD,

I will answer the heavens

and they shall answer the earth;

[22]{#Hos.2.22} and the earth shall answer the grain, the wine, and the oil,

and they shall answer Jezre'el;[^Hos-2-22i]

[23]{#Hos.2.23} and I will sow him[^Hos-2-23j] for myself in the land.

And I will have pity on Not pitied,

and I will say to Not my people, ‘You are my people’;

and he shall say ‘You are my God.’”

#### The Lord’s Love for His Unfaithful People

## Hosea 3 {#Hos.3}

[1]{#Hos.3.1} 
And the LORD said to me, “Go again, love a woman who is beloved of a paramour and is an adulteress; even as the LORD loves the people of Israel, though they turn to other gods and love cakes of raisins.” [2]{#Hos.3.2} So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver and a homer and a lethech of barley. [3]{#Hos.3.3} And I said to her, “You must dwell as mine for many days; you shall not play the harlot, or belong to another man; so will I also be to you.” [4]{#Hos.3.4} For the children of Israel shall dwell many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or pillar, without ephod or teraphim. [5]{#Hos.3.5} Afterward the children of Israel shall return and seek the LORD their God, and David their king; and they shall come in fear to the LORD and to his goodness in the latter days.

#### God Accuses Israel

## Hosea 4 {#Hos.4}

[1]{#Hos.4.1} 
Hear the word of the LORD, O people of Israel;

for the LORD has a controversy with the inhabitants of the land.

There is no faithfulness or kindness,

and no knowledge of God in the land;

[2]{#Hos.4.2} there is swearing, lying, killing, stealing, and committing adultery;

they break all bounds and murder follows murder.

[3]{#Hos.4.3} Therefore the land mourns,

and all who dwell in it languish,

and also the beasts of the field,

and the birds of the air;

and even the fish of the sea are taken away.

[4]{#Hos.4.4} Yet let no one contend,

and let none accuse,

for with you is my contention, O priest.[^Hos-4-4k]

[5]{#Hos.4.5} You shall stumble by day,

the prophet also shall stumble with you by night;

and I will destroy your mother.

[6]{#Hos.4.6} My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge;

because you have rejected knowledge,

I reject you from being a priest to me.

And since you have forgotten the law of your God,

I also will forget your children.

[7]{#Hos.4.7} The more they increased,

the more they sinned against me;

I will change their glory into shame.

[8]{#Hos.4.8} They feed on the sin of my people;

they are greedy for their iniquity.

[9]{#Hos.4.9} And it shall be like people, like priest;

I will punish them for their ways,

and repay them for their deeds.

[10]{#Hos.4.10} They shall eat, but not be satisfied;

they shall play the harlot, but not multiply;

because they have forsaken the LORD

to cherish harlotry.

[11]{#Hos.4.11} Wine and new wine

take away the understanding.

[12]{#Hos.4.12} My people inquire of a thing of wood,

and their staff gives them oracles.

For a spirit of harlotry has led them astray,

and they have left their God to play the harlot.

[13]{#Hos.4.13} They sacrifice on the tops of the mountains,

and make offerings upon the hills,

under oak, poplar, and terebinth,

because their shade is good.

Therefore your daughters play the harlot,

and your brides commit adultery.

[14]{#Hos.4.14} I will not punish your daughters when they play the harlot,

nor your brides when they commit adultery;

for the men themselves go aside with harlots,

and sacrifice with cult prostitutes,

and a people without understanding shall come to ruin.

[15]{#Hos.4.15} Though you play the harlot, O Israel,

let not Judah become guilty.

Enter not into Gilgal,

nor go up to Beth-a'ven,

and swear not, “As the LORD lives.”

[16]{#Hos.4.16} Like a stubborn heifer,

Israel is stubborn;

can the LORD now feed them

like a lamb in a broad pasture?

[17]{#Hos.4.17} E'phraim is joined to idols,

let him alone.

[18]{#Hos.4.18} A band[^Hos-4-18l] of drunkards, they give themselves to harlotry;

they love shame more than their glory.[^Hos-4-18m]

[19]{#Hos.4.19} A wind has wrapped them[^Hos-4-19n] in its wings,

and they shall be ashamed because of their altars.[^Hos-4-19o]

[1]{#Hos.10.1} 
Israel is a luxuriant vine

that yields its fruit.

The more his fruit increased

the more altars he built;

as his country improved

he improved his pillars.

[2]{#Hos.10.2} Their heart is false;

now they must bear their guilt.

[3]{#Hos.10.3} For now they will say:

“We have no king,

for we fear not the LORD,

and a king, what could he do for us?”

[4]{#Hos.10.4} They utter mere words;

with empty oaths they make covenants;

so judgment springs up like poisonous weeds

in the furrows of the field.

[5]{#Hos.10.5} The inhabitants of Samar'ia tremble

for the calf[^Hos-10-5o] of Beth-a'ven.

and its idolatrous priests shall wail[^Hos-10-5p] over it,

break up your fallow ground,

for it is the time to seek the LORD,

that he may come and rain salvation upon you.

[13]{#Hos.10.13} You have plowed iniquity,

you have reaped injustice,

you have eaten the fruit of lies.

Because you have trusted in your chariots[^Hos-10-13v]

and in the multitude of your warriors,

[14]{#Hos.10.14} therefore the tumult of war shall arise among your people,

and all your fortresses shall be destroyed,

as Shal'man destroyed Beth-ar'bel on the day of battle;

mothers were dashed in pieces with their children.

[15]{#Hos.10.15} Thus it shall be done to you, O house of Israel,[^Hos-10-15w]

because of your great wickedness.

In the storm[^Hos-10-15x] the king of Israel

shall be utterly cut off.

#### God’s Compassion Despite Israel’s Unfaithfulness

## Hosea 11 {#Hos.11}

[1]{#Hos.11.1} 
When Israel was a child, I loved him,

and out of Egypt I called my son.

[2]{#Hos.11.2} The more I[^Hos-11-2y] called them,

the more they went from me;[^Hos-11-2z]

they kept sacrificing to the Ba'als,

and burning incense to idols.

[3]{#Hos.11.3} Yet it was I who taught E'phraim to walk,

I took them up in my[^Hos-11-3a] arms;

but they did not know that I healed them.

[4]{#Hos.11.4} I led them with cords of compassion,[^Hos-11-4b]

with the bands of love,

and I became to them as one

who raises an infant to his cheeks,

and I bent down to them and fed them.

[5]{#Hos.11.5} They shall return to the land of Egypt,

and Assyria shall be their king,

because they have refused to return to me.

[6]{#Hos.11.6} The sword shall rage against their cities,

consume the bars of their gates,

and devour them in their fortresses.[^Hos-11-6c]

[7]{#Hos.11.7} My people are bent on turning away from me;[^Hos-11-7d]

so they are appointed to the yoke,

and none shall remove it.

[8]{#Hos.11.8} How can I give you up, O E'phraim!

How can I hand you over, O Israel!

How can I make you like Admah!

How can I treat you like Zeboi'im!

My heart recoils within me,

my compassion grows warm and tender.

[9]{#Hos.11.9} I will not execute my fierce anger,

I will not again destroy E'phraim;

for I am God and not man,

the Holy One in your midst,

and I will not come to destroy.[^Hos-11-9e]

[10]{#Hos.11.10} They shall go after the LORD,

he will roar like a lion;

yes, he will roar,

and his sons shall come trembling from the west;

[11]{#Hos.11.11} they shall come trembling like birds from Egypt,

and like doves from the land of Assyria;

and I will return them to their homes, says the LORD.

[12]{#Hos.11.12} 
[^Hos-11-12f]E'phraim has encompassed me with lies,

and the house of Israel with deceit;

but Judah is still known by[^Hos-11-12g] God,

and is faithful to the Holy One.

#### The Long History of Israel’s Rebellion

## Hosea 12 {#Hos.12}

[1]{#Hos.12.1} 
E'phraim herds the wind,

and pursues the east wind all day long;

they multiply falsehood and violence;

they make a bargain with Assyria,

and oil is carried to Egypt.

[2]{#Hos.12.2} The LORD has an indictment against Judah,

and will punish Jacob according to his ways,

and repay him according to his deeds.

[3]{#Hos.12.3} In the womb he took his brother by the heel,

and in his manhood he strove with God.

[4]{#Hos.12.4} He strove with the angel and prevailed,

he wept and sought his favor.

He met God at Bethel,

and there God spoke with him[^Hos-12-4h]—

[5]{#Hos.12.5} the LORD the God of hosts,

the LORD is his name:

[6]{#Hos.12.6} “So you, by the help of your God, return,

hold fast to love and justice,

and wait continually for your God.”

[7]{#Hos.12.7} A trader, in whose hands are false balances,

he loves to oppress.

[8]{#Hos.12.8} E'phraim has said, “Ah, but I am rich,

I have gained wealth for myself”;

but all his riches can never offset[^Hos-12-8i]

the guilt he has incurred.

[9]{#Hos.12.9} I am the LORD your God

from the land of Egypt;

I will again make you dwell in tents,

as in the days of the appointed feast.

[10]{#Hos.12.10} I spoke to the prophets;

it was I who multiplied visions,

and through the prophets gave parables.

[11]{#Hos.12.11} If there is iniquity in Gilead

they shall surely come to nothing;

if in Gilgal they sacrifice bulls,

their altars also shall be like stone heaps

on the furrows of the field.

[12]{#Hos.12.12} (Jacob fled to the land of Ar'am,

there Israel did service for a wife,

and for a wife he herded sheep.)

[13]{#Hos.12.13} By a prophet the LORD brought Israel up from Egypt,

and by a prophet he was preserved.

[14]{#Hos.12.14} E'phraim has given bitter provocation;

so his LORD will leave his bloodguilt upon him,

and will turn back upon him his reproaches.

#### God the Savior and Judge of Israel

## Hosea 13 {#Hos.13}

[1]{#Hos.13.1} 
When E'phraim spoke, men trembled;

he was exalted in Israel;

but he incurred guilt through Ba'al and died.

[2]{#Hos.13.2} And now they sin more and more,

and make for themselves molten images,

idols skilfully made of their silver,

all of them the work of craftsmen.

Sacrifice to these, they say.[^Hos-13-2j]

Men kiss calves!

[3]{#Hos.13.3} Therefore they shall be like the morning mist

or like the dew that goes early away,

like the chaff that swirls from the threshing floor

or like smoke from a window.

[4]{#Hos.13.4} I am the LORD your God

from the land of Egypt;

you know no God but me,

and besides me there is no savior.

[5]{#Hos.13.5} It was I who knew you in the wilderness,

in the land of drought;

[6]{#Hos.13.6} but when they had fed[^Hos-13-6k] to the full,

they were filled, and their heart was lifted up;

therefore they forgot me.

[7]{#Hos.13.7} So I will be to them like a lion,

like a leopard I will lurk beside the way.

[8]{#Hos.13.8} I will fall upon them like a bear robbed of her cubs,

I will tear open their breast,

and there I will devour them like a lion,

as a wild beast would tear them.

[9]{#Hos.13.9} I will destroy you, O Israel;

who[^Hos-13-9l] can help you?

[10]{#Hos.13.10} Where[^Hos-13-10m] now is your king, to save you;

where are all[^Hos-13-10n] your princes,[^Hos-13-10o] to defend you[^Hos-13-10p]—

those of whom you said,

“Give me a king and princes”?

[11]{#Hos.13.11} I have given you kings in my anger,

and I have taken them away in my wrath.

[12]{#Hos.13.12} The iniquity of E'phraim is bound up,

his sin is kept in store.

[13]{#Hos.13.13} The pangs of childbirth come for him,

but he is an unwise son;

for now he does not present himself

at the mouth of the womb.

[14]{#Hos.13.14} Shall I ransom them from the power of Sheol?

Shall I redeem them from Death?

O Death, where[^Hos-13-14q] are your plagues?

O Sheol, where[^Hos-13-14q] is your destruction?

Compassion is hidden from my eyes.

[15]{#Hos.13.15} Though he may flourish as the reed plant,[^Hos-13-15r]

the east wind, the wind of the LORD, shall come,

rising from the wilderness;

and his fountain shall dry up,

his spring shall be parched;

it shall strip his treasury

of every precious thing.

[16]{#Hos.13.16} 
[^Hos-13-16s]Samar'ia shall bear her guilt,

because she has rebelled against her God;

they shall fall by the sword,

their little ones shall be dashed in pieces,

and their pregnant women ripped open.

#### A Plea for Repentance and a Promise

## Hosea 14 {#Hos.14}

[1]{#Hos.14.1} 
Return, O Israel, to the LORD your God,

for you have stumbled because of your iniquity.

[2]{#Hos.14.2} Take with you words

and return to the LORD;

say to him,

“Take away all iniquity;

accept that which is good

and we will render

the fruit[^Hos-14-2t] of our lips.

[3]{#Hos.14.3} Assyria shall not save us,

we will not ride upon horses;

and we will say no more, ‘Our God,’

to the work of our hands.

In you the orphan finds mercy.”

[4]{#Hos.14.4} I will heal their faithlessness;

I will love them freely,

for my anger has turned from them.

[5]{#Hos.14.5} I will be as the dew to Israel;

he shall blossom as the lily,

he shall strike root as the poplar;[^Hos-14-5u]

[6]{#Hos.14.6} his shoots shall spread out;

his beauty shall be like the olive,

and his fragrance like Lebanon.

[7]{#Hos.14.7} They shall return and dwell beneath my[^Hos-14-7v] shadow,

they shall flourish as a garden;[^Hos-14-7w]

they shall blossom as the vine,

their fragrance shall be like the wine of Lebanon.

[8]{#Hos.14.8} O E'phraim, what have I to do with idols?

It is I who answer and look after you.[^Hos-14-8x]

I am like an evergreen cypress,

from me comes your fruit.

[9]{#Hos.14.9} Whoever is wise, let him understand these things;

whoever is discerning, let him know them;

for the ways of the LORD are right,

and the upright walk in them,

but transgressors stumble in them.
