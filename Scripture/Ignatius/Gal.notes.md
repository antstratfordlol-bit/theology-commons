# Galatians — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### Gal 1:6 {#nt.Gal.1.6}

After the greeting there is no commendation, as was usual, but rather strong rebuke.

### Gal 1:10 {#nt.Gal.1.10}

No doubt Paul was accused of exempting Gentile converts from the law of Moses in order to curry favor.

### Gal 1:19 {#nt.Gal.1.19}

*Lord’s brother*: See the note on *brethren* at Mt 12:46 above.

### Gal 2:16 {#nt.Gal.2.16}

*works of the law*: Paul is contrasting not faith with good works but faith in Jesus Christ with observance of the law of Moses.

### Gal 3:2 {#nt.Gal.3.2}

*Spirit*: He probably refers to the outward manifestations of the Spirit, such as the gift of tongues.

### Gal 5:11 {#nt.Gal.5.11}

*stumbling block of the cross*: So far as the Jews were concerned, this would consist largely in the exemption of converts from the obligations of the law of Moses.
