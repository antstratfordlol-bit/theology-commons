# Colossians — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### Col 1:15 {#nt.Col.1.15}

*first-born*: Born of the Father before all ages. The reference here is to the divine person of the Word; see verse 16.

### Col 1:18 {#nt.Col.1.18}

His human nature.

### Col 1:24 {#nt.Col.1.24}

*what is lacking*: Christ’s sufferings were, of course, sufficient for our redemption, but all of us may add ours to his, in order that the fruits of his redemption be applied to the souls of men.

### Col 3:18—4:5 {#nt.Col.3.18}

The whole passage corresponds closely to Eph 5:22—6:9.

### Col 4:10 {#nt.Col.4.10}

Mark, the evangelist, and, probably, the John Mark of Acts 12:12, 25.

### Col 4:14 {#nt.Col.4.14}

Luke, the evangelist.
