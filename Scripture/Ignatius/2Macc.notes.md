# 2 Maccabees — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### 2 Mac 4:29 {#nt.2Macc.4.29}

Vulgate reads: “And Menelaus was removed from the priesthood, Lysimachus his brother succeeding: and Sostratus was made governor of the Cyprians.”

### 2 Mac 4:34 {#nt.2Macc.4.34}

*put him out of the way:* Vulgate has “slew him.”

### 2 Mac 4:40 {#nt.2Macc.4.40}

*Auranus:* Vulgate has “Tyrannus.”

### 2 Mac 7:9 {#nt.2Macc.7.9}

*to an everlasting renewal of life:* Vulgate has: “in the resurrection of eternal life.”

### 2 Mac 12:45 {#nt.2Macc.12.45}

Vulgate has (verses 45–46): “45And because he considered that they who had fallen asleep with godliness had great grace laid up for them. 46It is therefore a holy and wholesome thought to pray for the dead, that they may be loosed from sins.”
