# Sirach

#### The Prologue

Whereas many great teachings have been given to us through the law and the prophets and the others that followed them, on account of which we should praise Israel for instruction and wisdom; and since it is necessary not only that the readers themselves should acquire understanding but also that those who love learning should be able to help the outsiders by both speaking and writing, my grandfather Jesus, after devoting himself especially to the reading of the law and the prophets and the other books of our fathers, and after acquiring considerable proficiency in them, was himself also led to write something pertaining to instruction and wisdom, in order that, by becoming conversant with this also, those who love learning should make even greater progress in living according to the law.

You are urged therefore to read with good will and attention, and to be indulgent[^Sir-0-1a] in cases where, despite our diligent labor in translating, we may seem to have rendered some phrases imperfectly. For what was originally expressed in Hebrew does not have exactly the same sense when translated into another language. Not only this work, but even the law itself, the prophecies, and the rest of the books differ not a little as originally expressed.

When I came to Egypt in the thirty-eighth year of the reign of Euer'getes and stayed for some time, I found opportunity for no little instruction.[^Sir-0-1b] It seemed highly necessary that I should myself devote some pains and labor to the translation of the following book, using in that period of time great watchfulness and skill in order to complete and publish the book for those living abroad who wished to gain learning, being prepared in character to live according to the law.

#### In Praise of Wisdom

## Sirach 1 {#Sir.1}

[1]{#Sir.1.1} All wisdom comes from the Lord

and is with him for ever.

[2]{#Sir.1.2} The sand of the sea, the drops of rain,

and the days of eternity—who can count them?

[3]{#Sir.1.3} The height of heaven, the breadth of the earth,

the abyss, and wisdom—who can search them out?

[4]{#Sir.1.4} Wisdom was created before all things,

and prudent understanding from eternity.[^Sir-1-4c]

[5]{#Sir.1.5} The source of wisdom is God’s word in the highest heaven,

and her ways are the eternal commandments.

[6]{#Sir.1.6} The root of wisdom—to whom has it been revealed?

Her clever devices—who knows them?[^Sir-1-6d]

[7]{#Sir.1.7} The knowledge of wisdom—to whom was it manifested?

And her abundant experience—who has understood it?

[8]{#Sir.1.8} There is One who is wise, the Creator of all,

the King greatly to be feared, sitting upon his throne, and ruling as God.

[9]{#Sir.1.9} The Lord himself created wisdom[^Sir-1-9e] in the holy spirit;

he saw her and apportioned her,

he poured her out upon all his works.

[10]{#Sir.1.10} She dwells with all flesh according to his gift,

and he supplied her to those who love him.

[11]{#Sir.1.11} The fear of the Lord is glory and exultation,

and gladness and a crown of rejoicing.

[12]{#Sir.1.12} The fear of the Lord delights the heart,

and gives gladness and joy and long life.

[13]{#Sir.1.13} With him who fears the Lord it will go well at the end;

on the day of his death he will be blessed.

[14]{#Sir.1.14} To fear the Lord is the beginning of wisdom;

she is created with the faithful in the womb.

[15]{#Sir.1.15} She made[^Sir-1-15f] among men an eternal foundation,

and among their descendants she will be trusted.

[16]{#Sir.1.16} To fear the Lord is wisdom’s full measure;

she satisfies[^Sir-1-16g] men with her fruits;

[17]{#Sir.1.17} she fills their whole house with desirable goods,

and their storehouses with her produce.

[18]{#Sir.1.18} The fear of the Lord is the crown of wisdom,

making peace and perfect health to flourish.

[19]{#Sir.1.19} He saw her and apportioned her;

he rained down knowledge and discerning comprehension,

and he exalted the glory of those who held her fast.

[20]{#Sir.1.20} To fear the Lord is the root of wisdom,

and her branches are long life.[^Sir-1-20h]

[22]{#Sir.1.22} Unrighteous anger cannot be justified,

for a man’s anger tips the scale to his ruin.

[23]{#Sir.1.23} A patient man will endure until the right moment,

and then joy will burst forth for him.

[24]{#Sir.1.24} He will hide his words until the right moment,

and the lips of many will tell of his good sense.

[25]{#Sir.1.25} In the treasuries of wisdom are wise sayings,

but godliness is an abomination to a sinner.

[26]{#Sir.1.26} If you desire wisdom, keep the commandments,

and the Lord will supply it for you.

[27]{#Sir.1.27} For the fear of the Lord is wisdom and instruction,

and he delights in fidelity and meekness.

[28]{#Sir.1.28} Do not disobey the fear of the Lord;

do not approach him with a divided mind.

[29]{#Sir.1.29} Be not a hypocrite in men’s sight,[^Sir-1-29i]

and keep watch over your lips.

[30]{#Sir.1.30} Do not exalt yourself lest you fall,

and thus bring dishonor upon yourself.

The Lord will reveal your secrets

and cast you down in the midst of the congregation,

because you did not come in the fear of the Lord,

and your heart was full of deceit.

#### Duties toward God

## Sirach 2 {#Sir.2}

[1]{#Sir.2.1} My son, if you come forward to serve the Lord,

remain in justice and in fear,

and prepare yourself for temptation.[^Sir-2-1j]

[2]{#Sir.2.2} Set your heart right and be steadfast,

incline your ear, and receive words of understanding,

and do not be hasty in time of calamity.

[3]{#Sir.2.3} Await God’s patience, cling to him and do not depart,

that you may be wise in all your ways.

[4]{#Sir.2.4} Accept whatever is brought upon you,

and endure it in sorrow;

in changes that humble you be patient.

[5]{#Sir.2.5} For gold and silver are tested in the fire,

and acceptable men in the furnace of humiliation.

[6]{#Sir.2.6} Trust in God, and he will help you;

hope in him, and he will make your ways straight.

Stay in fear of him, and grow old in him.

[7]{#Sir.2.7} You who fear the Lord, wait for his mercy;

and turn not aside, lest you fall.

[8]{#Sir.2.8} You who fear the Lord, trust in him,

and your reward will not fail;

[9]{#Sir.2.9} you who fear the Lord, hope for good things,

for everlasting joy and mercy.

You who fear the Lord, love him,

and your hearts will be made radiant.

[10]{#Sir.2.10} Consider the ancient generations and see:

who ever trusted in the Lord and was put to shame?

Or who ever persevered in his commandments[^Sir-2-10k] and was forsaken?

Or who ever called upon him and was overlooked?

[11]{#Sir.2.11} For the Lord is compassionate and merciful;

he forgives sins and saves in time of affliction,

and he is the shield of all who seek him in truth.

[12]{#Sir.2.12} Woe to timid hearts and to slack hands,

and to the sinner who walks along two ways!

[13]{#Sir.2.13} Woe to the faint heart, for it has no trust!

Therefore it will not be sheltered.

[14]{#Sir.2.14} Woe to you who have lost your endurance!

What will you do when the Lord punishes you?

[15]{#Sir.2.15} Those who fear the Lord will not disobey his words,

and those who love him will keep his ways.

[16]{#Sir.2.16} Those who fear the Lord will seek his approval,

and those who love him will be filled with the law.

[17]{#Sir.2.17} Those who fear the Lord will prepare their hearts,

and will humble themselves before him.

[18]{#Sir.2.18} Let us fall[^Sir-2-18l] into the hands of the Lord,

but not into the hands of men;

for as his majesty is,

so also is his mercy.

#### Duties toward Parents and Others

## Sirach 3 {#Sir.3}

[1]{#Sir.3.1} Listen to me your father, O children;

and act accordingly, that you may be kept in safety.

[2]{#Sir.3.2} For the Lord honored the father above the children,

and he confirmed the right of the mother over her sons.

[3]{#Sir.3.3} Whoever honors his father atones for sins,

and preserves himself from them.

When he prays, he is heard;

[4]{#Sir.3.4} and whoever glorifies his mother is like one who lays up treasure.

[5]{#Sir.3.5} Whoever honors his father will be gladdened by his own children,

and when he prays he will be heard.

[6]{#Sir.3.6} Whoever glorifies his father will have long life,

and whoever obeys the Lord will refresh his mother;

[7]{#Sir.3.7} he will serve his parents as his masters.[^Sir-3-7m]

[8]{#Sir.3.8} Honor your father by word and deed,

that a blessing from him may come upon you.

[9]{#Sir.3.9} For a father’s blessing strengthens the houses of the children,

but a mother’s curse uproots their foundations.

[10]{#Sir.3.10} Do not glorify yourself by dishonoring your father,

for your father’s dishonor is no glory to you.

[11]{#Sir.3.11} For a man’s glory comes from honoring his father,

and it is a disgrace for children not to respect their mother.

[12]{#Sir.3.12} O son, help your father in his old age,

and do not grieve him as long as he lives;

[13]{#Sir.3.13} even if he is lacking in understanding, show forbearance;

and do not despise him all the days of his life.

[14]{#Sir.3.14} For kindness to a father will not be forgotten,

and against your sins it will be credited to you

—a house raised in justice to you.

[15]{#Sir.3.15} in the day of your affliction it will be remembered in your favor;

as frost in fair weather, your sins will melt away.

[16]{#Sir.3.16} Whoever forsakes his father is like a blasphemer,

and whoever angers his mother is cursed by the Lord.

[17]{#Sir.3.17} My son, perform your tasks in meekness;

then you will be loved more than a giver of gifts.

[18]{#Sir.3.18} The greater you are, the more you must humble yourself;

so you will find favor with God.[^Sir-3-18n]

[19]{#Sir.3.19} There are many who are noble and renowned,

but it is to the humble that he reveals his mysteries.

[20]{#Sir.3.20} For great is the might of the Lord;

he is glorified by the humble.

[21]{#Sir.3.21} Seek not what is too difficult for you,

nor investigate what is beyond your power.

[22]{#Sir.3.22} Reflect upon what has been assigned to you,

and do not be curious about many of his works,

for you do not need to see with your eyes what is hidden.

[23]{#Sir.3.23} Do not meddle in what is beyond your tasks,

for matters too great for human understanding have been shown you.

[24]{#Sir.3.24} For their hasty judgment has led many astray,

and wrong opinion has caused their thoughts to slip.[^Sir-3-24o]

[26]{#Sir.3.26} A stubborn mind will be afflicted at the end,

and whoever loves danger will perish by it.

[27]{#Sir.3.27} A stubborn mind will be burdened by troubles,

and the sinner will heap sin upon sin.

[28]{#Sir.3.28} The affliction of the proud has no healing,

for a plant of wickedness has taken root in them,

though it will not be perceived.

[29]{#Sir.3.29} The mind of the wise man will ponder the words of the wise,

and an attentive ear is the wise man’s desire.

[30]{#Sir.3.30} Water extinguishes a blazing fire:

so almsgiving atones for sin.

[31]{#Sir.3.31} Whoever repays favors gives thought to the future;

at the moment of his falling he will find support.

#### Precepts for Everyday Life

## Sirach 4 {#Sir.4}

[1]{#Sir.4.1} My son, deprive not the poor of his living,

and do not keep needy eyes waiting.

[2]{#Sir.4.2} Do not grieve the one who is hungry,

nor anger a man in want.

[3]{#Sir.4.3} Do not add to the troubles of an angry mind,

nor delay your gift to a beggar.

[4]{#Sir.4.4} Do not reject an afflicted suppliant,

nor turn your face away from the poor.

[5]{#Sir.4.5} Do not avert your eye from the needy,

nor give a man occasion to curse you;

[6]{#Sir.4.6} for if in bitterness of soul he calls down a curse upon you,

his Creator will hear his prayer.

[7]{#Sir.4.7} Make yourself beloved in the congregation;

bow your head low to a great man.

[8]{#Sir.4.8} Incline your ear to the poor,

and answer him peaceably and gently.

[9]{#Sir.4.9} Deliver him who is wronged from the hand of the wrongdoer;

and do not be fainthearted in judging a case.

[10]{#Sir.4.10} Be like a father to orphans,

and instead of a husband to their mother;

you will then be like a son of the Most High,

and he will love you more than does your mother.

[11]{#Sir.4.11} Wisdom breathes life into her sons

and gives help to those who seek her.

[12]{#Sir.4.12} Whoever loves her loves life,

and those who seek her early

will win the Lord’s good favor.

[13]{#Sir.4.13} Whoever holds her fast will obtain glory,

and the Lord will bless the place she[^Sir-4-13p] enters.

[14]{#Sir.4.14} Those who serve her will minister to the Holy One;[^Sir-4-14q]

the Lord loves those who love her.

[15]{#Sir.4.15} He who obeys her will judge the nations,

and whoever gives heed to her will dwell secure.

[16]{#Sir.4.16} If he has faith in her he will obtain her;

and his descendants will remain in possession of her.

[17]{#Sir.4.17} For she will walk with him in disguise,

and at first she will put him to the test;

she will bring fear and cowardice upon him,

and will torment him by her discipline

until he holds her in his thoughts,

and she trusts him.

[18]{#Sir.4.18} Then she will come straight back to him and strengthen him,

she will gladden him and will reveal her secrets to him,

and store up for him knowledge

and the discernment of what is right.

[19]{#Sir.4.19} But if he goes astray she will forsake him,

and give him over into the hands of his foe.

[20]{#Sir.4.20} Observe the right time, and beware of evil;[^Sir-4-20r]

and do not bring shame on yourself.

[21]{#Sir.4.21} For there is a shame which brings sin,

and there is a shame which is glory and favor.

[22]{#Sir.4.22} Do not show partiality, to your own harm,

or deference, to your downfall.

[23]{#Sir.4.23} Do not refrain from speaking at the crucial time,[^Sir-4-23s]

and do not hide your wisdom.[^Sir-4-23t]

[24]{#Sir.4.24} For wisdom is known through speech,

and education through the words of the tongue.

[25]{#Sir.4.25} Never speak against the truth,

but be mindful of your ignorance.

[26]{#Sir.4.26} Do not be ashamed to confess your sins,

and do not try to stop the current of a river.

[27]{#Sir.4.27} Do not subject yourself to a foolish fellow,

nor show partiality to a ruler.

[28]{#Sir.4.28} Strive even to death for the truth

and the Lord God will fight for you.

[29]{#Sir.4.29} Do not be reckless in your speech,

or sluggish and remiss in your deeds.

[30]{#Sir.4.30} Do not be like a lion in your home,

nor be a faultfinder with your servants.

[31]{#Sir.4.31} Let not your hand be extended to receive,

but withdrawn when it is time to repay.

## Sirach 5 {#Sir.5}

[1]{#Sir.5.1} Do not set your heart on your wealth,

nor say, “I have enough.”

[2]{#Sir.5.2} Do not follow your inclination and strength,

walking according to the desires of your heart.

[3]{#Sir.5.3} Do not say, “Who will have power over me?”

or “Who will bring me down because of my deeds?”

for God will surely punish you.

[4]{#Sir.5.4} Do not say, “I sinned, and what happened to me?”

for the Most High is slow to anger.

[5]{#Sir.5.5} Do not be so confident of atonement

that you add sin to sin.

[6]{#Sir.5.6} Do not say, “His mercy is great,

he will forgive[^Sir-5-6u] the multitude of my sins,”

for both mercy and wrath are with him,

and his anger rests on sinners.

[7]{#Sir.5.7} Do not delay to turn to the Lord,

nor postpone it from day to day;

for suddenly the wrath of the Lord will go forth,

and at the time of punishment you will perish.

[8]{#Sir.5.8} Do not depend on dishonest wealth,

for it will not benefit you in the day of calamity.

[9]{#Sir.5.9} Do not winnow with every wind,

nor follow every path:

the double-tongued sinner does that.

[10]{#Sir.5.10} Be steadfast in your understanding,

and let your speech be consistent.

[11]{#Sir.5.11} Be quick to hear,

and be deliberate in answering.

[12]{#Sir.5.12} If you have understanding, answer your neighbor;

but if not, put your hand on your mouth.

[13]{#Sir.5.13} Glory and dishonor come from speaking,

and a man’s tongue is his downfall.

[14]{#Sir.5.14} Do not be called a slanderer,

and do not lie in ambush with your tongue;

for shame comes to the thief,

and severe condemnation to the double-tongued.

[15]{#Sir.5.15} In great or small matters do not act amiss,

and do not become an enemy instead of a friend;

## Sirach 6 {#Sir.6}

[1]{#Sir.6.1} for a bad name incurs shame and reproach:

so fares the double-tongued sinner.

[2]{#Sir.6.2} Do not exalt yourself through your soul’s counsel,

lest your soul be torn in pieces like a bull.[^Sir-6-2v]

[3]{#Sir.6.3} You will devour your leaves and destroy your fruit,

and will be left like a withered tree.

[4]{#Sir.6.4} An evil soul will destroy him who has it,

and make him the laughingstock of his enemies.

[5]{#Sir.6.5} A pleasant voice multiplies friends and softens enemies,

and a gracious tongue multiplies courtesies.

[6]{#Sir.6.6} Let those that are at peace with you be many,

but let your advisers be one in a thousand.

[7]{#Sir.6.7} When you gain a friend, gain him through testing,

and do not trust him hastily.

[8]{#Sir.6.8} For there is a friend who is such at his own convenience,

but will not stand by you in your day of trouble.

[9]{#Sir.6.9} And there is a friend who changes into an enemy,

and will disclose a quarrel to your disgrace.

[10]{#Sir.6.10} And there is a friend who is a table companion,

but will not stand by you in your day of trouble.

[11]{#Sir.6.11} In prosperity he will make himself your equal,

and be bold with your servants;

[12]{#Sir.6.12} but if you are brought low he will turn against you,

and will hide himself from your presence.

[13]{#Sir.6.13} Keep yourself far from your enemies,

and be on guard toward your friends.

[14]{#Sir.6.14} A faithful friend is a sturdy shelter:

he that has found one has found a treasure.

[15]{#Sir.6.15} There is nothing so precious as a faithful friend,

and no scales can measure his excellence.

[16]{#Sir.6.16} A faithful friend is an elixir of life;

and those who fear the Lord will find him.

[17]{#Sir.6.17} Whoever fears the Lord directs his friendship aright,

for as he is, so is his neighbor also.

[18]{#Sir.6.18} My son, from your youth up choose instruction,

and until you are old you will keep finding wisdom.

[19]{#Sir.6.19} Come to her like one who plows and sows,

and wait for her good harvest.

For in her service you will toil a little while,

and soon you will eat of her produce.

[20]{#Sir.6.20} She seems very harsh to the uninstructed;

a weakling will not remain with her.

[21]{#Sir.6.21} She will weigh him down like a heavy testing stone,

and he will not be slow to cast her off.

[22]{#Sir.6.22} For wisdom is like her name,

and is not manifest to many.

[23]{#Sir.6.23} Listen, my son, and accept my judgment;

do not reject my counsel.

[24]{#Sir.6.24} Put your feet into her chains,

and your neck into her collar.

[25]{#Sir.6.25} Put your shoulder under her and carry her,

and do not fret under her bonds.

[26]{#Sir.6.26} Come to her with all your soul,

and keep her ways with all your might.

[27]{#Sir.6.27} Search out and seek, and she will become known to you;

and when you get hold of her, do not let her go.

[28]{#Sir.6.28} For at last you will find the rest she gives,

and she will be changed into joy for you.

[29]{#Sir.6.29} Then her chains will become for you a strong protection,

and her collar a glorious robe.

[30]{#Sir.6.30} Her yoke[^Sir-6-30w] is a golden ornament,

and her bonds are a cord of blue.

[31]{#Sir.6.31} You will wear her like a glorious robe,

and put her on like a crown of gladness.

[32]{#Sir.6.32} If you are willing, my son, you will be taught,

and if you apply yourself you will become clever.

[33]{#Sir.6.33} If you love to listen you will gain knowledge,

and if you incline your ear you will become wise.

[34]{#Sir.6.34} Stand in the assembly of the elders.

Who is wise? Cling to him.

[35]{#Sir.6.35} Be ready to listen to every[^Sir-6-35x] narrative,

and do not let wise proverbs escape you.

[36]{#Sir.6.36} If you see an intelligent man, visit him early;

let your foot wear out his doorstep.

[37]{#Sir.6.37} Reflect on the statutes of the Lord,

and meditate at all times on his commandments.

It is he who will give insight to[^Sir-6-37y] your mind,

and your desire for wisdom will be granted.

#### Advice for Right Conduct

## Sirach 7 {#Sir.7}

[1]{#Sir.7.1} Do no evil, and evil will never befall you.

[2]{#Sir.7.2} Stay away from wrong, and it will turn away from you.

[3]{#Sir.7.3} My son, do not sow the furrows of injustice,

and you will not reap a sevenfold crop.

[4]{#Sir.7.4} Do not seek from the Lord the highest office,

nor the seat of honor from the king.

[5]{#Sir.7.5} Do not assert your righteousness before the Lord,

nor display your wisdom before the king.

[6]{#Sir.7.6} Do not seek to become a judge,

lest you be unable to remove iniquity,

lest you be partial to a powerful man,

and thus put a blot on your integrity.

[7]{#Sir.7.7} Do not offend against the public,

and do not disgrace yourself among the people.

[8]{#Sir.7.8} Do not commit a sin twice;

even for one you will not go unpunished.

[9]{#Sir.7.9} Do not say, “He will consider the multitude of my gifts,

and when I make an offering to the Most High God he will accept it.”

[10]{#Sir.7.10} Do not be fainthearted in your prayer,

nor neglect to give alms.

[11]{#Sir.7.11} Do not ridicule a man who is bitter in soul,

for there is One who abases and exalts.

[12]{#Sir.7.12} Do not devise[^Sir-7-12z] a lie against your brother,

nor do the like to a friend.

[13]{#Sir.7.13} Refuse to utter any lie,

for the habit of lying serves no good.

[14]{#Sir.7.14} Do not prattle in the assembly of the elders,

nor repeat yourself in your prayer.

[15]{#Sir.7.15} Do not hate toilsome labor,

or farm work, which were created by the Most High.

[16]{#Sir.7.16} Do not count yourself among the crowd of sinners;

remember that wrath does not delay.

[17]{#Sir.7.17} Humble yourself greatly,

for the punishment of the ungodly is fire and worms.[^Sir-7-17a]

[18]{#Sir.7.18} Do not exchange a friend for money,

or a real brother for the gold of Ophir.

[19]{#Sir.7.19} Do not deprive yourself of a wise and good wife,

for her charm is worth more than gold.

[20]{#Sir.7.20} Do not abuse a servant who performs his work faithfully,

or a hired laborer who devotes himself to you.

[21]{#Sir.7.21} Let your soul love[^Sir-7-21b] an intelligent servant;

do not withhold from him his freedom.

[22]{#Sir.7.22} Do you have cattle? Look after them;

if they are profitable to you, keep them.

[23]{#Sir.7.23} Do you have children? Discipline them,

and make them obedient[^Sir-7-23c] from their youth.

[24]{#Sir.7.24} Do you have daughters? Be concerned for their chastity,[^Sir-7-24d]

and do not show yourself too indulgent with them.

[25]{#Sir.7.25} Give a daughter in marriage; you will have finished a great task.

But give her to a man of understanding.

[26]{#Sir.7.26} If you have a wife who pleases you,[^Sir-7-26e] do not cast her out;

but do not trust yourself to one whom you detest.

[27]{#Sir.7.27} With all your heart honor your father,

and do not forget the birth pangs of your mother.

[28]{#Sir.7.28} Remember that through your parents[^Sir-7-28f] you were born;

and what can you give back to them that equals their gift to you?

[29]{#Sir.7.29} With all your soul fear the Lord,

and they will not wallow in sins.

[13]{#Sir.23.13} Do not accustom your mouth to lewd vulgarity,

for it involves sinful speech.

[14]{#Sir.23.14} Remember your father and mother

when[^Sir-23-14x] you sit among great men;

lest you be forgetful in their presence,

and be deemed a fool on account of your habits;

then you will wish that you had never been born,

and you will curse the day of your birth.

[15]{#Sir.23.15} A man accustomed to using insulting words

will never become disciplined all his days.

[16]{#Sir.23.16} Two sorts of men multiply sins,

and a third incurs wrath.

The soul heated like a burning fire

will not be quenched until it is consumed;

a man who commits fornication with his near of kin[^Sir-23-16y]

[17]{#Sir.23.17} To a fornicator all bread tastes sweet;

[18]{#Sir.23.18} A man who breaks his marriage vows

Darkness surrounds me, and the walls hide me,

and no one sees me. Why should I fear?

The Most High will not take notice of my sins.”

are ten thousand times brighter than the sun;

they look upon all the ways of men,

and perceive even the hidden places.

[20]{#Sir.23.20} Before the universe was created, it was known to him;

[21]{#Sir.23.21} This man will be punished in the streets of the city,

and provides an heir by a stranger.

[23]{#Sir.23.23} For first of all, she has disobeyed the law of the Most High;

and third, she has committed adultery through harlotry

[25]{#Sir.23.25} Her children will not take root,

[26]{#Sir.23.26} She will leave her memory for a curse,

#### The Praise of Wisdom

## Sirach 24 {#Sir.24}

[1]{#Sir.24.1} Wisdom will praise herself and is honored in God,

and will glory in the midst of her people.[^Sir-24-1a]

[2]{#Sir.24.2} In the assembly of the Most High she will open her mouth,

and in the presence of his host she will glory.

In the midst of her people she is exalted;

in holy fulness she is admired.

In the multitude of the chosen she finds praise,

and among the blessed she is blessed, saying:

[3]{#Sir.24.3} “I came forth from the mouth of the Most High,

the first-born before all creatures.

I ordained that an unfailing light

should arise in the heavens,

and I covered the earth like a mist.

[4]{#Sir.24.4} I dwelt in high places,

and my throne was in a pillar of cloud.

[5]{#Sir.24.5} Alone I have made the circuit of the vault of heaven

and have walked in the depths of the abyss.

[6]{#Sir.24.6} In the waves of the sea, in the whole earth,

and in every people and nation I have gotten a possession.

[7]{#Sir.24.7} Among all these I sought a resting place;

I sought in whose territory I might lodge.

[8]{#Sir.24.8} “Then the Creator of all things gave me a commandment,

and the one who created me assigned a place for my tent.

And he said, ‘Make your dwelling in Jacob,

and in Israel receive your inheritance,

and among my chosen put down your roots.’

[9]{#Sir.24.9} From eternity, in the beginning, he created me,

and for eternity I shall not cease to exist.

[10]{#Sir.24.10} In the holy tabernacle I ministered before him,

and so I was established in Zion.

[11]{#Sir.24.11} In the beloved city likewise he gave me a resting place,

and in Jerusalem was my dominion.

[12]{#Sir.24.12} So I took root in an honored people,

in the portion of the Lord, who is their inheritance,

and my abode was in the full assembly of the saints.

[13]{#Sir.24.13} “I grew tall like a cedar in Lebanon,

and like a cypress on the heights of Hermon.

[14]{#Sir.24.14} I grew tall like a palm tree in En-ge'di,[^Sir-24-14b]

and like rose plants in Jericho;

like a beautiful olive tree in the field,

and like a plane tree I grew tall.

[15]{#Sir.24.15} Like cassia and camel’s thorn I gave forth the aroma of spices,

and like choice myrrh I spread a pleasant odor,

like galbanum, onycha, and stacte,

and like the fragrance of frankincense in the tabernacle.

[16]{#Sir.24.16} Like a terebinth I spread out my branches,

[17]{#Sir.24.17} Like a vine I caused loveliness to bud,

[19]{#Sir.24.19} “Come to me, you who desire me,

[20]{#Sir.24.20} For my teaching is sweeter than honey,

and my remembrance lasts throughout all generations.

[21]{#Sir.24.21} Those who eat me will hunger for more,

and those who drink me will thirst for more.

[22]{#Sir.24.22} Whoever obeys me will not be put to shame,

and those who work with my help will not sin.”

[23]{#Sir.24.23} All this is the book of the covenant of the Most High God,

the law which Moses commanded us

as an inheritance for the congregations of Jacob.[^Sir-24-23d]

[25]{#Sir.24.25} It fills men with wisdom, like the Pi'shon,

and like the Tigris at the time of the first fruits.

[26]{#Sir.24.26} It makes them full of understanding, like the Euphrates,

and like the Jordan at harvest time.

[27]{#Sir.24.27} It makes instruction shine forth like light,

like the Gi'hon at the time of vintage.

[28]{#Sir.24.28} Just as the first man did not know her perfectly,

the last one has not fathomed her;

[29]{#Sir.24.29} for her thought is more abundant than the sea,

and her counsel deeper than the great abyss.

[30]{#Sir.24.30} I went forth like a canal from a river

and like a water channel into a garden.

[31]{#Sir.24.31} I said, “I will water my orchard

and drench my garden plot”;

and behold, my canal became a river,

and my river became a sea.

[32]{#Sir.24.32} I will again make instruction shine forth like the dawn,

and I will make it shine afar;

[33]{#Sir.24.33} I will again pour out teaching like prophecy,

and leave it to all future generations.

[34]{#Sir.24.34} Observe that I have not labored for myself alone,

but for all who seek instruction.[^Sir-24-34e]

#### The Good and the Evil in Daily Life

## Sirach 25 {#Sir.25}

[1]{#Sir.25.1} My soul takes pleasure in three things,

and they are beautiful in the sight of the Lord and of men:[^Sir-25-1f]

agreement between brothers, friendship between neighbors,

and a wife and husband who live in harmony.

[2]{#Sir.25.2} My soul hates three kinds of men,

and I am greatly offended at their life:

a beggar who is proud, a rich man who is a liar,

and an adulterous old man who lacks good sense.

[3]{#Sir.25.3} You have gathered nothing in your youth;

how then can you find anything in your old age?

[4]{#Sir.25.4} What an attractive thing is judgment in gray-haired men,

and for the aged to possess good counsel!

[5]{#Sir.25.5} How attractive is wisdom in the aged,

and understanding and counsel in honorable men!

[6]{#Sir.25.6} Rich experience is the crown of the aged,

and their boast is the fear of the Lord.

[7]{#Sir.25.7} With nine thoughts I have gladdened my heart,

and a tenth I shall tell with my tongue:

a man rejoicing in his children;

a man who lives to see the downfall of his foes;

[8]{#Sir.25.8} happy is he who lives with an intelligent wife,

and he who has not made a slip with his tongue,

and he who has not served a man inferior to himself;

[9]{#Sir.25.9} happy is he who has gained good sense,

and he who speaks to attentive listeners.

[10]{#Sir.25.10} How great is he who has gained wisdom!

[11]{#Sir.25.11} The fear of the Lord surpasses everything;

to whom shall be likened the one who holds it fast?[^Sir-25-11g]

[13]{#Sir.25.13} Any wound, but not a wound of the heart!

[14]{#Sir.25.14} Any attack, but not an attack from those who hate!

[15]{#Sir.25.15} There is no venom[^Sir-25-15h] worse than a snake’s venom,[^Sir-25-15h]

and no wrath worse than an enemy’s wrath.

[16]{#Sir.25.16} I would rather dwell with a lion and a dragon

than dwell with an evil wife.

[17]{#Sir.25.17} The wickedness of a wife changes her appearance,

[18]{#Sir.25.18} Her husband takes his meals among the neighbors,

[19]{#Sir.25.19} Any iniquity is insignificant compared to a wife’s iniquity;

may a sinner’s lot befall her!

[20]{#Sir.25.20} A sandy ascent for the feet of the aged—

[21]{#Sir.25.21} Do not be ensnared by a woman’s beauty,

[22]{#Sir.25.22} There is wrath and impudence and great disgrace

when a wife supports her husband.

[23]{#Sir.25.23} A dejected mind, a gloomy face,

are caused by the wife who does not make her husband happy.

[24]{#Sir.25.24} From a woman sin had its beginning,

and because of her we all die.

[25]{#Sir.25.25} Allow no outlet to water,

and no boldness of speech in an evil wife.

[26]{#Sir.25.26} If she does not go as you direct,

separate her from yourself.

[29]{#Sir.26.29} A merchant can hardly keep from wrongdoing,

[24]{#Sir.27.24} I have hated many things, but none to be compared to him;

[26]{#Sir.27.26} He who digs a pit will fall into it,

[27]{#Sir.27.27} If a man does evil, it will roll back upon him,

[30]{#Sir.27.30} Anger and wrath, these also are abominations,

#### On Lending and Borrowing, Home, and Hospitality

## Sirach 29 {#Sir.29}

[1]{#Sir.29.1} He that shows mercy will lend to his neighbor,

and he that strengthens him with his hand keeps the commandments.

[2]{#Sir.29.2} Lend to your neighbor in the time of his need;

and in turn, repay your neighbor promptly.

[3]{#Sir.29.3} Confirm your word and keep faith with him,

and on every occasion you will find what you need.

[4]{#Sir.29.4} Many persons regard a loan as a windfall,

and cause trouble to those who help them.

[5]{#Sir.29.5} A man will kiss another’s hands until he gets a loan,

and will lower his voice in speaking of his neighbor’s money;

but at the time for repayment he will delay,

and will pay in words of unconcern,

and will find fault with the time.

[6]{#Sir.29.6} If the lender[^Sir-29-6t] exerts pressure, he will hardly get back half,

and will regard that as a windfall.

If he does not, the borrower[^Sir-29-6u] has robbed him of his money,

and he has needlessly made him his enemy;

he will repay him with curses and reproaches,

and instead of glory will repay him with dishonor.

[7]{#Sir.29.7} Because of such wickedness, therefore,[^Sir-29-7v] many have refused to lend;

they have been afraid of being defrauded needlessly.

[8]{#Sir.29.8} Nevertheless, be patient with a man in humble circumstances,

and do not make him wait for your alms.

[9]{#Sir.29.9} Help a poor man for the commandment’s sake,

and because of his need do not send him away empty.

[10]{#Sir.29.10} Lose your silver for the sake of a brother or a friend,

and do not let it rust under a stone and be lost.

[11]{#Sir.29.11} Lay up your treasure according to the commandments of the Most High,

and it will profit you more than gold.

[12]{#Sir.29.12} Store up almsgiving in your treasury,

and it will rescue you from all affliction;

[13]{#Sir.29.13} more than a mighty shield and more than a heavy spear,

it will fight on your behalf against your enemy.

[14]{#Sir.29.14} A good man will be surety for his neighbor,

but a man who has lost his sense of shame will fail him.

[15]{#Sir.29.15} Do not forget all the kindness of your surety,

for he has given his life for you.

[16]{#Sir.29.16} A sinner will overthrow the prosperity of his surety,

[17]{#Sir.29.17} and one who does not feel grateful will abandon his rescuer.

[18]{#Sir.29.18} Being surety has ruined many men who were prosperous,

and has shaken them like a wave of the sea;

it has driven men of power into exile,

and they have wandered among foreign nations.

[19]{#Sir.29.19} The sinner who has fallen into suretyship

and pursues gain will fall into lawsuits.

[20]{#Sir.29.20} Assist your neighbor according to your ability,

but take heed to yourself lest you fall.

[21]{#Sir.29.21} The essentials for life are water and bread

and clothing and a house to cover one’s nakedness.

[22]{#Sir.29.22} Better is the life of a poor man under the shelter of his roof

than sumptuous food in another man’s house.

[23]{#Sir.29.23} Be content with little or much.[^Sir-29-23w]

[24]{#Sir.29.24} It is a miserable life to go from house to house,

and where you are a stranger you may not open your mouth;

[25]{#Sir.29.25} you will play the host and provide drink without being thanked,

and besides this you will hear bitter words:

[26]{#Sir.29.26} “Come here, stranger, prepare the table,

and if you have anything at hand, let me have it to eat.”

[27]{#Sir.29.27} “Give place, stranger, to an honored person;

my brother has come to stay with me; I need my house.”

[28]{#Sir.29.28} These things are hard to bear for a man who has feeling:

scolding about lodging[^Sir-29-28x] and the reproach of the moneylender.

#### Discipline of Children, Right Attitudes

## Sirach 30 {#Sir.30}

[1]{#Sir.30.1} He who loves his son will whip him often,

in order that he may rejoice at the way he turns out.

[2]{#Sir.30.2} He who disciplines his son will profit by him,

[3]{#Sir.30.3} He who teaches his son will make his enemies envious,

[10]{#Sir.30.10} Do not laugh with him, lest you have sorrow with him,

[14]{#Sir.30.14} Better off is a poor man who is well and strong in constitution

[15]{#Sir.30.15} Health and soundness are better than all gold,

and a robust body than countless riches.

[16]{#Sir.30.16} There is no wealth better than health of body,

and there is no gladness above joy of heart.

[17]{#Sir.30.17} Death is better than a miserable life,

and eternal rest[^Sir-30-17b] than chronic sickness.

[18]{#Sir.30.18} Good things poured out upon a mouth that is closed

are like offerings of food placed upon a grave.

[19]{#Sir.30.19} Of what use to an idol is an offering of fruit?

So is he who is afflicted by the Lord;

[20]{#Sir.30.20} he sees with his eyes and groans,

like a eunuch who embraces a maiden and groans.

[21]{#Sir.30.21} Do not give yourself over to sorrow,

and do not afflict yourself deliberately.
