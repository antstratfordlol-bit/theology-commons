# Nehemiah — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### Neh 8–10 {#nt.Neh.8.1}

As it was only thirteen years since the reform of Ezra, some scholars think that this passage belongs to the book of Ezra.

### Neh 8:8 {#nt.Neh.8.8}

The book was of course written in Hebrew, but the people, since their sojourn in Babylonia, now spoke Aramaic, and it had to be translated for them into that tongue.
