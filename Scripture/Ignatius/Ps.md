# Psalms

#### The Two Ways

## Psalms 1 {#Ps.1}

[1]{#Ps.1.1} 
 Blessed is the man

who walks not in the counsel of the wicked,

nor stands in the way of sinners,

nor sits in the seat of scoffers;

[2]{#Ps.1.2} but his delight is in the law of the LORD,

and on his law he meditates day and night.

[3]{#Ps.1.3} He is like a tree

planted by streams of water,

that yields its fruit in its season,

and its leaf does not wither.

In all that he does, he prospers.

[4]{#Ps.1.4} The wicked are not so,

but are like chaff which the wind drives away.

[5]{#Ps.1.5} Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment,

nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous;

[6]{#Ps.1.6} for the LORD knows the way of the righteous,

but the way of the wicked will perish.

#### God’s Promise to His Anointed

## Psalms 2 {#Ps.2}

[1]{#Ps.2.1} 
 Why do the nations conspire

and the peoples plot in vain?

[2]{#Ps.2.2} The kings of the earth set themselves,

and the rulers take counsel together,

against the LORD and his anointed, saying,

[3]{#Ps.2.3} “Let us burst their bonds asunder,

and cast their cords from us.”

[4]{#Ps.2.4} He who sits in the heavens laughs;

the LORD has them in derision.

[5]{#Ps.2.5} Then he will speak to them in his wrath,

and terrify them in his fury, saying,

[6]{#Ps.2.6} “I have set my king

on Zion, my holy mountain.”

[7]{#Ps.2.7} I will tell of the decree of the LORD:

He said to me, “You are my son,

today I have begotten you.

[8]{#Ps.2.8} Ask of me, and I will make the nations your heritage,

and the ends of the earth your possession.

[9]{#Ps.2.9} You shall break them with a rod of iron,

and dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.”

[10]{#Ps.2.10} Now therefore, O kings, be wise;

be warned, O rulers of the earth.

[11]{#Ps.2.11} Serve the LORD with fear,

with trembling [12]{#Ps.2.12} rejoice,[^Ps-2-12a]

lest he be angry, and you perish in the way;

for his wrath is quickly kindled.

Blessed are all who take refuge in him.

## Psalms 10 {#Ps.10}

[1]{#Ps.10.1} Why do you stand afar off, O LORD?

Why do you hide yourself in times of trouble?

[2]{#Ps.10.2} In arrogance the wicked hotly pursue the poor;

let them be caught in the schemes which they have devised.

[3]{#Ps.10.3} For the wicked boasts of the desires of his heart,

and the man greedy for gain curses and renounces the LORD.

[4]{#Ps.10.4} In the pride of his countenance the wicked does not seek him;

all his thoughts are, “There is no God.”

[5]{#Ps.10.5} His ways prosper at all times;

your judgments are on high, out of his sight;

as for all his foes, he puffs at them.

[6]{#Ps.10.6} He thinks in his heart, “I shall not be moved;

throughout all generations I shall not meet adversity.”

[7]{#Ps.10.7} His mouth is filled with cursing and deceit and oppression;

under his tongue are mischief and iniquity.

[8]{#Ps.10.8} He sits in ambush in the villages;

in hiding places he murders the innocent.

His eyes stealthily watch for the hapless,

[9]{#Ps.10.9} he lurks in secret like a lion in his den;

he lurks that he may seize the poor,

he seizes the poor when he draws him into his net.

[10]{#Ps.10.10} The hapless is crushed, sinks down,

and falls by his might.

[11]{#Ps.10.11} He thinks in his heart, “God has forgotten,

he has hidden his face, he will never see it.”

[12]{#Ps.10.12} Arise, O LORD; O God, lift up your hand;

forget not the poor.

[13]{#Ps.10.13} Why does the wicked renounce God,

and say in his heart, “You will not call to account”?

[14]{#Ps.10.14} You see; yes, you note trouble and vexation,

that you may take it into your hands;

the hapless commits himself to you;

you have been the helper of the fatherless.

[15]{#Ps.10.15} Break the arm of the wicked and evildoer;

seek out his wickedness till you find none.

[16]{#Ps.10.16} The LORD is king for ever and ever;

the nations shall perish from his land.

[17]{#Ps.10.17} O LORD, you will hear the desire of the meek;

you will strengthen their heart, you will incline your ear

[18]{#Ps.10.18} to do justice to the fatherless and the oppressed,

so that man who is of the earth may strike terror no more.

## Psalms 18[17] {#Ps.18}
