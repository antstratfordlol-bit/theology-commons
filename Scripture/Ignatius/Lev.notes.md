# Leviticus — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### Lev 11:1 {#nt.Lev.11.1}

These regulations concerning what is ritually pure or impure depend, for the most part, on circumstances peculiar to that time; e.g., animals, such as the pig, used in pagan sacrifices. Some correspond to ancient taboos.

### Lev 17:1 {#nt.Lev.17.1}

The so-called “Code of Holiness” (chapters 17–26) implies an exalted idea of the holiness and otherness of the God of Israel.
