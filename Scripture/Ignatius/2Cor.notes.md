# 2 Corinthians — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### 2 Cor 1:8 {#nt.2Cor.1.8}

*affliction*: Possibly the disturbance at Ephesus (Acts 19:23–41), or perhaps a serious illness.

### 2 Cor 3:18 {#nt.2Cor.3.18}

Cleansed in baptism through the power of the Holy Spirit, our soul shines with the reflected glory of God.

### 2 Cor 4:7 {#nt.2Cor.4.6}

*this treasure*: i.e., the apostolate.

### 2 Cor 4:12 {#nt.2Cor.4.12}

i.e., we suffer, if necessary, even unto death, that you may have (spiritual) life.

### 2 Cor 5:19 {#nt.2Cor.5.19}

Or, “God was reconciling the world to himself through Christ.”

### 2 Cor 5:21 {#nt.2Cor.5.21}

*made him to be sin*: i.e., “sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and for sin, he condemned sin in the flesh” (Rom 8:3).

### 2 Cor 9:1 {#nt.2Cor.9.1}

*superfluous*: Yet Paul goes on to do so at some length, exhorting them to be generous.

### 2 Cor 10:1 {#nt.2Cor.10.1}

Paul is referring ironically to what some people are saying about him; see verse 10.

### 2 Cor 12:7 {#nt.2Cor.12.7}

*a thorn*: Perhaps some form of sickness or disability, or the opposition of Israel to his teaching.

### 2 Cor 12:13 {#nt.2Cor.12.13}

Paul ironically asks forgiveness for not being a charge on them as the other apostles were.
