# Lamentations — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### Lam 1:1 {#nt.Lam.1.1}

This book is traditionally ascribed to Jeremiah but is probably not all by him. It seems rather to have been composed by more than one author, though at about the same period of the siege and exile. The poems, written in the rhythm known as *qinah,* were probably composed for the liturgical services that continued to be held on the site of the temple.
