# Nahum — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### Nah 1:1 {#nt.Nah.1.1}

The opening words of the Book of Nahum announce the central theme—the coming destruction of Nineveh, the great oppressor of God’s people. The passion with which this lyric poetry is imbued is explained by the tyranny endured for so long by Israel. But the deliverance announced will not, unfortunately, be of long duration. The fall of Nineveh (612 B.C.) will be followed by the fall of Jerusalem, but that is not part of the prophet’s message.
