# 2 John

#### Salutation

## 2 John 1 {#2John.1}

[1]{#2John.1.1} The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in the truth, and not only I but also all who know the truth, [2]{#2John.1.2} because of the truth which abides in us and will be with us for ever:

[3]{#2John.1.3} Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us, from God the Father and from Jesus Christ the Father’s Son, in truth and love.

#### Truth and Love

[4]{#2John.1.4} I rejoiced greatly to find some of your children following the truth, just as we have been commanded by the Father. [5]{#2John.1.5} And now I beg you, lady, not as though I were writing you a new commandment, but the one we have had from the beginning, that we love one another. [6]{#2John.1.6} And this is love, that we follow his commandments; this is the commandment, as you have heard from the beginning, that you follow love. [7]{#2John.1.7} For many deceivers have gone out into the world, men who will not acknowledge the coming of Jesus Christ in the flesh; such a one is the deceiver and the antichrist. [8]{#2John.1.8} Look to yourselves, that you may not lose what you[^2John-1-8a] have worked for, but may win a full reward. [9]{#2John.1.9} Any one who goes ahead and does not abide in the doctrine of Christ does not have God; he who abides in the doctrine has both the Father and the Son. [10]{#2John.1.10} If any one comes to you and does not bring this doctrine, do not receive him into the house or give him any greeting; [11]{#2John.1.11} for he who greets him shares his wicked work.

#### Final Greetings

[12]{#2John.1.12} Though I have much to write to you, I would rather not use paper and ink, but I hope to come to see you and talk with you face to face, so that our joy may be complete.

[13]{#2John.1.13} The children of your elect sister greet you.

---

[^2John-1-8a]: 2John 1:8 — Other ancient authorities read *we*.
