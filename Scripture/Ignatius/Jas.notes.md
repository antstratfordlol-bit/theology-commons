# James — Notes

## Doctrinal notes

### Jas 1:1 {#nt.Jas.1.1}

*twelve tribes*: i.e., Jewish Christians outside Palestine.

### Jas 1:22 {#nt.Jas.1.22}

This is the main theme of the letter.

### Jas 2:1–7 {#nt.Jas.2.1}

These are hard words, but no harder than those of Jesus.

### Jas 2:10 {#nt.Jas.2.10}

In keeping the law, we must keep *the whole law*. We cannot pick and choose.

### Jas 2:14 {#nt.Jas.2.14}

Good works are necessary besides faith.

### Jas 5:3 {#nt.Jas.5.3}

The “treasure” they have laid up is described in the following verses.

### Jas 5:13–15 {#nt.Jas.5.13}

This passage is the scriptural basis for the sacrament of anointing the sick.
