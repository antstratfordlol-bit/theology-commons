# Philemon

## Philemon 1 {#Phlm.1}

#### Greeting

[1]{#Phlm.1.1} Paul, a prisoner for Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, To Philemon our beloved fellow worker [2]{#Phlm.1.2} and Apphia our sister and Archippus our fellow soldier, and the church in your house:

#### Thanksgiving

[3]{#Phlm.1.3} Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

[4]{#Phlm.1.4} I thank my God always when I remember you in my prayers, [5]{#Phlm.1.5} because I hear of your love and of the faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and all the saints, [6]{#Phlm.1.6} and I pray that the sharing of your faith may promote the knowledge of all the good that is ours in Christ. [7]{#Phlm.1.7} For I have derived much joy and comfort from your love, my brother, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you.

#### A plea on Onesimus’ behalf

[8]{#Phlm.1.8} Accordingly, though I am bold enough in Christ to command you to do what is required, [9]{#Phlm.1.9} yet for love's sake I prefer to appeal to you -- I, Paul, an ambassador and now a prisoner also for Christ Jesus -- [10]{#Phlm.1.10} I appeal to you for my child, Onesimus, whose father I have become in my imprisonment. [11]{#Phlm.1.11} (Formerly he was useless to you, but now he is indeed useful to you and to me.) [12]{#Phlm.1.12} I am sending him back to you, sending my very heart. [13]{#Phlm.1.13} I would have been glad to keep him with me, in order that he might serve me on your behalf during my imprisonment for the gospel; [14]{#Phlm.1.14} but I preferred to do nothing without your consent in order that your goodness might not be by compulsion but of your own free will.

[15]{#Phlm.1.15} Perhaps this is why he was parted from you for a while, that you might have him back for ever, [16]{#Phlm.1.16} no longer as a slave but more than a slave, as a beloved brother, especially to me but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord. [17]{#Phlm.1.17} So if you consider me your partner, receive him as you would receive me. [18]{#Phlm.1.18} If he has wronged you at all, or owes you anything, charge that to my account. [19]{#Phlm.1.19} I, Paul, write this with my own hand, I will repay it--to say nothing of your owing me even your own self. [20]{#Phlm.1.20} Yes, brother, I want some benefit from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in Christ.

[21]{#Phlm.1.21} Confident of your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even more than I say. [22]{#Phlm.1.22} At the same time, prepare a guest room for me, for I am hoping through your prayers to be granted to you.

#### Greetings and last requests

[23]{#Phlm.1.23} Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends greetings to you, [24]{#Phlm.1.24} and so do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.

[25]{#Phlm.1.25} The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
