CHRONOGRAPHY WRITTEN BY THE COMMAND OF CONSTANTINE, THE CHRIST-LOVING AND PORPHYROGENNETOS EMPEROR, SON OF LEO, THE MOST WISE EMPEROR AND AUTOCRAT
Beginning where Theophanes left off... for the Emperor Michael, son of Theophilus, the Curopalates, that is, from the reign of Leo the Armenian. The affairs of each detail were painstakingly collected by the same Emperor Constantine and set forth concisely, so that the things of the subsequent time might become clear... of the reign.
This, O most wise Emperor, is added to your many other great and magnificent qualities and virtues: that you strive to recall from the dead, as it were, and to bring back to a new life and rebirth the things that have slipped away and ceased to be with the passage of time, and that you pursue the benefit of history, and do not allow it to be neglected by you as it was by those who held the rudder of the empire before you. For, while we make no mention of those men who emulated that which no good man would ever emulate, there were some who, even though they excelled the common herd, contributed no effort toward this, because they were not educated in letters, but have been both a mockery and a disgrace of rusticity. For what is it fitting for such an emperor to cultivate, which brings no benefit to a man endowed with reason? Since it is far more fitting to have a care for men, and especially for that rational science by which the strength of the empire is secured, lest men, rushing headlong, little by little become brutalized, and lest that in which we excel all others be endangered by being consigned to silence.
