ORDER OF SUBJECTS CONTAINED IN THIS VOLUME
Maximus Planudes' Encomium on the saints and those deserving every praise, the chiefs of the apostles, Peter and Paul. — This very long homily, curtailed by half due to the defect of the codex, was edited by Gretser under the name of Gregory of Nyssa at Ingolstadt in the year 1620. It now appears complete as translated by us from the Coislinian codex 1187, up to the place where the edition of Gretser begins. 1017
Maximus Planudes' interpretation of certain chapters of St. Augustine from the fifteenth book *De Trinitate*, which prove that the Holy Spirit proceeds also from the Son. 1111
Maximus Planudes' syllogisms regarding the procession of the Holy Spirit against the Latins. — See vol. CLXI, col. 309, where they are edited along with the refutations of Bessarion and Demetrius Cydones. — "One might wonder," says Cydones there, "at this man, who, for these few and weak arguments, attempts to overturn the book of St. Augustine, on which he labored much while translating it into the Greek tongue. For if he undertook that labor wishing to harm those who might read it, he is truly absurd and deserves much reproach among pious men for acting as a harm to the souls of others; but if, knowing it would be useful to those who might encounter it and wishing to please our own people, he chose to translate it, why then, as if led by repentance, does he exhort them, as if it were a bane, to beware of what he translated for the usefulness of the readers?" However, the cause of this contradiction is the terror of him who was ruling at that time, whom he knew would be inexorable to those who assert that the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son, and by these arguments he wished to free himself from prison and chains. 1129
Appendix. — From an uncertain author, from Augustine on John, concerning the procession of the Holy Spirit. 1151
Fragment of a book ascribed to St. Augustine, *On the Knowledge of the True Life*, translated into Greek. 1157
Maximus Planudes' epistle to Manuel Philes. Attached is a Catalog of the Epistles of Planudes from the Vatican codex. 1165
Maximus Planudes' *Comparison of Winter and Spring*, from the Greek Parisian codex, noted as no. 1187, now edited for the first time. 1169
Maximus Planudes' iambic verses on the Saints Marcian and Martyrius and on the Church of St. Andrew. 1175
Indices to the Ecclesiastical History of Nicephorus Callistus. 1177
