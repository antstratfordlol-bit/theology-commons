Eustathius Cpolitanus (a), monachus S. Flori (b), sive illius monasterii quod ædi SS. Flori et Lauri in urbe Cpoli vicinum erat. Deinde libellorum supplicum magister, magister rhetorum (d), hinc Magnæ Ecclesiæ Cpol. diaconus (e), et ex designato Myrensis Ecclesiæ episcopo archiepiscopus, Thessalonicensis, insigni virtutis et
S. Florum sitæ? In fine hujus expositionis noto, Eustathius Cpoli eruditum fuisse, et quidem in monasterio S. Euphemiæ. Videatur ejus epistola 30: Εἴη καὶ τοὺς καλούς μου ποτε συντρόφους, τοὺς τῆς εὐεργετιδάς μου ἁγίας Εὐφημίας κληρικούς.

IN THE YEAR OF THE LORD 1192: EUSTATHIUS, METROPOLITAN OF THESSALONICA — NOTICE
(Fabric. Biblioth. Gr. ed. Harles, t. XI, p. 282. — Subjiciuntur notae quas addidit Opusculorum Eustathii editor, D. Theoph. Lucas Fridericus Tafel in libro De Thessalonica ejusque agro, p. 351).
Eustathius Cpolitanus (a), monk of Saint Florus (b), or of that monastery which was near the house of the Holy Florus and Laurus in the city of Constantinople. For so I prefer, with Bauduri, to interpret the words regarding *the one at Florus*, in Demetrius Chomatenus, rather than with Du Cange, as *deacon at the house of Florus* (c). Then [he was] master of the petitions, master of rhetoricians (d), hence deacon of the Great Church of Constantinople (e), and, having been designated bishop of the church of Myra, [he became] archbishop, [namely] of Thessalonica, of distinguished virtue and [learning].
