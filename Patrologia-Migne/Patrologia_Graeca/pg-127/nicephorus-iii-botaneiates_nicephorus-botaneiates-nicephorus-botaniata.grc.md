NICEPHORUS BOTANIATES
AUREA BULLA
Confirming the synodal decrees concerning incestuous marriages and betrothals, made under John Xiphilinus, the most holy and ecumenical patriarch, after whose death the following golden bull was offered to the Church.
It is confirmed by the divine and wise Word that God is always present in the greatest administrations, and we all know and do not doubt that the greatest affairs that are conducted are brought to completion in this way. If, then, among the greatest works of God is also human life, and if the procreation of these and their growth into this life depends upon His own dispensation, then much more is marriage recognized as resulting from His care and command—that marriage which is lawful according to the laws and not separated from God; just as it is alien and pagan, that which retreats from and is divided from His commandment. For if He Himself clearly declared and proclaimed that those whom God hath joined together let no man put asunder, as the evangelical and divine voice says, who would deny that according to His will incestuous marriages are prohibited, and that those marriages have a place according to His verdict which are contracted by mutual consent in accordance with the sacred and ecclesiastical canons and are not forbidden? Or if anyone should contumaciously reject those things which are performed according to the prescription and excellent order of the sacred and synodal laws, would this not be openly to oppose God and to fall away from Him? But truly, lest anyone be so furious, and so far depart from his senses, as to wish to fall into the hands of a greater and inexorable judge, by opposing what is good and declining toward evil—therefore, if the divine law and the synodal law compel even those who are unwilling to be brought to better things, lest they be cast out by the holy sentence of the synod, it is much more worthwhile for the prince himself, along with the synodal and pontifical suffrage, to also decree and to present himself as, so to speak, a partner and assistant
to prevail and to deliberate with those who have been rightly and well ordained and administered by the most holy and divine high priests and by the all-holy patriarch himself. For since, by a synodal judgment of the blessed patriarch, Lord John Xiphilinus, promulgated in the month of April of the past fourth indiction, and also by the metropolitan bishops and archbishops then present at the queen of cities, my Majesty learned that in that synod it was determined concerning incestuous marriages, in what way those forbidden by the sacred canons and laws ought to be restrained, and in what way those taking place according to the laws, and not synodically forbidden, ought to be affirmed, and as indeed those co-operating in these matters, well decreed by the most holy and divine high priests and the all-holy patriarch himself, were reminded of this, my Majesty was moved to confirm such a judgment, so that it might not be overturned by anyone by reason of fear, or evil deceit, or any other non-laudable method; and since the matter is divine and pious, it heard the petition of those requesting it, and royally ratified things pleasing to God, and in accordance with this, also joined in opinion with the holy and sacred synod, so that matters well performed of old might not be newly subverted for the worse, nor things immovable be moved from their place, and become, if I may so say, profane. For my Majesty decrees by this present golden bull that the patriarchical and synodal judgment that has taken place be firm, and that no one dare to call it into question or rescind it even by a mere word. For he who opposes this holy and synodal sentence immediately overturns the rudders of our faith, and is rendered liable to anathema, and is removed and cast out from the great Church, as one inhuman and wicked, who opposes the canons and rescinds and tears them up. Indeed, my Majesty, sealing what was well adjudged and performed by the synod itself, has promulgated the present Golden Bull for the confirmation and stabilization of those matters, and has subscribed to this bull with red letters, and through them dictating a clearer truth, has appended to it a true seal, as a sort of golden corollary, in the year of the world 6598, in this same third indiction, in the month of January, in which also our pious and divinely established Majesty has subscribed.
