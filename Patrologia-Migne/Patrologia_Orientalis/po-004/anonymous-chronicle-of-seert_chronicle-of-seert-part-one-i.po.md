When Sapor returned from the land of the Romans, taking captives and booty, the kings his adversaries who lived on the most remote frontiers envied him and resolved to go and fight him. He then sent to Ouliâhin (?), king of the Kafrathnatan, diamonds and very beautiful clothes such as were not found in his kingdom. He also sent him three hundred virgin young girls, from those he had taken captive from the Romans. He chose them, clothed them in magnificent garments and diamonds, and had them accompanied by ambassadors. He ordered them to take care of them during the journey and to bathe every three days, for fear that their color might change. When they were on the road and learned that they were being sent as presents to the barbarian king, they preferred death to life and deliberated how they would escape the corruption of this king. His father, called Gregory, belonged to the sect of the Samaritans. His mother, who was called Nonna (Younä), was a Christian. When she was pregnant, she vowed to send her child to the monastery if it were a boy and if her husband converted to the Christian religion. This was during the meeting of the three hundred and eighteen bishops. His father had a dream in which he believed he was reciting the psalm that begins with these words: hedith kâdh amrin, that is to say: I rejoiced when it was said to me that we would go into the house of the Lord || was frightened by it. But he made a mistake and said: "You govern the Church of God without failing," using the formula for the ordination of bishops. When the priest had finished, he saw the neophyte enveloped in a brilliant light and he predicted that he would one day be a bishop. Indeed, he was named bishop of Nazianzus (Anzianz) towards the end of his life: he was of eminent virtue and knew all the sciences of the Greeks.
