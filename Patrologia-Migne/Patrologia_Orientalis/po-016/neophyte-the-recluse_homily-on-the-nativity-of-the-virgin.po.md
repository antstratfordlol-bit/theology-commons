The parents of this Daughter of God were indeed Joachim and Anna, of the lineage of King David, of the tribe of Judah, guardians of the law and accurate observers of the divine commandments, abounding in riches and wealthier in divinely infused virtue, but bereft of offspring because of the bonds of blessed Anna's sterility. Whence they often received reproach from the sons of Israel; they offered double gifts to God, but even the priests who received them dared to revile them because they had no seed in Israel. Wherefore the righteous Joachim, being vehemently saddened and separating himself from all human intercourse, withdrew into the desert, where, fasting for forty days, he besought the Lord in weeping and fasting, saying: "Take away from me, O Lord, take away from me the reproach and contempt of sterility; and may you be pleased, O Lord, to give a comely seed, and I, in turn, will return it, O Lord, to your goodness."
