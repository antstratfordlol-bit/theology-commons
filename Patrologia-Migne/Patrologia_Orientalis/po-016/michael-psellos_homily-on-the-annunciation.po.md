But the Virgin, just as she had immediately suspected the greeting above and had been troubled, so here, after the angel explained everything accurately, and recalled the prophecies to memory, and recounted the descent of the Word, and announced the ineffable conception, and confirmed her preparation and sanctification as having been effected by the Father and the Spirit, perceiving the divine grace in his words, she receives the messenger with a calm mind, and as a handmaid submits herself to the Lord for the accomplishment of the mystery, and stands ready for the ministry; and would that the first mother, Eve, had shown such diligence at the promise of the serpent! For if she had questioned him as to who he was who spoke such things to her, and how, being an irrational beast, he nevertheless emitted a human voice, and whence he had this knowledge about deification, and how, endowed with such knowledge, he had erred regarding the first voice; if the first mother had diligently inquired into all these things at the serpent's unusual conversation, the serpent would surely have retreated in shame, or rather, he who through the serpent had vomited the poison of death against the first mother.
