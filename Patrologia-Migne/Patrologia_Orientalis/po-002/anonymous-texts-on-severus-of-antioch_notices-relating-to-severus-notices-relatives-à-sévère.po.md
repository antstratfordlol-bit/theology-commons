! The ordination (cheirotonia) of Patriarch Mar Severus took place [on November 8], indiction VI, in the year 823 of Alexander, and he sat on the throne (thronos) of Antioch for six years. May Our Lord grant us a good end that pleases Him, through the intercession of His mother and His saints! Amen.

Patriarch Severus is from Pisidia, and his (native) city is Sozopolis. He was ordained on November 8 of the year 820, which is the year 509 of Our Lord. He was driven out after six years. He died in Alexandria twenty-three years later, on Saturday, February 8, of the year 849.

Saint Mar Severus, Patriarch of Antioch, was Pisidian by origin (genos). His (native city was) Sozopolis. His ancestors were among the great men of the city. Before his time, there were many prophecies about him. Thus, someone by the name of Menas prophesied that he would rise over the whole earth like a cloud and that he would water the whole world with the streams of his teaching. - Another famous man saw him in a dream holding a spade in his hand and cleaning a fountain full of mire and stench; [he declared that] he would be illustrious among the bishops. - These (predictions) and even greater ones were realized when he received the seat and the dignity of patriarch of the Church of Antioch. His ordination (cheirotonia) took place on November 8 in the year 820, which is the year 508 after the arrival of Our Lord. He was driven from his seat six years after his ordination. He died and was buried in Alexandria after having lived twenty-three years in exile (exoria), on Saturday, February 8, in the year 849. - (The name) Severus is interpreted "(who) drives away
