Take care, therefore, my dear friend, to observe these written commandments, so that you may be saved with the saints who have observed the precepts of Our Lord Jesus Christ. But, if someone reads them and does not observe them, he resembles him who sees his face in a mirror and who immediately forgets what he was like. 73. But if someone reads these things and observes them, he resembles the good seed that is sown in good soil and that bears fruit. God can make it so that we are found among those who listen and who observe, so that He may also receive from us the fruit (of our labors) safe and sound by His grace, for to Him is the strength, the glory, and the power in the ages of ages.
