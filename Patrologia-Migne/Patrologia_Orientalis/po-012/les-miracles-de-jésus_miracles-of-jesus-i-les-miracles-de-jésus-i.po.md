1 The Magi came to Bethlehem. The Magi Kings who were in the East saw a star in the sky. Its light was like the light of the sun. The light of this star was like a column that reached from the sky to the earth. There was in the center of the star the image of the Virgin. When the Magi had seen this, they were greatly astonished. They consulted their books and made the calculation (astronomical of this star). They did not find among * the stars any star that resembled it. They found all the stars, each in its place. They found the Fâlêk solid as usual. They said to each other: "We must return to consult the Book of the Commandment of the Fathers in the King's Treasures. If we find in it the mention of this star, we will do everything it commands us. But if we do not find the mention of it, (nothing) is (to be done), for (the star) is (then) a symbol."
