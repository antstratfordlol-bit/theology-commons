That there is a set place and a cleansing fire, as we have already said, in the world to come, is shown first of all from the second book of Maccabees, chapter 12, where it is said: 'It is therefore a holy and wholesome thought to pray for the dead, that they may be loosed from sins.' It is not necessary to pray for those who are in paradise, because they have no need of anything, nor for those who are in hell, since they cannot be loosed or cleansed from their sins. There are therefore some after this life not yet loosed from their sins, who can be loosed and cleansed.
