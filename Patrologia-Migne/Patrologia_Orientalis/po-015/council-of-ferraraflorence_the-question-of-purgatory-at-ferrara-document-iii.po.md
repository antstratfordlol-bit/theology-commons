The same, in the same discourse: "One must not overlook the supplications for the spirits of the dead, which ought to be made for all who have fallen asleep in the Christian communion, even if their names are not mentioned, the Church has received them by a general commemoration, so that for those who do not have parents or children or relatives or friends to perform these things, they might be provided by one common mother. But if such supplications, made with right faith and piety for those who have fallen asleep, are lacking, I think that it will not benefit their spirits at all, in whatever holy places their lifeless bodies may be deposited."
