I beseech you, however, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all say the same thing, and that there be no schisms among you, so that you may safely guard our true and ancestral faith as an excellent deposit, adding nothing, taking away nothing: for we have not had a deficient faith until now, nor did we need a synod or definition to learn new things, we who are both sons and disciples of the ecumenical synods and of those Fathers who participated in them or flourished in subsequent times. This is our glory, our faith, the most excellent inheritance of our fathers. Instructed by this, we hope that we shall stand before God and obtain remission of the faults we have committed; but if we lack this, I do not know by what justice we shall be able to be freed from eternal punishment. Whoever shall attempt to shake this from us, and to substitute another more new one in its place, he, even if he were an angel from heaven, let him be anathema; let him clearly fall from the memory of both God and men. No one rules over our faith, neither emperor, nor pontiff, nor false synod, nor anyone else, except one God, who Himself delivered it to us by Himself and by His disciples.
