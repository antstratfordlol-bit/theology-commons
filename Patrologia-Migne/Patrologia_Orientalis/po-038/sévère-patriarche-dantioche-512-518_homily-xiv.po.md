ܘܠܐ ܡܕܡ ܓܝܪ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܕܡܛܢܫ ܠܗܠܝܢ ܩܕܝ̈ܫܬܐ ܇ ܐܠܐ ܐܢ ܚܛܝܬܐ ܒܠܚܘܕ. ܘܥܕܠܝܐ ܕܡܢ ܬܪ̈ܥܝܬܐ ܕܝܠܗ ܇ ܗܘ ܕܡ̈ܥܓܠܢ ܠܢ ܒܝܕܝ̈ܗܝܢ ܗܠܝܢ ܩܕܝ̈ܫܬܐ.

### HOMILY XIV
ܡܐܡܪ̈ܐ ܕܐܪ̈ܒܥܬܥܣܪ ܕܐܬܬܡܖܪ ܒܕܘܟܪ̈ܢܗ̇ ܕܩܕܝܫܬܐ ܝܿܠܕܬ̇ ܐܠܗܐ ܘܒܬܘܠܬܐ ܒܟܠܙܒܢ ܡܪܝܡ ܐ. ܦܐܝܬܐ ܡܢ ܐܝܬܝܗ̇ ܘܚܣܝܬܐ ܆ ܕܠܟܠܗܘܢ ܩܕܝ̈ܫܐ ܢܩܖ̈ܒ ܬܫܒ̣̈ܚܬܐ ܕܡܢ ܡ̈ܠܐ. ܢܝܩܪ ܕܝܢ ܠܗܠܝܢ ܒܡܐܡܪ̈ܐ ܡܩ̈ܠܣܢܐ ܘܒܚ̈ܓܐ ܆ ܐܝܟ ܀ ܕܠܐܝܠܝܢ ܕܫܪ̈ܝܪ̈ܐܝܬ ܫܡܫܘ ܠܡܪ̈ܐ ܕܝܠܗܘܢ ܇ ܘܠܘܬ ܡܕܒܪ̈ܢܘܬܐ ܕܦܘܪ̈ܩܝܐ ܕܝܠܢ ܡܗܝܡܢܐܝܬ ܕܝܿܠܘ. ܘܠܢܒ̈ܝܐ ܡܶܢ ܢܩܠܣ ܆ ܐܝܟ ܕܠܐܝܠܝܢ ܕܣܦܩܐܝܬ ܒܝܕ ܡܝܬܪ̈ܘܬܐ ܒܝܬܝܬܐ ܐܬܚܼܙܝܘ ܇ ܕܢܩ̇ܕܡܘܢ ܢܐܡܪܘܢ ܠܪܐܙܐ ܪܒܐ ܕܫܦܝܪ̈ܘܬ ܕܚܼܠܬܐ. ܠܫܠܝ̈ܚܝܐ ܕܝܢ ܆ ܐܝܟ ܕܠܐܝܠܝܢ ܕܕܠܗܢܐ ܐܟܪܙܘ. ܠܣܗ̈ܕܐ ܕܝܢ ܆ ܐܝܟ ܕܠܐܝܠܝܢ ܕܠܢܒ̈ܝܘܬܐ ܡܶܢ ܕܗܿܢܘܢ ܇ ܠܟܪ̈ܘܙܘܬܐ ܕܝܢ ܕܗܠܝܢ ܆ ܒܝܕ ܕܡܐ ܕܝܠܗܘܢ ܫ̇ܪܪ̈ܘ. ܡܢ ܗܪ̈ܟܐ ܐܦ ܠܗ ܠܟܘܢܝܐ ܗܢܐ ܆ ܡܢܗ ܕܫܪܖ̈ܪܐ ܢܣܼܒܘ. ܣܗ̈ܕܐ ܓܝܪ ܐܝܬ ܥܝܕܐ ܠܡܩܪ̈ܐ ܇ ܠܗ̇ܿܢܘܢ ܕܠܣܘܥܪ̈ܢܐ ܠܐ ܡܬܚܘ̈ܝܢܐ ܐܘ ܕܐܚܪ̈ܢܝܐܝܬ ܐܝܟܢ ܕܗܼܘ ܠܐ ܡܬܗܝܡܢܝܢ ܇ ܡܬܗܝܡܢܢܘܬܐ ܝ̇ܲܗܒܝܢ
Pronounced in memory of the holy Mother of God and ever-Virgin Mary 1 It is beautiful and worthy that we offer words of praise to all the saints: let us therefore honor them with eulogistic discourses and solemnities, as people who have truly served their Master and who have faithfully contributed to the Economy of our salvation. Let us praise first the prophets as those who by their own perfection appeared sufficient to proclaim in advance the great mystery of piety; then, that of the apostles, as those who announced this mystery; of the martyrs finally, as those who by their own blood confirmed the prophecies of the former and the preaching of the latter; that is why, for having thus confirmed (the words of one and the other), they have also received this name, for it is the custom to call "martyrs" witnesses, those who by their word give credence to facts that cannot be demonstrated or that are not objects of faith, in any way whatsoever.
ܒܝܕ ܒܪܬ ܩܠܐ ܕܝܠܗܘܢ ܀ ܒܪܬ ܩܠܐ ܕܝܢ ܕܣܗ̈ܕܐ ܕܡܫܝܚܐ ܐܝܬܝܗ̇ ܆ ܡܫܬܿܦܥܢܘܬܐ ܕܕܡܐ ܕܝܠܗܘܢ ܇ ܗܺܝ ܕܢܩܼܦܬ̇ ܠܡܫܬܿܦܥܢܘܬܐ ܩܕܡܝܬܐ ܘܐܠܗܝܬܐ ܇ ܕܕܡܐ ܗܘ ܕܐܫܼܕ ܚܠܦܝܢ ܐܡܪܗ ܕܐܠܗܐ ܗܘ ܕܫܿܩܠ ܚܛܝܼܬܗ ܕܥܠܡܐ ܇ ܗܿܘ ܕܐܣܼܗܕ ܗܼܘ ܠܢܦܫܗ ܩܕܡ ܗܠܝܢ ܐܚܪ̈ܢܐ. ܐܦ ܓܝܪ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܐܦ ܒܗܕܐ ܠܐ ܣܢܝܩܐ ܇ ܗ̇ܘ ܕܐܣܼܗܕ ܩܕܡ ܦܘܢܛܝܘܣ ܦܝܠܐܛܘܣ ܬܘܕܝܬܐ ܫܦܝܪܖܪܬܐ ܇ ܐܝܟܢܐ ܕܫܠܝܚܐ ܦܘܠܘܣ ܐܡܪܿ. ܘܛܒ ܟܐܢܐܝܬ. ܥܠ ܡܢܘ ܓܝܪ ܐܚܪ̈ܢܐ ܣܗܕܐ ܕܝܬܝܪ̈ ܡܗܝܼܡܢ ܛܒ ܡܢܗ ܇ ܥܬܝܕ ܗܘܐ ܠܡܣܬܼܢܩܘ ܗܘ ܕܗܼܘ ܫܪܪ̈ܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ. ܐܠܐ ܟܕ ܠܫܦܝܪܘܬ ܬܪ̈ܥܝܬܐ ܕܨܒܝܢܐ
2 Now the word of the martyrs of Christ is the shedding of their blood, which followed the first and divine shedding of the blood poured out for us by the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world who testified for himself before others. Now he had no need of it, he who testified a beautiful confession before Pontius Pilate just as the apostle Paul says, and very rightly; for what other witness who is much more faithful than him, could he have needed, he who is himself the Truth? However, by accepting the generosity of his servants, he associated them with this title which is his own and had them called martyrs.

4 Now who is the prophetess mentioned in the divine books, who bore a son called "Pillage quickly and plunder rapidly", a son who, as soon as he is born and before he knows how to say papa and mama, pillages the enemies and plunders them—(who is she), if not the Mother of God, the Virgin who bore Emmanuel, who, from the beginnings of his birth according to the flesh, has overthrown the Calumniator, by taking the power of Damascus and by carrying off the plunder of Samaria?

5 These words designate idolatry symbolically by a kind of antonomasia: in effect Damascus translates as "bloodthirsty", and Samaria is the city that manufactured the golden calves and made the worship in truth and the adoration of the one who is the only God disappear among its (inhabitants); it is quite evident to all that it is by these two facts that idolatry is recognized, in that we name and adore as gods (idols) made by the hands (of men) and in that we offer sacrifices through the intermediary of blood and holocausts. It is of this therefore that Emmanuel, as soon as he is born according to the flesh, takes the booty, when, on the one hand, he attracted the magi to come to adore him in swaddling clothes at the age of his infancy (and that) on the other hand, he went into Egypt because of the massacre of the children by Herod and that he shook his idols made by the hands (of men) as Isaiah also prophesies. Now he did these things and took this booty before the king of the Assyrians, now the prophecies call the Calumniator the king of the Assyrians in many passages and one can find so to speak everywhere among the prophets that he is thus nicknamed.

18 This is why we also honor the holy Mother of God and ever-Virgin Mary with honors that are most remarkable, as the one who finds herself capable, more than all the other saints, of raising prayers for us, (as the one) also whom we boast of having obtained as the ornament of our race: Rational Earth, from which the second Adam, who is neither fashioned nor created, formed himself according to the flesh; Virginal Plant, from which Christ, the heavenly ladder, was formed according to the flesh by the Spirit, so that we ourselves, by strengthening our steps on his, may be able to ascend to the heavens; Spiritual Mountain of Sinai, which is not in darkness, but which shines with the sun of justice and which grants not only the Law of the ten commandments, but the legislator himself when he appeared on earth and conversed with men; and it is not one people, nor Israel alone, but every people and every race that he instructs by the gospel and captivates by persuasion.

19 What honor shall we therefore render to the Mother of God, or rather to God who became incarnate from her for our salvation? For it is there that he finds honor, sacrifice, and holocaust. For how is it not beautiful that, by his coming in the flesh, the earth has become heaven, so that even the angels also dwell on this (earth), according to what he himself also said in the gospel: Truly, I say to you, henceforth you will see the heavens opened and the angels of God ascending and descending.

20 But we, who were bound to show a way of life worthy of the heavens, do we not do even what is fitting for the earth, but (only) what is fitting for Sheol and the abyss of perdition? While we should apply ourselves to virginity and observe it because of God who was born of a virgin, we do not even restrain in a chaste marriage the lusts that the cross of Christ has blunted and made easy to overcome, since it has blunted the sin which is the sting of death; but we dishonor the temple of God by fornication, and we become horses that neigh after the females as a prophet says.
