1 OF SAINT SEVERUS ARCHBISHOP OF ANTIOCH AND PATRIARCH HOMILY | On himself, after he was promoted from the beginning and came to the pontificate and the apostolic see of the Church of the Antiochians. And that one must confess Christ of two natures, one Lord, one Son, and not two natures after the ineffable union. And against the atheistic opinion of Eutyches. And it was pronounced in the martyrium of the holy martyr Romanos.

2 Formerly, after having departed toward Mesopotamia, the patriarch Jacob, admirable for his virtues, having traveled part of the way, (...) evening (...): for the fatigue of the journey invited him to it. Having fallen asleep, in a vision he perceived a great ladder that rose to heaven; its foot rested on the earth, while its head reached heaven. And the Lord was leaning on the top, as the holy Scripture has said.

3 Now the blessed Jacob, after having been judged worthy of this vision and having profited from this great grace, understood by this revelation the mystery full of wonders that had been figured from the beginning.

4 He took a stone, placed it as a stele with a view to honor, - for it was holy -, and for the glory of God who had explained the vision to him; he did not stop there only, but he poured oil on the stone, then spoke thus: This is the house of God and this is the gate of heaven.
ϩⲓⲧⲛ ⲛⲉⲓϣⲁϫⲉ ϭⲉ ⲛⲧⲉⲓⲙⲓⲛⲉ ⲥⲭⲉⲇⲱⲛ ϩⲱⲥ ⲉϥⲁϣⲕⲁⲕ ⲉ̅ⲃⲟⲗ ⲉϥϫⲱ ⲙ̅ⲙⲟⲥ. ϫⲉ ϩⲟⲧⲁⲛ ⲉϥϣⲁⲛⲣ̀ⲟⲩⲟⲉ̅ⲓⲛ ⲉⲛⲉⲧⲟⲩⲏϩ ϩⲓϫⲙ̅ ⲡⲕⲁϩ ⲛ̅ϭⲓ ⲡⲣⲏ ⲛ̅ⲧⲇⲓⲕⲁⲓⲟⲥⲩⲛⲏ ⲡⲗⲟⲅⲟⲥ ⲙ̅ⲡⲛⲟⲩⲧⲉ ⲡⲉⲓⲱⲧ. ⲡⲱⲛⲉ ⲛ̅ⲕⲟⲟϩ ⲛ̅ⲥⲱⲧ̅ⲡ̅ ⲉⲧⲧⲁⲉⲓⲏⲩ ⲕⲁⲧⲁ ⲧⲉⲥⲙⲏ ⲛⲏⲥⲁⲓⲁⲥ. ⲡⲉⲛⲧⲁⲩⲧⲁϩⲥ̅ϥ̅ ⲙ̅ⲡⲛⲉϩ ⲙ̅ⲡⲧⲉⲗⲏⲗ ⲡⲁⲣⲁ ⲛⲉⲧϩⲓⲧⲟⲩⲱϥ. ⲁⲩⲱ ⲛ̅ϥ̅ⲡⲁϩⲧϥ̅ ⲉ̅ⲃⲟⲗ ⲙ̅ⲙⲓⲛ ⲙ̅ⲙⲟϥ ⲉⲛϥⲣⲥⲁⲣ̅ⲝ̅ ⲉⲧⲃⲏⲏⲧⲛ̅. ⲧⲟⲧⲉ ϥⲛⲁϣⲱⲡⲉ ⲛⲁⲛ ⲛ̅ϭⲗⲟⲟϭⲉ. ⲛ̅ϥϫⲓⲧ̅ⲛ̅ ⲉϩⲣⲁⲓ ⲉⲧⲡⲉ ⲁⲛⲟⲛ ⲛⲉⲧⲛⲏϫ ⲉⲃⲟⲗ ϩⲓϫⲙ̅ ⲡⲕⲁϩ· ϩⲓⲧⲙ̅ ⲡⲛⲟⲃⲉ ⲛ̅ⲧⲡⲁⲣⲁⲃⲁⲥⲓⲥ ⲛⲁⲇⲁⲙ. ⲁⲩⲱ
5 With such words, it is just if he cries out: When the sun of justice, the Word of God the Father, the chosen and precious cornerstone, according to the word of Isaiah, shines upon the inhabitants of the earth, He who was anointed with the oil of gladness in preference to those of his entourage and who humbled himself and became incarnate for us, then He will be for us a ladder; and He will make us ascend to heaven, we who lie on the earth through the sin of Adam's transgression; He will be for us the gate of heaven:

6 He will reveal to us the glory of His Father, and will show Himself to us with the Holy Spirit, will teach us the one Lordship and the one Divinity in the holy Trinity leaning on the ladder, which is our Savior Jesus Christ, He by whom those of heaven are reunited to those who are on earth 6... (He will reveal) the Father and Himself and the Holy Spirit and one single Lordship in the holy Trinity which was leaning on the ladder, by which what is on earth was reunited to what is in heaven.

13 But having become flesh and man, and having continued equally to be God without change, he, this same Word of God, tasted death for us, after he became flesh for us; and he continued to be immortal, because he is immortal according to his nature. Thus, he who first will confess with an upright heart, a firm thought and an immutable faith, that the Word of God became flesh in a flesh belonging to the same essence as us, in the way I spoke of above, will confess again that the same is truly God and man; he will attribute to the same individual the sufferings and the wonders that befit divinity and humanity, the cross, the tomb, the resurrection, and immortality. 32 Let us give glory to Him who dwells in the heavens and who came to earth for us, who has ascended again to the heavens, who fills all places and governs them all, by abandoning the cares of the earth, by occupying ourselves with the things of heaven, seeking the things of heaven, where Christ dwells, seated at the right hand of the Father. There is our true homeland, from which we have fallen, which has been restored to us by grace through the love for humanity of Our Lord, who became man for us, until he restored us to our first dignity. Indeed, all the works of this life differ in no way from dreams or shadows that deceive those who are in pleasure just as those who are in the theater.

33 Let us therefore pass beyond all that and pursue the things of heaven with eagerness, like those who run in the stadium, before all that passes beyond us, for the form of this world will pass away, according to the word of Paul.
