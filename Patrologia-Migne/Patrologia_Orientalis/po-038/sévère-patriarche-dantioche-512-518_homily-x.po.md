### HOMILY X
ܡܐܡܪ̈ܐ ܕܥܣܪ̈ܐ ܥܠ ܢܘܗܪ̈ܐ
On the Epiphany
ܬܘܒ. ܥܐܕܐ ܕܐܠܗܐ ܘܦܪ̈ܘܩܐ ܝܫܘܥ ܡܫܝܚܐ ܆ ܘܬܘܒ ܬܘܪ̈ܨܐ ܕܓܢܣܐ ܕܝܠܢ. ܡܬܝܠܕ ܗܘܐ ܡܢ ܩܕܝܡ ܒܒܣܪ ܆ ܘܒܝܠܝܕܘܬܐ ܕܝܠܝ ܡܫܬܘܬܦ ܗܘܐ ܕܠܐ ܙܪܥܐ ܘܕܠܐ ܚܛܗ ܇ ܗ̇ܘ ܕܝܿܕܥ ܠܟܠܗܝܢ ܩܕܡ ܗ̈ܘܝܗܝܢ ܘܡܢ ܓܙܪ ܕܝܚܐ ܗܘ ܩܕܡܝܐ. ܡܫܬܪ̈ܝܢ ܗ̈ܘܝ ܐܡܼܗ̈ܬܐ ܇ ܗܝ ܕܫܕܪ̈ܬ̇ ܥܠܝܗܝܢ ܚܘܐ ܇ ܟܕ ܗܺܝ ܕܒܥܩ̈ܬܐ ܬܐܠܕܝܢ ܒܫ̈ܝܐ ܇ ܩܲܒܠܬ̇ ܡܪ̈ܫܘܬܐ ܕܚܠܦ ܛܥܡܬܐ ܡܪ̈ܝܪ̈ܬܐ ܘܠܐܼ ܡܸܫܬܡܥܢܘܬܐ. ܡܠܐܟܐ ܓܝܪ ܡܣ̇ܒܪ ܗܘܐ ܗܕܐ ܠܪ̈ܥܘܬܐ ܆ ܟܕ ܐܡܪ ܗܘܐ ܘܡܣ̇ܒܪ ܐܢܐ ܠܟܘܢ ܚܕܘܬܐ ܪ̈ܒܬܐ ܗܺܝ ܕܬܗܘܐ ܠܟܠܗ ܥܠܡܐ. ܐܬܝܠܕ ܠܟܘܢ ܝܘܡܢܐ ܦܪ̈ܘܩܐ ܇ ܗܘ ܕܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܡܫܝܚܐ ܡܪܝܐ ܒܡܕܝܢܬܗ ܕܕܘܝܕ. ܐܠܐ ܐܦ ܠܘܛܬܐ ܗܺܝ ܕܐܒܘܢ ܩܕܡܝܐ ܐܕܡ ܆ ܥܡ ܥܩܬܐ ܡܬܚܿܒܠܐ ܗܘܬ. ܐܦ ܓܝܪ ܫܶܡܥܐ ܗܘܬ. ܗ݈ܝ ܕܝ̇ܠܕܬ̇ ܐܠܗܐ ܒܬܘܠܬܐ ܆ ܥܡ ܗܺܝ ܕܚܕܝ ܡܠܝܬ̇ ܛܝܒܘܬܐ ܡܪ̈ܝܐ ܥܡܟܝ ܆ ܐܦ ܗܝ ܕܡܒܪ̈ܟܬܐ ܐܢܬܝ ܒܢ̈ܫܐ ܇
1 It is again the feast of the God and Savior Jesus Christ and it is again the straightening of our race. He was born for the first time according to the flesh, and he shared my way of being born, he who is without seed and without sin, he who knows all things before they exist, and mothers were delivered from the first sentence that Eve had launched against them, by receiving the (word): It is in pain that you will give birth to sons, as a reproach in response to the bitter taste and disobedience. Indeed the angel announced this to the shepherds, saying: I announce to you a great joy, which will be for the whole world: there is born to you today a Savior, who is Christ the Lord, in the city of David. But with the pain was destroyed also the curse of our first father Adam. Indeed the Mother of God, the Virgin, at the same time as these (words): Rejoice, full of grace; the Lord is with you, also heard: You are blessed among women, and the fruit of your womb is blessed; now the blessing suppresses the curse.
ܘܡܸܒܿܪܟ ܗܘ ܦܐܖܪ̈ܐ ܕܟܪ̈ܣܟܝ. ܒܘܪ̈ܟܬܐ ܕܝܢ ܡܢܿܒܠܢܝܬܐ ܕܠܘܛܬܐ ܐܝܬܝܗ̇ ܀ ܬܘܒ ܡܫܬܘܫܛ ܗܘܐ ܝܫܘܥ ܒܩܘܡܬܐ ܘܒܚܟܡܬܐ ܘܒܛܝܒܘܬܐ ܇ ܗ̇ܘ ܕܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܗ݈ܝ ܚܟܡܬܐ ܘܝܗܘܒܐ ܕܚܟܡܬܐ ܘܕܛܝܒܘܬܐ ܠܟܠ ܇ ܐܝܟܢܐ ܕܚܢܢ ܒܗ ܐܝܟ ܕܒܪܖ̈ܝܼܫܝܬܐ ܕܬܪ̈ܬܝܢ ܕܓܢܣܢ ܀ ܠܘܬ ܛܝܒܘܬܐ ܘܚܟܡܬܐ ܢܦܲܢܐ ܢܸܣܩ ܇ ܗ̇ܝ ܕܢܦܼܠܝܢ ܡܢܗ̇ ܟܕ ܐܬܬܛܥܝܢܢ ܡܢ ܚܘܝܐ ܀
2 Moreover, Jesus progressed in age, in wisdom, and in grace, he who is Wisdom itself and gives wisdom and grace to all, so that in him, as in the second firstfruits of our race, we might rise, we, again toward the grace and wisdom from which we had fallen, after having been deceived by the serpent.
ܒܡܢܐ ܕܓܝܪ ܥܬܝܕ ܗܘܐ ܢܫܬܘܫܛ ܗ̇ܘ ܕܠܗܠ ܡܢ ܟܠ ܫܘܘܫܛܐ ܇ ܗ̇ܘ ܬܚܘܡܐ ܐܚܪ̈ܝܐ ܐܠܐ ܡܬܥܒܪܢܝܐ ܕܗܠܝܢ ܕܐܝܬܝܗܝܢ ܇ ܗܿܘ ܕܠܘܬܗ ܚܦܝܼܛ ܟܠ ܡܢ ܕܡܫܬܘܫܛ ܒܡܝܬܪ̈ܘܬܐ. ܐܠܐ ܠܡܬܚܘܝܢܘܬܐ ܕܒܩܠܝܠ ܩܠܝܠ. ܘܠܡܬܓܠܝܢܘܬܐ ܕܐܠܗܘܬܐ ܕܝܠܗ ܗܝ ܕܡܣܬܩܡܐܝܬ ܘܠܦܘܬ ܡܸܫܘܚܬܐ ܕܩܘܡܬܐ ܡܬܓܠܝܐ ܗܘܬ ܘܡܬܝܕܥܐ ܆ ܫܘܘܫܛܐ ܩܪ̈ܗ̇ ܐܘܢܓܠܝܣܛܐ. ܡܛܠ ܗܕܐ ܐܦ ܚܲܥܠܛ ܩܘܡܬܐ ܥܡ ܚܟܡܬܐ ܘܛܝܒܘܬܐ. ܟܕ ܐܝܟ ܕܠܡܐܡܪܿ. ܡ̇ܙܥܩ ܆ ܕܐܠܘ ܠܐ ܕܟܕ ܡܛܠܬܢ ܐܬܒܪ̈ܢܫ ܗܘܐ ܬܚܝܬ ܩܘܡܬܐ ܘܙܒܢܐ ܗܘ ܕܠܐ ܣܝܒܘ ܘܕܠܐ ܩܘܡܬܐ ܘܕܠܐ ܙܒܢܐ ܆ ܠܐ ܡܬܐܡܖܪ̈ ܗܘܐ ܕܒܚܟܡܬܐ ܘܒܛܝܒܘܬܐ ܡܫܬ̇ܘܫܛ ܗܘܐ. ܐܠܐ ܡܛܠ ܟܝܬ ܕܒܩܘܡܬܐ. ܡܫܬܘܫܛ ܗܘܐ ܆ ܡܛܠܗܢܐ ܐܦ ܒܚܟܡܬܐ ܐܦ ܒܛܝܒܘܬܐ ܆ ܟܕ ܒܟܠܗܝܢ ܡܒ̈ܝܬܐ
3 Toward what, indeed, was he to progress, He who is far from all progress, the final but impassable term of what exists, He toward whom converges whoever progresses in perfection? But what the evangelist called a progress is the manifestation that takes place little by little and the revelation of his divinity which is revealed and known in relation to age and according to its measure. For this reason he also joined age with wisdom and grace, crying out as it were that if he who is without old age and without age and without time had not been subject to age and time after having become man for us, one would not say that he progresses in wisdom and grace. But because in truth it is in age that he progressed, for


[p. 354] {#po.38.p354}
ܠܗ ܠܐ ܡܫܠܡܝܘܬܐ ܗ̇ܝ ܕܝܠܢ ܇ ܘܕܪ̈ܫܿ ܠܢ ܐܘܪ̈ܚܐ ܠܘܬ ܡܫܡܠܝܘܬܐ ܗܺܝ ܕܝܠܗ. ܘܕܗܕܐ. ܗܟܢܐ ܐܝܬ ܠܗ̇ ܆ ܘܕܐܠܝܗܘܬܗ ܡܕܒܪ̈ܢܝܬܐ ܕܥܡܢܘܐܝܠ ܗܺܝ ܕܒܐܝܼܕܐ ܒܐܼܝܕܐ ܡܬܕܢܚܐ ܗܘܬ ܐܬܐܡܼܪ̈ܬ̇ ܫܘܘܫܛܐ ܆ ܝܕܝܥܐ. ܡܢ ܬܡܢ ܀
this reason (he progressed) also in wisdom and in grace, by appropriating in everything our imperfection and by paving for us a path toward his perfection. That it be so, and that it be the divinity of the Economy of the Emmanuel, which, in its gradual apparition, is called a progress, here is how one knows it.
ܟܕܓܝܪ ܐܡܗ ܝܿܠܕܬ̇ ܐܠܗܐ ܐܡܪ ܐܢܐ ܒܬܘܠܬܐ ܡܖܲܝܡ ܘܝܘܣܦ ܆ ܥܛܦܝܢ ܗܘܘ ܡܢ ܒܬܪ ܥܐܕܐ ܕܦܣܟܐ ܡܢ ܐܘܪ̈ܫܠܝܡ ܘܒ̈̇ܥܝܢ ܗܘܘ ܠܗ ܒܐܘܪ̈ܚܐ ܟܕ ܨܒܝܢܐܝܬ ܦܫ ܡܢ ܒܢ̈ܝ ܠܘܼܝܬܗܘܢ ܒܐܘܪ̈ܚܐ ܆ ܘܬܘܒ ܗܦܼܟܘ ܠܐܘܪ̈ܫܠܡ ܘܐܫܟܚܘܗܝ ܆ ܐܡܪ ܗܘܐ ܠܗܘܢ ܕܠܡܢܐ ܒ̇ܥܝܢ ܗܘܝܬܘܢ ܠܝ܆ ܠܐ ܝܿܕܥܝܢ ܗܘܝܬܘܢ ܕܒܝܬ ܐܒܝ ܘ̇ܠܐ ܠܝ ܕܐܗܘܐ ܐܝܬܝ ܆ ܟܕ ܘܠܐ ܡܕܡ ܐܚܪ̈ܢܐ ܡܢ ܗܪ̈ܟܐ ܡܘܕܥ ܇ ܐܠܐ ܐܢ ܕܚܕܐ ܐܠܗܘܬܐ ܘܡܖܘܬܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܇ ܗܼܘ ܟܝܬ ܘܐܒܐ ܇ ܘܕܗ̇ܘ ܕܒ̇ܶܥܝܢ ܗܘܘ ܐܟ ܕܠܛܠܝܐ ܕܛܥܼܐ ܡܢ ܐܘܪ̈ܚܐ ܇ ܗܢܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܕܫܠܛ ܐ ܥܠ ܟܠܗܝܢ ܗܠܝܢ ܕܐܒܐ ܇ ܘܐܦ ܥܠܝܗ̇ ܕܡܕܝܢܬܐ ܐܘܪ̈ܫܠܡ ܇ ܗܺܝ ܟܝܬ ܕܡܢ ܢܒ̈ܝܐ
4 When his mother, indeed—I speak of the mother of God, the Virgin Mary—and Joseph were returning from Jerusalem after the feast of Passover and they were looking for him on the way after he had voluntarily moved away from his traveling companions, and when they had returned to Jerusalem and had found him, he said to them: Why were you looking for me? Did you not know that I must be in my Father's house? He did not make known anything else by that, if not that He in truth and the Father are one divinity and Lordship, and that the one whom they were looking for as a little boy who had taken the wrong path is the very one who exercises his empire over all the (affairs) of the Father, as well as over the city of Jerusalem, which in truth was nicknamed by the prophets the city of God.
ܡܟ̇ܢܝܐ ܗܘܬ ܡܕܝܢܬܐ ܕܐܠܗܐ ܀ ܘܟܕ ܗܕܐ ܐܫܬܿܥܝ ܠܘܩܐ ܐܘܢܓܠܝܣܛܐ ܆ ܐܝܼܬܝ ܒܬܪܟܢ ܗܺܝ ܕܐܦ ܝܫܘܥ ܡܫܬܘܫܛ ܗܘܐ ܒܚܟܡܬܐ ܘܒܩܘܡܬܐ ܘܒܛܝܒܘܬܐ. ܐܝܟܢܐ ܕܢܐܠܦ ܚܢܢ. ܕܫܘܘܫܛܐ ܩܼܪܐ ܠܡܬܓܠܝܢܘܬܐ ܕܒܐܝܕܐ ܒܐܹܝܕܐ ܕܐܠܗܘܬܗ ܕܝܫܘܥ. ܗܘܓܝܪ ܕܕܝ̈ܠܢܝܬܐ ܐܝܬ ܠܗ ܠܟܠܗܝܢ ܗܠܝܢ ܕܝ̈ܠܢܝܬܐ ܕܐܒܐ ܆ ܐܝܕܐ ܬܪ̈ܒܝܬܐ ܡܩܒܠ. ܒܪܡ ܕܝܢ ܕܡܫܬܘܫܛ ܡܣܬܼܒܪ ܗܘܐ ܠܘܬ ܐܠܗܐ ܘܠܘܬ ܒܢܝ̈ܢܫܐ ܇ ܟܕ ܡܣܼܒܪ̈ܢܘܬܐ ܦܐܝܬ ܠܐܠܗܐ ܗܿܝ ܕܥܠܘܗܝ ܒܩܠܝܠ ܩܠܝܠ ܡܫܬܘܫܛܐ ܗܘܬ ܆ ܗܝ ܕܝܢ ܒܒܢ̈ܝ̈ܢܫܐ ܗܺܝ ܕܒܨܒܝܢܐ ܕܐܒܐ ܡܥܝ̇ܘܐ ܗܘܐ ܆ ܟܕ ܡܿܦܩ ܗܘܐ ܐܟܚܕܐ ܥܡ
5 After the evangelist Luke gave this account, he added then these (words): Jesus also progressed in wisdom and in age and in grace, so that we might learn that he called progress the gradual revelation of the divinity of Jesus. For he who has as his own everything that is proper to the Father, what increase will he receive? And yet he seemed to progress before God and before men when an idea worthy of God progressed then little by little regarding him among men, which he manifested by the will of the Father, when he made appear publicly, at the same time as the time of age, the apparition of his divinity.
ܙܒܢܐ ܕܩܘܡܬܐ ܇ ܠܕܢܼܚܐ ܕܐܠܗܘܬܗ ܀ ܒܗܢܐ ܙܢܐ ܐܦ ܠܘܬ ܝܘܪ̈ܕܢܢ ܆ ܘܠܘܬ ܡܥܡܘܕܝܬܐ ܐܿܬܐ. ܠܘ ܟܕ ܗܼܘ ܣܢܝܩ ܗܘܐ ܥܠ ܕܘܟܝܐ ܆ ܐܠܐ ܟܕ ܠܝ ܩ̇ܕ݁ܡ ܡܿܕܟܐ ܘܡܩ̇ܕܫ ܠܡܺܝܐ ܇ ܘܗܼܘ ܠܗ ܒܗ̇ܢܘܢ ܕܠܬܝܒܘܬܐ ܥ̇ܡܕܝܢ. ܐܟܚܕܐ ܡܢܐ ܇ ܗܘ ܕܥܬܝܕ ܠܡܥܡܕܘ ܒܢܘܪ̈ܐ ܘܒܪ̈ܘܚܐ ܘܠܡ̇ܕܟܝܘ ܡܢ ܚܛܝܬܐ ܘܠܡܫܿܟܢܘ ܛܝܒܘܬܐ ܕܣ̇ܝܘܡܘܬ ܒܢ̈ܝܐ. ܘܕܠܘ ܟܕ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܗܼܘ ܒܣܘܢܩܢܐ ܕܗܕܐ ܆ ܠܢ ܥܶܡܕ ܗܘܐ ܆ ܐܘܕܥ ܝܘܚܢܢ ܟܕ ܐܙܥܼܩ ܠܘܬܗ ܇ ܕܐܢܐ ܣܢܝܩ ܐܢܐ ܕܡܢܟ ܐܬܿܥܡܕ ܇ ܘܐܢܬ ܐܬܐ ܐܢܬ ܠܘܬܝ. ܐܢ ܗܟܝܠ ܗܿܘ ܕܡܿܥܡܕ. ܣܢܝܩ ܗܘܐ ܥܠ ܕܘܟܝܐ ܕܡܢ ܗ̇ܘ ܕܥܡܕ ܆ ܠܐܼ ܣܼܢܝܩܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܗܘܐ
6 It is in this way also that he comes to the Jordan and to baptism, not because he himself had need of purification, but because, for me, in advance, he purifies and sanctifies the waters, and he himself ranks at the same time with those who are baptized for repentance, he who is to baptize in fire and in the Spirit, to purify from sin and to grant the grace of adoption. That he did not have need of it for himself, when he was baptized for us, John made well known when he cried out while addressing him: It is I who have need to be baptized by you, and it is you who come to me. If, therefore, the Baptist had need of (receiving) a purification from the one who was being baptized, it is therefore that He was without need and that he had in himself that which gave him his fullness.
ܗܘ ܕܥܡܕ ܆ ܘܕܐܝܬ ܠܗ ܗܺܝ ܕܡܼܠܝܐ ܗܼܘ ܒܗ ܀ ܢ̇ܚܙܐ ܕܝܢ ܆ ܘܕܡܢܝܐ ܠܘܬ ܗܠܝܢ ܆ ܗܼܘ ܝܫܘܥ ܐ ܡܦ̈ܢܐ. ܫܒܘܩ ܗܫܐ. ܟܿܠܢܐ ܠܗ ܠܡܥܡܕܢܐ ܕܫ̇ܪ̈ܝ ܠܡܥܪܙܘ ܠܡܥܿܠܝܘܬܐ ܘܠܐ ܡ̣ܬܩܪ̈ܒܢܘܬܐ

"Now," he says, "is the time of the Economy, and not of the manifestation," as if he were saying: "What you say is certainly true, but it is not a matter of this present time." For it is in this way that it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness. Now I am filling the full measure of a voluntary humiliation; I am hiding my sublimity of nature and I am fulfilling all righteousness. Now what is this: to fulfill all righteousness? He had to let the practice of the Law rest, not by destroying it nor by legislating in opposition to it, but by making it pale and eclipsing it by the brilliance of the commandments of the Gospel. Indeed, while the Law says: You shall not kill, he commanded: You shall not even be angry, so that in truth, once the anger that gives birth to murder was suppressed, the law that forbade murder would then be superfluous. Likewise also the (word): You shall not commit adultery, is suppressed in advance by the following prohibition: You shall not look (at a woman) with lust.
