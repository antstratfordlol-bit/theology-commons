8 The preacher of the truth personifies the creation itself, wanting to show manifestly the greatness of the reality, and he says that it waits, that is to say that it desires very strongly in its expectation, in relation to the day of the resurrection, which is the manifestation of the children of God and the revelation of the just. Indeed, expectation is called a prolonged observation of what one hopes for, so that the one who waits, so to speak, bows his head and directs his eyes as if already immediately he were seeing what is awaited.
