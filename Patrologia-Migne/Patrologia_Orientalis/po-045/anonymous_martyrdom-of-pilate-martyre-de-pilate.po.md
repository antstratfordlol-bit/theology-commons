### MARTYRDOM OF PILATE
ስምዐ ፡ ኢላጦስ ። "በስመ ፡ አብ ፡ ወወልድ ፡ ወመንፈስ፤ ፡ ቅዱስ ፡ አሐዱ ፡ አምላክ ። ድርሳን ፡ ዘደረሰ" ፡ አቡነ ፡ ቅዱስ ፡ ወብጹዕ ፡ አባ ፡ ህርያቆስ ፡ ዘሀገረ፤ ፡ ብህነሳ ፡ በእንተ ፡ ዘኮነ ፣፡ ስምዐ ፡ ጴላጦስ ፤ መስፍን ፡ በሀገረ ፣ ሮሜ ፡፤ ብእሲቱ፤" ፡ ወደቂቁ፤! ፡ ወበእንተ ፡ ኩሉ ፡ዘረከቦሙ ፡'እምአይሁድ ፡ እኩያን ፡ ወካዕበ ፡ በእንተ ፡ዘረከቦሙ፤፡ ፡ 'ለብጹዓን ፡ ወንጹሓን፤ ፡ አርድእተ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፥ ክርስቶስ፤ ፡ ዮሴፍ ፡ ወኒቆዲሞስ" ። ወረከበ፤ ፡ አባ ፡ ህርያቆስ ፡ ጽሑፈ ፡ በጽሕፈተ ፡ ገማልያል፤ ፡ ወአበዮስ ፡ በእንተ ፣ ተኣምራት" ፣ ወመንክራት" ፡፣ እለ ፡ አስተርአያ! ፡ እመቃብሩ፤! ፡ ለእግዚእነ፤፤ ፣ ወመድኀኒነ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፡ ክርስቶስ ፣ እምድኅረ ፤ ትንሣኤሁ ፤ እምው ታን፤ ፡ በሰላመ! ፣ እግዚአብሔር! ፡ አሜን፤ ።
1 In the name of the Father, of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, one God. Homily that our holy and blessed father, Abba Heryagos (Cyriacus) of Behnasa, preached concerning what was the martyrdom of Pilate, the governor, in the city of Rome, of his wife and of his children and of all that happened to them at the hands of the evil Jews, and also of what happened to the blessed and pure disciples of Jesus Christ: Joseph and Nicodemus. 2 Abba Heryagos found a writing by the hand of Gamaliel and Abayos relative to the miracles and wonders that appeared by the tomb of Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, after his Resurrection from the dead in the peace of God, Amen.
ነበረ፤ ፡ ብእሲ ፣ እምድኅረ ፣ ትንሣኤሁ ፡ ለእግዚእነ ፡ ወአምላክነ ፡ ወመድኅኀኒነ፤ ፡ ኢየሱስ ፣ ክርስቶስ ፤፡ እምውታን?” ፡ ወዕርገቱ ፡ ውስተ ፡ ሰማያት ፡ በውዳሴሁ ፡ ዘኢይትከሀል ፡ ይትነገር ፡ ወዘከመ ፡ ነበረ ፡ "ውስተ ፡ አርያም ፡ በየማነ ፡ አብ!" ። ወእምድኅረዝ ፡ ኩሉ፤፤ ፡ አስተርአየ ፡ ተኣምራተ፤ ፡ እመቃብሩ፤፤ ፡ ቅዱስ፤ ። ወነበረ ፡ ብእሲ ፡ ዘስሙ ፡ ሏላጦስ፤ ፡ መስፍነ፤ ፡ ኢየሩሳሌም፤' ፡ ወብእሲቱ፤ ፡ አብሮቅላ፤! ፡ ወይገይሱ ፡ ኀበ ፡ መቃብሩ ፡ ቅዱስ ፡ ወይጹልዩ ፡ ሀየ፤ ። ወሶበ ፡ ርእዩ ፡ ተኣምራተ ፡ ወመንክራተ ፣ዘያስተርኢ ፡ እምኔሁ ፡ ነበሩ ፤ እንዘ ፤ ይገብሩ ፣ ምጽዋተ ፡ ብዙኀ ፡ 'ለምስኪናን ፡ ወለነዳያን፤" ፡ ወእምኩሉሰ”" ፡ ዘይበዝኅ ፡ ሠናያተ ፡ ትገብር ፡ አብሮቅላ፤ ፡ ብእሲቱ ፡ በእንተ ፡ ተኣምር፥ ፡ ዘርእየት ፡ እንዘ ፣ ሀለወት ፣ ውስተ ፡ ቤታ ፡ ወነበረት ፡ እገዘ ፡ ታስተዳሉ ፡ አፈዋተ ፡ ወተሐውር ፡ ከመ ፡ ታስተሣኒ ፡ መቃብሮ?" ፡ ወት ጸሊቶ ፡ በህየ ። ወሶበ ፡ ርእይዋ ፣ አይሁድ ፡ ከሓድያን ፡ ተማከሩ ፡ በበይናቲ
3 There was a man... - after the Resurrection of Our Lord, Our God and Our Savior Jesus Christ from the dead and his ascension into the heavens, in his ineffable glory, as well as the manner in which he is seated in the high heaven, at the right hand of the Father; after all that, miracles appeared produced from his holy tomb - ... there was a man by the name of Pilate, governor of Jerusalem, and his wife Procula (Abrogla). They went early in the morning to his holy tomb and prayed there. When they saw the miracles and the wonders that were produced through it, they remained, giving many alms to the poor and the needy. Procula, his wife, did good works superior to everything that was great, because of the miracles that she saw. They sent a messenger to Barabbas (Barban), the thief, whom they had had released from prison, and in whose place they had crucified Our Lord Jesus Christ. 4 As soon as he had heard their words, Barabbas agreed with them, especially when he had heard that they would plunder his property. For he was needy, especially in the prison, but he loved gold and silver; in effect, he was the brother of the wife of Judas. For they had a temperament of people loving gold, and the wife of Judas persisted with obstinacy with her husband, for many days, saying: "Speak to your master, so that he may deliver my brother from prison," and for many days Judas spoke of him to the Savior. But the Savior did not want to speak of it, for he knew what would happen, before it occurred. When they heard this, the disciples gave thanks to God, saying: "Glory to you, Christ wonder-worker, for the ages of ages, amen."
