In the second year since Arterkserksês the Great reigned, on the first day of the month of Nisan, Mardokëwos, (son) of lyairu, (son) of Sémyu, (son) of Qêsyu, of the tribe of Benyâm, a Jewish man, saw a dream; and he dwelt in the city of Susâ; and he was a great man; and he was healing in the courtyard of the king's palace. He was one of the captives, whom Nâbukednäâsor, king of Bâbilon, brought from lyarusälêm with Yäkonyu, king of Yehudâ.
