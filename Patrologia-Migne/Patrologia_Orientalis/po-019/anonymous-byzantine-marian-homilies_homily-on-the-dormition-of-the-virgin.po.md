3. Thus she was celebrated from afar. And in truth, that the Blessed one is the fruit of creatures, and that she has this relation to the Universe, is manifest from this: Since, when in all things the fruit is that which restores the nature yielding to non-being to the generator, and shows it new from the beginning, who reformed men? Whence the new creature? Who changed this universe? And indeed, those whom heaven received, the Virgin transferred as certain new citizens from the earth. The earth, however, had as an inhabitant the new man, the Lord of heaven himself; since it did not produce the old fruit of sin, namely thorns and thistles, but the new flower of justice, I mean the Virgin. Not only in this way did she remove old age from the midst and grant to all as it were to revive, but even heaven itself, and the moon and the earth and the stars are to receive a body more beautifully, free from all corruption. For the creature cannot rise again from corruption before the sons of God have attained liberty; and the price of this liberty, namely the firstborn of the dead, the Virgin contributed.
