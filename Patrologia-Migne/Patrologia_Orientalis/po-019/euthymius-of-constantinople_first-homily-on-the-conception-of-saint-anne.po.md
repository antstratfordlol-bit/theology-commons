May you become a consolation to us as we fall from these earthly things. For then you alone are worth something, O queen worthy of all praise; then make the angels propitious to us by your prayers. For you know our fragility entirely and our proneness to evil; you know also those who are against us even now, both invisibly and visibly. Suppress these, and cast them into the ropes of hell; but soothe and reconcile those. For you are the author of peace and the reconciler. To those who unceasingly glorify and invoke you, may you become a physician and nurse and salutary potion for the purification of the soul and the remedy of the body. For after God you can do all things, and in all things your Son and God and the Lord of us all yields to you, as to a mother; since He is rightly and deservedly moved by maternal bowels, as the only just and merciful One, and the One who fills the life of us, your unworthy servants and your most humble inheritance, with mercy and compassion, who through you offer glory to the Father without beginning, and to His co-eternal and co-honored Son, as well as to the life-giving Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of ages.
