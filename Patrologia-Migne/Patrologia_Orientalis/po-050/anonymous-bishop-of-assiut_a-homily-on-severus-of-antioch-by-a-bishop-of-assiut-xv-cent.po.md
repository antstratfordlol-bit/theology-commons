### Prologue
بسم الاب والابن والروح القدس الاله الواحد له المجد نبتدي بعون الله وحسن توفيقه بشرح ميمر قاله احد المومنين بربنا يسوع المسيح له المجد "وهو اسقف مدينة اسيوط ولاجل اتضاعه لم يظهر اسمه! في مديح القديس العظيم في البطاركة المكرم بكل نوع اللابس فضايل الروح القدس الراعي الصالح ناقوس الارتدكسية المجاهد عن الاماثة. المسقيمة القديس. ساويرس. بطريرك مدينة: انطاكية وليس انطاكية فقط بل والعالم كله الذي اكمل سيرته الطاهرة وتنيح في الرابع عشر من شهر
1 In the name of the Father, and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, One God, to whom be glory! We start, with the help of God and His good providence, to explain the discourse delivered by a believer in our Lord Jesus Christ - to whom be glory -, the bishop of the city of Assiut, who due to his humility did not mention his name in praising, the great among the patriarchs, the honoured by every kind of (honour), the bearer of the virtues of the Holy Spirit, the good shepherd, the bell of Orthodoxy, the fighter of the straight faith, the holy Severus the patriarch of the city of Antioch, and not only Antioch but of the whole world, who finished his holy life and rested on the fourteenth day of the month Amsir
امشير. يظهر في هذا الميمركيف كان حضوره في حياته الى صعيد مصر وحلوله ببرية ستنا الست السيدة العذرى الذي تحت الصخرة بجبل مدينة سيوط واقامته فيه اياماً وكيف اوهبته سيدتنا العذرى الطاهرة مرثمريم الدير المذكور وصار على اسمه الى اليوم الناس هذا ويظهر عودته الى الوجه البحري وكيف كانت نياحته بمدينة سخا في بيت دورثاوس الارخن المحب للمسيح وكيف كان انتقال جسده الى دير الزجاج وكيفث كان سبب انتقاله بعد الايام الطويلة من دير الزجاج الى ديره بجبل مدينة سيوط.
2 It is revealed in this discourse how he came during his life to Upper Egypt and dwelt in the desert of our Lady the Madonna, the Lady the Virgin which is under the rock in the mountain near the city of Assiut, and how he stayed there for many days, and how our Lady the pure Virgin Mary granted him the abovementioned monastery, which bears his name among the people up to this day. He tells of his return to Lower Egypt and of his death in the city of Saha in the house of the Christ-loving notable * Dorotheus, and how his body was translated to the monastery of al-Zugag (He explains) the reason for his translation, after many years, from the monastery of al-Zugag to his monastery in the mountain near the city of Assiut.
ومدح في هذا الميمر مدينة انطاكية واولادها الشهدا وبطريركها القديس ساويرس ومدح ايضاً مدينة سيوط وتخومها التي صارت اجسادهم الطاهرة فيها ووضع في هذا الميمر اقولاً نافعة للسامعين تحث على الطهارة والرحمة والصوم
3 He praises in this discourse, the city of Antioch and its native martyrs, and its patriarch the holy Severus. He praises also the city of Assiut and its regions where their holy bodies are kept. He includes in this discourse several useful sayings exhorting his hearers to practise purity, mercy, fasting, prayer and the rest of the virtues taken from the words of the Holy Scriptures.
والصلاة وبقية الفضايل من كلام الكتب المقدسة. ووضع هذا الميمر في ديره الذي تحت الصخرة بحضور الابا الرهبان والشعب المسيحيين المجتمعين في يوم تذكاره الطاهر الذي هو الرابع عشر من شهر امشير واستقرت العادة ايضا ان نعيد له في الرابع عشر من شهر مسرى بسلام من الرب.
4 He delivered this discourse in his monastery under the rock, and in the presence of the fathers the monks and the Christian people assembled on the day of his pure commemoration, which is the fourteenth day of the month Amsir. And also it was customary to hold his commemoration on the fourteenth day of the month Misra In the peace of the Lord!

9 So that is why we forever exalt His name and acknowledge His abundant mercy upon us, for he had pity upon us and made us worthy to assemble in His presence in this pure church for the commemoration of His chosen one, the one who fought in His name, the successor of His disciples and apostles who provided the proofs of the Christian beliefs and interpreted the statements and the meanings of the divine words, the great saint, our father Abba Severus, the Church's bell. 10 Thus, my beloved, today on earth we celebrate a feast for the bell of Orthodoxy, who is feasting with Christ - let His name be praised - in Heaven. We assemble in this holy Church, while he is in the assembly of the church of the first-born, the sublime bride. We remember him on the holy altars for he is worthy to be remembered * in all the Orthodox churches, not only in his jurisdiction of Antioch but also throughout all the jurisdiction of the great city of Alexandria. We remember with our feeble mouths him whom the Lord has made his mouth like a cutting sword and like a piercing arrow against the enemies of the truth, the blasphemous with their wicked mouths. We assemble today for the commemoration of he who has been worthy to join the assembly of the church of the first-born. We recline in the banquet of the master, our father, the great patriarch Abba Severus who is resting in the bosoms of the chief patriarchs, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.
