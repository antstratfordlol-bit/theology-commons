1 In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, one God. We write then the book of the contest of the blessed and holy Peter, which is read in every month of his, the 29th of Hedar before the holy church, (writing) that was spread about him - the blessed patriarch Peter - for the sake of the salvation of the believers. A stumbling block for the heretics, may he glorify him with his mouth. May he protect us, by virtue of the cross of our Lord. Whoever indeed despises and insults him, let him be far from the portion of the saints, for ever and ever, amen. Listen, all of you, community of Christians, with the ears of the heart, and make the commemoration of the death of the blessed Peter, patriarch and last of the martyrs, who died for the name of the Lord. Have you not heard what he said? He made the commemoration of all the saints, when he said of them that they had found grace on the mount of Zion, in the kingdom of heaven; and as a tithe of every month, one hundred and fifty souls are given to him. And they adorn him with an ornament of gems and silver and gold and crowns and garments of light. And may the bread that is prepared for (the meal of) his commemoration be to him the salvation of his soul and his body. And this he heard from afar, and the blessed and holy patriarch made the memory of our Lord.

3 Listen, my holy fathers and brothers, children of the church, men and women gathered: I will narrate to you the spiritual combat of the holy and blessed Peter, patriarch and last of the martyrs: may his prayer and his blessing be with his servant [empty space]. In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, one God; the 29th of Hedar: on this day occurred the martyrdom of Saint Peter, patriarch of Alexandria, last of the martyrs. He is indeed the 17th in the number of the patriarchs. Let us return then to the beginning of the discourse so that we may narrate the story of the birth of the blessed and holy Peter, patriarch and last of the martyrs. And the father of this Saint Peter was the head of the priests of Alexandria, and his name was Theodore, and the name of his mother was Sophia. And they were very God-fearing and had no children. And when it was the 5th of the month of Hamle when it was the feast of the apostles Peter and Paul, the mother of Saint Peter saw many Christians, and their children with them, adorned in their garments while they were going to church. And after he had finished his prayer, the priests and the deacons fell to the ground and said: "Have mercy on us: we pray you and we supplicate you". He took them and raised them and said to them: "Why do you do this?". And they said to him: "We supplicate you for your holiness, since the Lord of us all has called you, in the praise and the glory of eternity, as you have joined yourself to the Son with sufferings, since for the sake of his name you have fought until death for justice, and the crown of martyrdom is prepared for you, blessed Peter. But the blessed patriarch cried out and said to them: "You pray to me for Arius?". 22 Hail to your heels, heels of excellent stability, and also to your two bases, hail, Peter, patriarch and martyr of the dewy blood, assign me behind the Lord on the terrible day of Christ, to stand at your right with you and not at the left (Mt 25:33).
ሰላም ፡ ለአፃብዒከ ፡ ወለአጽፋሪከ ፡ ዳግመ ። አድኅነኒ ፡ ጴጥሮስ ፡ እምዘእፈርሐ ፡ ፍጽመ ። እምጋላተ ፡ ንጉሥ ፡ ከሐዲ ፡ እለ ፡ ይክዕዉ ፡ ደመ ። በከመ ፡ አድኅንከ ፡ በዕለተ ፡ ሞትከ ፡ ቅድመ ። ሰብአ ፡ ሞርሞንቄ ፡ ምስሌከ ፡ ዘገብሩ ፡ ሰላመ ።
23 Hail to your fingers and to your nails again, save me, Peter, from that which, in front, causes fear, from the praetorians of the faithless king who shed blood, as He saved you on the day of your death; before, the Marmarica men made peace with you.
<ሰላም ፡> ለቆምከ ፡ ዘአምጣነ ፡ አዳም ፡ አምጣኒሁ ። ወለመልክዕከ ፡ ቅዱሳት ፡ አባላቲሁ ። ለወልደ ፡ <ማርያም ፡ ጴጥሮስ ፡> ተፍጻሜተ ፡ ሰማዕት ፡ አእላፈሁ ። ነጋድያን ፡ ሐዋሳቲከ ፡ ኀበ ፡ ፍና ፡ ነግድ ፡ ተመርሑ ። መካልየ ፡ ስብሐት ፡ ሊተ ፡ በህየ ፡ ያርብሑ ።
24 (Hail) to your stature which has the same proportions as Adam, and to your sacred effigy, the limbs of the Son of (Mary, Peter), last of thousands of martyrs, your pilgrim limbs were led to the way of pilgrimage, may the talents (Mt 25:20) of praise be fruitful here for me.

26 (Hail) to the funeral wrapping of your body according to the law of the honored bishops, and to your tomb, hail, in the place of Alexandria, last of the martyrs of the blood of justice, amen; it borrowed my voice to pronounce this discourse, give me the rest of the weary in the new time.

27 (Hail) to your struggle, abba, when the gleaning drew near, the crowns, daughters of glory, stood upon your head, they gathered the grape of this effigy of yours from the field of the tongue, for today your senses, daughters of much labor, completely finished their entrusted work.
<ሰላም ፡> ለልደቱ ፡ ዘይትዓፀፍ ፡ ልዴረ ። በሥጋ ፡ ሠርዐ ባሶረ ። ኪያሃ ፡ እንበለ ፡ ቱሳሔ ፡ ደመረ ። አርዮስ ፡ ሶበ ፡ ረሰየ ፡ እምድኅረ ፡ ሥጋዌ ፡ ነኪረ ። አራዘ ፡ ስንዱኖ ፡ ሥጡጠ ፡ ሠሪሪዐባ ነጸረ ።

<ሰላም ፡> ለልደቱ ፡ በከመ ፡ ኃረየ ። ልደተ ፡ ሠናየ ። ኪያሁ ፡ ፈጣሬ ፡ አመ ፡ ፍጡረ ፡ ረሰየ ። እቲዘ ፡ ሥጠተ ፡ ገርዜኖ ፡ ለ[/ርዐ ተርእየ ። እንዘ ፡ ይብል ፡ አርዮስ ፡ ሠጠጠ ፡ ልብስየ ።
