But those who are thoroughly infected with this plague avoid the labor of their hands as if it were an evil, and giving themselves over to sleep, they call the phantasms of dreams prophecies. The leaders of this heresy were Dadoes and Sabbas and Adelphius and Hermas and Symeones and others besides, who did not separate themselves from ecclesiastical communion, because they said that the divine food, of which the Lord Christ said: 'He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood will live forever,' neither profits nor harms. Furthermore, striving to hide their disease, even when convicted, they impudently deny and condemn the very things they are thinking, which they themselves carry in their soul. Letoius, therefore, ruling the church of the Melitenians, a man adorned with divine zeal, seeing that many monasteries, or rather dens of thieves, had drunk in that plague, burned them and drove the wolves from the flock.

For he said that the accusers were slandering and the witnesses were lying. And he kindly called Adelphius, who was advanced in age, and bidding him sit near him, 'We,' he said, 'O old man, who have prolonged our life further, have also learned human nature more accurately, and we have known the machinations of the opposing demons, and we have found by experience the help of grace. But these men, since they are young and understand nothing of these things accurately, do not endure to hear more spiritual discourses. Therefore, explain to me, by what reason do you affirm that both the contrary spirit departs and the grace of the most Holy Spirit arrives?' Deluded by these words, that old man vomited out all his hidden poison, saying that divine baptism brings no utility to those who become participants in it, but that only diligent prayer expels the indwelling demon.
