And Daniel prophesied concerning the little horn that grew, and three of those joined to it fell before it, and it spoke words against the Most High; this is the wicked Antiochus who, in the one hundred and forty-third year of the empire of the Greeks, which is his sixth year, went up to Jerusalem and took away the golden altar and all the vessels of the ministry, and polluted the sanctuary, and built altars there, and having introduced strangers there, he cast out the inhabitants. 4. At that time, when tribulation surrounded, battles were ordered, distresses were constant, and many were the groans: joy fled, hilarity was taken away, peace was driven off, rest was removed, no help, no succor near, pride was elongated, haughtiness was humbled, elation fell, humility became as great as possible, riches were reduced to poverty, the kingdom ceased, death arrived, Hell hastened, with gaping mouth it took away, with extended tongue it devoured, it carried off the glorious and honored into its own perdition, and the wicked and sinners [to itself] for food. It hid the bodies of the righteous in its midst, it crushed the bodies of the impious with its teeth. Hiding them in its bowels, it corrupted the flesh of the perverse and cast them into its dark dwellings.
