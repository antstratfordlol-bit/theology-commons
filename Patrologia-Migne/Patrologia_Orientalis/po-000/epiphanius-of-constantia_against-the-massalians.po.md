Yet they, having arisen from the Gentiles and neither pertaining to Judaism nor being Christians, nor originating from the Samaritans, but being truly Gentiles and asserting that there are gods, they worship none at all, but prescribe veneration to one alone, calling him the Omnipotent. For they build certain houses for themselves, or spacious places like forums, calling these 'proseuchae'.

These Massalians, having arisen long ago from the Gentiles, are prior to those who have recently arisen from the name of Christianity; and these, in certain regions, prepared for themselves such localities as those which are called proseuchae and oratories. Elsewhere, in turn, by nature and similarity making churches for themselves, they gather in the evening and morning time with great illumination and lights; furthermore, they compose certain elegies by the followers of their own flock and truly pious sentiments about God (as elegies and euphemies are wont to be), as if they were going to appease God, they deceive themselves. For ignorance, being blind, prepares all these things for those deceived by the error of their opinion. But before

Wherefore, why do we not rather flee to him, venerate him, and celebrate him, so that on account of our flattering worship he might cease to harm us, or rather, that he might spare us as servants made for himself?' For which reason they called themselves Satanians. We thought their heresy should be joined with those aforementioned, about which we are going to treat next, since in a similar way they come forth into the open, having abandoned the truth, and devote themselves to praying and singing. They are wanderers, and stretch out their hands for alms, just as if they were lacking food and possession. Furthermore, their speeches surpass all dullness. For if you ask one of them from whom he originates, he will confess himself to be such as you might wish.
