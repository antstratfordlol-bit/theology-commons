### XXII
ܒ݁ܗܳܢܳܐ ܙܰܒܢܳܐ ܢܶܒܥܰܬ ܗܶܪܶܣܝܣ ܕܰܡܨܰܠ̈ܝܳܢܶܐ ܒܐܘܽܪܗܳܝ ܒܐܝܺܕܰܝ ܐ݈ܢܳܫ ܕܰܫܶܡܗ ܐܰܘܣܶܒܺܝܘܣ ܘܬܰܠܡܺܝܕ݂ܰܘܽܗ̱ܝ ܣܳܒܳܐ ܘܕܳܕܘܽ ܘܐܰܕܶܠܦܺܝܘܣ ܘܐܰܪܡܳܐ ܘܫܶܡܥܘܽܢ. ܐܶܫܬ݁ܰܡܰܝܘܘ ܕ݁ܶܝܢ ܡܰܥܒܺܝܕ݁ܳܢܘܳܬܳܐ ܕܕܰܝ̈ܘܶܐ ܓ݁ܶܠܝܳܢܳܐ ܕܪܘܺܚܳܐ ܩܳܪܶܝܢ܀
FROM THE CHRONICLE OF MICHAEL THE GREAT, PATRIARCH OF THE JACOBITES. At that time the heresy of the Messalians boiled over in Edessa, authored by a certain man whose name was Eusebius, and his disciples, Sabas, Dadoes, Adelphius, Hermes, and Simeon. They were called Euchites and Enthusiasts; for they called dreams, the operations of demons, the revelation of the Spirit.
