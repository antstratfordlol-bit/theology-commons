### XXX-XXXI
From the acts of the Synod held in the year 585 under the presidency of Iesuiahb.

### XXX. CANON EIGHT
ܩܳܢܘܳܢ ܕܰܬܡܺܝܢܳܝܳܐ܀ܥܰܠ ܐܰܝܠܶܝܢ ܕܥܳܝܛܺܝܢ ܨܠܘܳܬܳܐ ܕܥܺܕ݂ܬܳܐ ܘܳܣܪܺܝܢ ܥܰܠ ܡܰܣܳܒܳܐ
Concerning those who despise the prayers of the Church, and the reception of the mysteries of Christ
ܐ̱ܪܳܙܰܘܗ̱ܝ ܕܰܡܫܺܝܚܳܐ: ܘܰܒܐܶܣܟܺܡܳܐ ܕܰܓܠܳܐ ܕܥܰܢ̈ܘܳܝܶܐ ܓܳܒܶܝܢ ܠܗܘܢ ܘܰܗܘܳܝܳܐ ܕܠܳܐ ܥܘܽܡܪܳܐ ܘܰܡܟܰܪܟܺܝܢ ܥܰܡܗܘܽܢ ܢ̈ܶܫܶܐ ܘܥܳܡܪܝܺܢ ܒܰܚܕ݂ܳܐ
despise them, and in the lying habit of ascetics choose for themselves a wandering life without a domicile, and taking women with them, men and women live indiscriminately in one monastery.
ܕ݁ܰܝܪܳܐ ܓܰܒܪ̈ܶܐ ܘܢܶܫܳܐ ܫܰܘܝܳܐܝܺܬ܀ ܐܶܬ݁ܬ݁ܰܡܰܪ ܗܳܟܝܶܠ ܡܶܢ ܦ݁ܳܪ̈ܽܘܫܶܐ ܩܕ݂ܳܡ ܣܘܽܢܗܳܕ݂ܳܘܣ ܕܐܝܺܬ ܐ݈ܢܳܫ̈ܳܐ ܚܰܕ݂ ܟܡܳܐ ܕܰܠܒܺܝܫܝܺܢ ܐܶܣܟ݁ܺܡܳܐ ܝܰܩܺܝܪܳܐ ܕܰܢܙܝܺܪ̈ܶܐ ܘܕܰܡܣܰܡ̈ܒܪܳܢܶܐ ܘܕܰܒܢܰܚ̈ܬܶܐ ܩܕ݂ܳܡܳܝܳܐ ܡܰܛܥܝܳܢܳܐܝܺܬ. ܘܦܳܗܶܝܢ ܕܠܳܐ ܥܘܽܡܪܳܐ ܘܰܡܚܰܠܕ݁ܝܺܢ ܒܩܘܰܪ̈ܝܳܐܣ ܘܒܰܡܕܺܝܢܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܘܒܳܣܪܺܝܢ ܥܰܠ ܨܰܘܡܳܐ ܘܰܨܠܘܳܬܳܐ ܘܥܰܠ ܡܰܣܳܒܳܐ ܕܐ̱ܪܳܙܳܐ ܩܕ̈ܝܺܫܶܐ. ܐ̱ܚܪ̈ܳܢܶܐ ܕ݁ܶܝܢ ܟܰܕ݂ ܝܰܬܝܰܪ ܡܶܬܕܼܰܥܠܺܝܢ ܒܡܰܠܛܠܣܳܚܙܚܘܵܬܗܘܽܢ: ܡܰܟܪܟܺܝܢ ܥܰܡܗܘܽܢ ܢܺ̈ܫܶܐ ܕܰܠܒܺܝܫܳܢ ܐܶܣܟܺܡܳܐ ܕܰܩܕܺܝܫܳܬܳܐ. ܐ̱ܚܪ̈ܳܢܐ ܕ݁ܶܝܢ ܐܳܦ ܒܕܺܝܪܳܐ ܚܕ݂ܳܐ ܡܰܡܝܡܪܚܝܢ ܘܥܳܡܪܝܺܢ ܫܰܘܝܳܐܝܺܬ ܓܰܒܪ̈ܶܐ ܘܢܶܫ̈ܶܐ. ܘܰܒܗܳܕ݂ܶܐ ܟܽܠܳܗ̇
It was therefore said by discreet men before the synod that there are men who, sometimes fraudulently dressed in the venerable habit of Nazirites and ascetics and religious, wander about without a domicile and, stealthily invading villages and cities, despise fasting, prayer, and the reception of the sacred mysteries; likewise, that others, failing even more because of their error, lead around with them women dressed in the habit of nuns, or indeed that others have dared to live together in one monastery, men and women, and that by that error and dissolution of morals the faithful are scandalized, the unfaithful mock, and the duty of teaching falters.


[p. 285] {#po.0.p285}
ܡܰܛܠܚܳܢܘܽܬܳܐ ܘܪܰܦܝܘܽܬܳܐ ܡܶܬܟܰܫܠܺܝܢ ܡܗܰܝ̈ܡܢܶܐ ܘܰܡܒܰܙܚܚܢ ܠܳܐ ܡܗܰܝ̈ܡܢܶܐ ܘܡܰܠܚܔ݁ܰܪ ܬܘܰܠܡܳܕ݂ܳܐ܀ ܦܰܩܕ݂ܰܬ ܗܳܟܝܶܠ ܣܘܢܗܳܕ݂ܳܘܣ ܘܐܺܝܡܰܬ ܒܥܶܡܠܬܳܐ ܕܐܰܠܳܗܳܐ: ܕܟܽܠܗܘܽܢ ܐܰܝܠܶܝܢ ܕܐܶܣܟܺܡܳܐ ܕ݁ܰܩܕܳܫܳܐ ܠܒܺܝܫܝܺܢ: ܘܰܢܙܝܺܪܳܐܝܺܬ ܘܰܡܣܰܪܩܳܐܝܺܬ ܨܳܒܶܝܢ ܠܡܶܚܳܐ: ܢܶܬܟ݁ܰܪܘܽܢ ܠܗܘܽܢ ܥܘܽܡܪ̈ܶܐ ܝܺܕ̈ܝܺܥܳܐ ܒܒܺܝܬ ܐܰܒܳܗܰܝ̈ܗܘܽܢ ܐܰܘ ܥܰܡ ܩܠܺܝܪܺܝܩܶܐ ܒܓܰܘ ܥܺܕ݂ܬܳܐ: ܐܰܘ ܥܰܡ
The synod therefore commanded and defined by the word of God that to all who have put on the monastic habit and wish to live in the manner of Nazirites and ascetics, distinct dwellings shall be assigned, whether in the paternal home, or with the clergy in the church, or with the monks in the monastery. But if, moved by the desire for learning, they set out for some place where there is more study of the holy


[p. 286] {#po.0.p286}
ܕܰܝܪܳܝܶܐ ܒܓ݂ܰܘ ܕܰܝܪܳܐ. ܐܶܢ ܕ݁ܶܝܢ ܪܶܚܡܰܬ ܝܘܽܠܦܿܳܢܳܐ ܡܙܝܰܥܳܐ ܠܗܘܽܢ ܠܡܺܐܙܰܠ ܠܟܰܪ ܕܝܰܬܝܰܪ ܦܟܬܚ ܕܘܰܪܳܫܳܐ ܕܰܟܬܳܒ݂ܶܐ ܩܰܕ̈ܝ̈ܫܶܐ:
Scriptures, let them be provided by the bishop of the place with letters of recommendation, so that they may the more recall their thoughts from corruption and the wandering that provides an opportunity for sin, and be assiduous in prayer and ecclesiastical fasting and in

the reception of the expiatory mysteries, yoked to the yoke of the Apostolic and paternal Constitutions, and let them be subject, whether as clerics, to the head of the clergy, or as learners, to the prefects of students, or to a decent craft or honest labor. Nor is it permitted to any Christian, by the power of the word of God, to despise fasting and prayer and the reception of the holy mysteries and any ecclesiastical Constitutions whatsoever. Nor is it permitted to anyone to lead a woman around with him or to cohabit with her, whether in a monastery or in a cell, nor is it permitted for women to live in the monasteries of men, nor for men, by the power of the word of God, to dwell in the monasteries of nuns. And if anyone is caught having done otherwise, let him be stripped of the religious garment, which he wore unworthily; and let every Christian who has had association with him and helped him be interdicted and alienated. Let the execration and wrath of God come upon the helper and the helped, nor let reconciliation be given to them until they repent of their lasciviousness and become conformable to the precepts. But women, if

any have approached a monastery of men, or men, if they have visited a convent of women, let them be kept in a safe place, removed from the suspicion of dissolution and dishonesty, for the short time that they are there, namely, a night or one day, as the cause demands, lest the name of God and His doctrine be blasphemed. These matters are to be rectified by the care of the bishops, in that they will either correct them, as sons of obedience, or cast them out, as sons of disobedience.

### XXXI. CANON NINE


[p. 288] {#po.0.p288}
ܒܕܘܽܟܬܳܐ ܙܗܝܰܪܬܳܐ ܕܪܰܚܚܺܩܳܐ ܡܶܢ ܡܣܰܒܪܳܢܘܽܬܳܐ ܕܪܰܦܚܘܽܐܳܐ ܘܕܰܫܟܚܳܪܘܳܬܳܐ ܢܶܬܢܰܛܪܘܽܢ ܙܰܒܢܳܐ ܙܥܘܳܪܳܐ ܕܗܳܘܶܝܢ ܬܰܡܶܢ: ܗܳܢܰܘ ܕܝܢ ܐܰܘ ܚܰܕ݂ ܒܰܘܬܳܐ ܐܰܘ ܚܰܕ݂ ܝܰܘܡܳܐ ܠܦܘܽܬ ܥܶܠܺܠܳܬ݁ܳܐ ܕܩܳܕ̈ܝܳܢ: ܡܶܣܰܬ ܕܠܳܐ ܢܶܬܓܰܝܕ݁ܰܦ ܘܒܗ ܕܐܰܠܳܗܳܐ ܘܝܘܺܠܦܳܢܶܗ. ܘܗܳܢܶܝܢ ܗܳܠܶܝܢ ܒܰܫܩܳܠ ܛܰܥܢܳܐ ܕܐܶܦܝܣܺܩܘܽܦ݁ܶܐ ܡܶܬܬܰܨܳܝܳܐ: ܐܰܘ ܟܰܕ݂ ܝܳܕܥܺܝܢ ܕܡܶܬܛܦܣܳܢܘܽܬܳܐ: ܐܰܘ ܟ݁ܰܕ݂ ܛܳܪܕܝܺܢ ܠܗܘܽܢ ܐܰܝܟ ܕܠܰܒܢܰܝ̈ܗ̇ ܕܠܳܐ ܡܶܬܛܦܣܳܢܘܽܬܳܐ܀ ܩܳܢܘܳܢܳܐ ܕܐܶܫܥܳܐ܀ ܥܰܠ ܕܺܝܪ̈ܳܬܳܐ ܘܥܘܽܡܪ̈ܶܐ ܕ݁ܝܺܚ̈ܝܕ݂ܳܝܳܐ ܕܰܡܦܰܠܗܕ݁ܝܺܢ ܟܢܽܘܽܫܝܶܐ ܥܺܕ݂ܬܳܢܳܝܶܐ ܕܚܰܕܒܫ̈ܰܒܶܐ ܘܰܕ݂ܥܺܐܕ݁ܶܐ ܘܡܰܠܦܝ̈ܢ ܐܰܦܚܘܽܬܳܐ

In every way, therefore, Satan, the hater of our nature, plots to disturb the minds of the simple children of the Church and to lead them into error by the deceitful arts of impostors, so that the delightful appearances of Nazirite life and continence, which are justly practiced according to the laws of Nazirite life by those who are strong in spirit, may be perverted by the hater of the Church, and through men of narrow mind, he may prepare a scandal from them for those within, and laughter for those without.

If anyone, however, has dared to transgress this canon, which is full of chastity, he shall be struck by the anathema of the word of Christ.
