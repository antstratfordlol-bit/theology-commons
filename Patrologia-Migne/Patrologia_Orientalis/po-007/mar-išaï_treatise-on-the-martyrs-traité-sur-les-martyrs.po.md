### TREATISE ON THE MARTYRS

Chapter I. - What was the reason for which this treatise was written? Into how many chapters is it divided? Chapter II. - Why are those who suffered for Christ known by the name of martyrs and confessors? Chapter III. - What does the word commemoration mean? It is not the saints, but we who derive advantage by making their commemoration. Chapter IV. - Why do we make the commemoration of the saints on this Friday? Chapter V. - Where will the souls of the righteous be until their return into their bodies?

Chapter VI. - Why were some of the saints helped in their combats and delivered (from torments), while others were not? Chapter VII. - By venerating the bones of the martyrs, we do not worship them with a cult of latria. Chapter VIII. - Each one, according to the goal he proposes for himself and not only according to his works, will be rewarded and will receive the prize.

called "martyrs and confessors" those who suffered for Christ? And when one has received this answer, one asks: What does the word commemoration mean and if it is the martyrs or rather us, who derive profit from their commemoration. Then, after having had an answer to that, one replies: Why is it on Friday that the commemoration of the saints is made? Then, after having been satisfied with this reply, one seeks where the souls of the righteous will be until their return into their bodies. Then, after having received a good answer, one wants to know why some of the martyrs were helped in their struggles and delivered (from tortures), while others were not. And when one has known that the cause for which the saints were delivered from the combats resides in their human weakness, with which they could not have supported the sufferings, and that the honor that we render to the bones of these martyrs is not a cult of latria, one will understand that each one will be rewarded according to his good will and not only according to his works. Finally, the spirit will make the ship of its word rest in the port of good advice.

Let us remember those who suffer as if we were suffering with them; let us extend compassionate hands to all the miserable: 'I was hungry,' says (Jesus), 'and you gave me to eat.' Let us die to this world in the body of Christ, so that we may give to God fruits of a pure heart, remembering the word of our Lord, who says: 'Whoever will lose his life will save it; but whoever will save his life will lose it.'
