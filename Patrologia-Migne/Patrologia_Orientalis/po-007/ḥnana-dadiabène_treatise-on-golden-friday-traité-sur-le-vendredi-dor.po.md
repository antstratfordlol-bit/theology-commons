What are these beings? It is the human race. And as men are composed of body and soul, in relation to the invisibility of the latter and the visibility of the former, the Creator has given them ears to hear and eyes to see, so that, by hearing which, like the soul, is incorporeal, and by sight which, like the body, is sensible, they can receive a perfect science and have perfect relations among themselves.

Just as creatures are divided into two parts: one sensible and the other intellectual, so the economy of the world, which is the guide of reason, is known to us either by hearing or by sight. To hearing belong the words of God, * who said: "Let such a thing be, and the thing was; I will not do this but that (add to it) the establishment of laws, warnings, exhortations, intimidations, and predictions. To sight belong all the actions that God does in relation to the whole human race or to each of us: such is the change of air, of fire, of time, of stature, of reign and of empire; such are the punishment of Adam, the fear of Cain, the translation of Enoch, the flood, the rain of fire on Sodom, the swallowing up of the Israelites, and the various divine manifestations; in short: if you descend in spirit from the Creator to the creatures, you will see that always in two ways are the works of the Creator and of the creatures done.

One can view this healing from four principal points of view: from the point of view of the day, the hour, the place, and the person. The healing took place on Friday, the day on which Our Lord had suffered: so that for those for whom the passion of Our Lord was an object of doubt, the walking of the impotent man might be an object of faith in his resurrection. The hour of the day was the same as that in which the soul of Our Lord moved to Paradise, that is to say at the ninth hour, so that those who had at that hour the knowledge of the separation of the soul of Our Lord from his body might also at the same hour, thanks to the healing of the lame man, have the consciousness of the return of the same soul to his body.

Knowledge of the truth without good works will be of no use to us; for the demons themselves know the truth: The demons also believe, the Holy Scripture says clearly, and they tremble, and it is certain that they do not believe what they do not know.
