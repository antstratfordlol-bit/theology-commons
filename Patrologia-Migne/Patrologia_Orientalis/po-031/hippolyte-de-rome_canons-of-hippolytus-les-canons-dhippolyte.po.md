Before all things, we speak of the holy and authentic faith in our Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God. And (this), we have established in the faith, and we adhere (to it) in all assurance, and we say, we, that the Trinity, equal and perfect in honor, is equal in glory, and that it has neither beginning nor end, the Word, Son of God, and that He is also the creator of every creature, of the visible and the invisible. This, we have established and we adhere to it truly. And those who have dared to say what one must not about the Word of God, in accordance with what our Lord Jesus Christ said concerning them, we have gathered the greatest number possible, with the power of God, and we have cut them off, because they are not in agreement with the holy Books, the word of God, nor with us, we the disciples of the Books. This is why we have cut them off from the Church, and we have remitted their case to God, who will judge every creature with justice. Those who do not know them, we teach them this without envy, so that they do not fall into an evil death, like heretics (*hairetikos*), but be worthy of eternal life, and teach their children and those who will come after them, this holy faith.

He is not established for the priesthood, but for the diaconate, as a servant of God. He serves the bishop and the priests in everything, not at the moment of the Liturgy only, but he also serves the sick of the people, those who have no one, and he informs the bishop (of them) so that he may pray over them, or give them what they need, or also to the persons who hide, but are in need. ونحب بعضنا بعضا ونكن بحبين لغرباء وبحبين للتعليم ونهرب من كل شريك سوء ونسرع المضي الى عبيد الله ونتعبد معهم لان ارراا قالت لداود هذه عبدتك مستعدة تكون لك عبدة اغسل ارجل عبيدك لكي نغسل نحن ايضا ارجل القديسين لتسمع ممن هو اعظم من داود يسوع المسيح زبنا ان مثالك مبارك+ لانه يجيب لكل واحد ممن حفظ وصاياه جيدا ايها العبد الخير الامين صرت امينا على القليل اني اجعلك على كثير ادخل الى فرح سيدك نكون بحق ان يقول لكل واحد منا الجتمعين باسمه تعالوا يا مباركي أي رثوا الملك امعد لهم من قبل انشاء العالم جعت فاطعمتموني وعطشت فسقيتموني وكنت غريبا فآويتموني وكنت عريانا فكسوتموني وكنت مريضا فعدتموني
Let us love one another, let us be hospitable, let us love teaching, let us flee from every evil companion, let us hasten to go toward the servants of God and let us serve them, for Abigail said to David: "Here is your servant, ready to become for you a servant who will wash the feet of your servants." Let us also wash the feet of the saints; let us listen to the one who is greater than David, Jesus Christ, our Lord—your blessed model—for he will answer each of those who have kept his precepts: "Well done, excellent and faithful servant, you have been faithful over a little, I will set you over much, enter into the joy of your Lord." It is in truth that he will say to each of us, gathered in his name: "Come, blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world; I was hungry, you fed me; I was thirsty, you gave me drink; I was a stranger, you welcomed me; I was naked, you clothed me; I was sick, you visited me; I was in prison, you came to me." He says, and the righteous will answer him and say to him:
وكنت مسجونا فتفقدتموني قال فانهم بببون الابرار ويقولون له يا رب متى رأيناك جائعا فطعمناك والاخر الاتية بعد هذا فعلناه بك فيجيب ويقول الحق اقول لكم اما فعلتموه مع هولاء الاخوة الصغار فانا الذي فعلتموه بي
"Lord, when did we see you hungry and feed you, and the rest that comes after that, (when) did we do it for you?" He will answer and say: "In truth, I say to you, you did it only to these little brothers, but it is to me that you did it."

<Author's conclusion.> Whoever keeps these canons (κανών), may the peace of the Lord and (his) mercy be upon him and upon all the Israel of God, and the enemy will not rest in them, but they themselves will rest with all the saints in the kingdom of our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom is glory to the Father and to the Holy Spirit in the ages of ages. Amen. <Translator's conclusion.> The Canons (κανών) of Saint Hippolytus, Archbishop of Rome, the Apostolic, are finished. In the peace of the Lord, to him praise and glory always, forever.
