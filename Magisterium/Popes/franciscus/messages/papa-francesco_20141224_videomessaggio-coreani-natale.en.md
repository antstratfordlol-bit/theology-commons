# Video message to Korean faithful on the occasion of Christmas (24 December 2014) {#francis-mes-20141224-videomessaggio-coreani-natale}
## VIDEO MESSAGE OF HIS HOLINESS POPE FRANCIS TO KOREAN FAITHFUL ON THE OCCASION OF CHRISTMAS

*Wednesday, 24 December 2014*

## \[[Multimedia](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2014/12/24/videomessaggiocorea.html)\]

*Dear Brothers and Sisters of Korea!*

With great pleasure I convey to you Holy Christmas greetings, remembering with joy and gratitude [the journey I made to your country last August](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/travels/2014/outside/documents/papa-francesco-repubblica-corea.html). The great [celebration in honour of the Martyrs](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/homilies/2014/documents/papa-francesco_20140816_corea-omelia-beatificazione.html), the meetings with young people, but also other moments of the visit remain vivid memories.

I pray to the Lord in order that the light, shining on the world from the Child of Bethlehem, may always be in your hearts, in your families and communities. At Christmas, once again, Jesus draws us to Him with his divine goodness. His presence alone can give man true happiness, for He is capable of rendering life ever new and beautiful.

Dearest ones, I ask you to pray for me, and from my heart I wish you a peaceful and holy Christmas!
