# Video Message to participants in the Laudato si' Inter-University Congress [Argentina, 1- 4 September 2021] (24 August 2021) {#francis-mes-20210824-videomessaggio-congresso-argentina}
## VIDEO MESSAGE OF THE HOLY FATHER FRANCIS TO PARTICIPANTS IN THE LAUDATO SI' INTER-UNIVERSITY CONGRESS

\[ARGENTINA, 1-4 SEPTEMBER 2021\]

## \[[Multimedia](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2021/8/24/videomessaggio-congresso-argentina.html)\]

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

I greet the members of the *Laudato si' *Inter-University Congress, to take place from 1 to 4 September.

I hope that this effort will advance social awareness and consciousness for the care of our common home. The Encyclical *[Laudato si'](https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/it/encyclicals/documents/papa-francesco_20150524_enciclica-laudato-si.html) *is not only a "green" Encyclical, it is also a "social" Encyclical. I hope that this Congress will help its full scope and all its consequences to be seen. I wish you all the best, God bless you and do not forget to pray for me. Thank you.

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
