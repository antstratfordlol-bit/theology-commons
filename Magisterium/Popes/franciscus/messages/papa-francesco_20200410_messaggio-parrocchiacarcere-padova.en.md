# Message of the Holy Father to the Parish of the “Due Palazzi” Detention Centre in Padua (10 April 2020) {#francis-mes-20200410-parrocchiacarcere-padova}
## MESSAGE OF HIS HOLINESS POPE FRANCIS TO THE PARISH OF THE “DUE PALAZZI” DETENTION CENTRE IN PADUA

*Good Friday, 10 April 2020*

*Dear friends of the Due Palazzi parish in Padua,*

I have read the [meditations for the *Via Crucis*](http://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/2020/documents/ns_lit_doc_20200410_via-crucis-meditazioni_en.html) that you donated. I dwelt amid your words and I felt welcomed, at home. Thank you for sharing part of your history with me. God tells of himself and speaks to us within a story; he invites us to listen attentively and mercifully. I want to thank you also because you have scattered your names not into a sea of anonymity, but rather into that of the many people linked to the world of penitentiaries. Thus [in the *Via Crucis*](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2020/4/10/via-crucis.html), you will lend your story to all those who share your same situation throughout the world. It is comforting to read a story in which there are also other stories, not only of detainees, but of all those who are passionate about the world of prisons. Together it is possible. Together. I embrace you. Even though I am sure that Don Marco always reminds you, I ask you: pray for me. I carry you always in my heart. Thank you.
