# Ecumenical Pilgrimage to Geneva: Greeting to journalists on the flight to Geneva (Papal flight, 21 June 2018) {#francis-spe-20180621-voloandata-ginevra}
[ECUMENICAL PILGRIMAGE OF HIS HOLINESS FRANCIS TO GENEVA\
TO MARK THE 70th ANNIVERSARY OF THE\
FOUNDATION OF THE WORLD COUNCIL OF CHURCHES](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/travels/2018/outside/documents/papa-francesco-ginevra_2018.html)

## GREETING TO JOURNALISTS ON THE FLIGHT TO GENEVA

*Papal flight\
Thursday, 21 June 2018*

\[**[Multimedia](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2018/6/21/voloandata-ginevra.html)**\]

## Pope Francis:

Good morning,

I greet you all and I thank you for your work in this trip, which is a journey towards unity, with the desire for unity. Thank you for your work and for all that you will do for the success of the trip.

Thank you!

## Greg Burke:

Thank you, Your Holiness.

\[Addressing the journalists\]: Let’s try to be quick, as it is a moment of greeting.
