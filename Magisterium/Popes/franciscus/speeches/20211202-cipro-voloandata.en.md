## WORDS OF THE HOLY FATHER TO JOURNALISTS ON THE FLIGHT FROM ROME TO LARNACA (CYPRUS)

* Papal flight\
Thursday, 2 December 2021*

## \[[Multimedia](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2021/12/2/cipro-voloandata.html)\]

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

## Matteo Bruni:

Good morning to you all, welcome. Today, Holy Father, there are 77 journalists accompanying you, including several from the countries we will be visiting. We are grateful and happy to be departing with you again. Have a good day, have a good trip!

## Pope Francis:

Thank you! Good morning, and many thanks for your company. It is a good journey, and we will also encounter some sorrows. I hope we can all welcome all the messages we will find. Thank you very much for your company!
