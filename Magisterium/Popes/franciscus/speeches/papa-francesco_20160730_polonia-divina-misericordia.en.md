# Apostolic Journey to Poland: Visit to the Shrine of Divine Mercy in Kraków (30 July 2016) {#francis-spe-20160730-polonia-divina-misericordia}
[APOSTOLIC JOURNEY OF HIS HOLINESS POPE FRANCIS\
TO POLAND\
ON THE OCCASION OF THE XXXI WORLD YOUTH DAY](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/travels/2016/outside/documents/papa-francesco-polonia-2016.html)\
(27-31 JULY 2016)

## VISIT TO THE SHRINE OF DIVINE MERCY

## ADDRESS OF THE HOLY FATHER

*Kraków\
Saturday, 30 July 2016*

## \[[Multimedia](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2016/7/30/poloniadivinamisericordia.html)\]

Good morning to you all!

Today the Lord wants us to feel ever more deeply his great mercy.

Let us never distance ourselves from Jesus! Even if we think that we are the least because of our sins and our failings, this is how he prefers us; this is how his mercy spreads. Let us seize the day that we may all receive the mercy of Jesus.

Let us pray together to the Mother of Mercy. Hail Mary...

\[Blessing\]

And, please, I ask you to pray for me.
