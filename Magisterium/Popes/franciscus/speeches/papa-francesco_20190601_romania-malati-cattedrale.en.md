# Apostolic Journey to Romania: Visit to Our Lady Queen of Iasi Cathedral (1 June 2019) {#francis-spe-20190601-romania-malati-cattedrale}
[APOSTOLIC JOURNEY OF HIS HOLINESS POPE FRANCIS\
TO ROMANIA](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/travels/2019/outside/documents/papa-francesco-romania-2019.html)\
\[31 MAY - 2 JUNE 2019\]

## VISIT TO OUR LADY QUEEN OF IASI CATHEDRAL

## GREETING OF HIS HOLINESS

*Saturday, 1 June 2019*

\[**[Multimedia](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2019/6/1/malati-iasi-romania.html)**\]

I would like to give you all my blessing, with my gratitude for being here.

Thank you for coming! Thank you because you stay with your sick; and thank you to those who persevere with illness, offering it to the Lord.

Now let us pray together to Our Lady, before the blessing.

\[Recitation of *Hail Mary*\]

\[*Blessing*\]

Please, pray for me. Do not forget!
