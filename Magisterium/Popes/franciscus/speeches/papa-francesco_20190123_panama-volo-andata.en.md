# Apostolic Journey to Panama: Greeting to journalists on the flight to Panama (Papal flight, 23 January 2019) {#francis-spe-20190123-panama-volo-andata}
[APOSTOLIC JOURNEY OF HIS HOLINESS POPE FRANCIS\
TO PANAMA ON THE OCCASION OF THE 34th WORLD YOUTH DAY](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/travels/2019/outside/documents/papa-francesco-gmg-panama-2019.html)

## GREETING TO JOURNALISTS ON THE FLIGHT TO PANAMA

*Papal flight\
Wednesday, 23 January 2019*

## \[[Multimedia](http://w2.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2019/1/23/volo-andata-panama.html)\]

## Msgr. Mauricio Rueda:

Good morning. I have the pleasure of presenting to you the new *ad interim *spokesman of the Holy See, Dr. Alessandro Gisotti. I am sure that many of you already know him. So, in this, his first journey as spokesman, let us welcome him.

## Alessandro Gisotti:

Holy Father, good morning. Here we are, for this occasion. Good morning to you all, *y* *Buenos dias a los colegas de Panamá que viajan con nosotros*. Once again we cross the Atlantic, Holy Father, just as on the occasion of your first International trip, for a World Youth Day. *Y la juventud del Papa La espera a Panamá*, the youth of the world! Over to you, Holy Father…

## Pope Francis:

Good morning! It is a pleasure to be here with you. I thank you very much for coming, and would like to thank you in advance for the work you will do.

Today’s flight is the first \[with reference to his international trips\] in which a colleague is missing, one I liked very much: Alexej Bukalov, of *Tass*. A man of great humanity, that humanity that is not afraid of the human, down to its lowest level, and is not afraid of the divine, up to the highest. A man who was capable of making a synthesis in the style of Dostoyevsky. I am sure he will be missed by us all. And now, I invite you to observe a moment of silence and then a prayer for him. Our Father… *Requiem aeternam…*

And now, I greet you, to start this journey.

\*[*Bulletin of the Holy See Press Office,* 24 January 2019](http://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2019/01/24/190124a.html)
