# Apostolic Journey to Turkey: Remarks at the conclusion of the Mass celebrated in the Latin Cathedral of the Holy Spirit in Istanbul (December 1, 2006) {#b16-spe-20061201-ringraziamento}
APOSTOLIC JOURNEY OF HIS HOLINESS\
BENEDICT XVI\
TO TURKEY\
(NOVEMBER 28 - DECEMBER 1, 2006)

## REMARKS OF THE HOLY FATHER AT THE END OF THE EUCHARISTIC CONCELEBRATION

*Latin-rite Cathedral of the Holy Spirit, Istanbul\
Friday, 1 December 2006*

Lastly, I want to thank the whole population of Istanbul and of the other cities in Turkey for the cordial welcome that I was given everywhere. My thanks are especially warm, because I know that my presence in these days has considerably disturbed people's daily lives. I also thank you with all my heart for your understanding and your patience.
