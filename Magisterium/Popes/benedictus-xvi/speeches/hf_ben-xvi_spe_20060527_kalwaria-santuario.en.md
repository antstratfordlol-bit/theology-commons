# Apostolic voyage in Poland: Visit to the Shrine of Our Lady of Kalwaria in Kalwaria Zebrzydowska (May 27, 2006) {#b16-spe-20060527-kalwaria-santuario}
PASTORAL VISIT\
OF HIS HOLINESS POPE BENEDICT XVI\
IN POLAND

## GREETING OF THE HOLY FATHER

## WELCOME CEREMONY

*Kalwaria Zebrzydowska, 27 May 2006*

*Dear (Franciscan) Fathers\
Dear Brothers and Sisters,*

[During his first journey to Poland](http://www.vatican.va/holy_father/john_paul_ii/travels/sub_index1979/trav_poland-1979.htm), Pope John Paul II visited this Shrine and dedicated his address to the topic of prayer. At the conclusion he said: “I ask you to pray for me here during my life and after my death.” Today, I wanted to pause for a moment in the Chapel of the Blessed Virgin Mary and, with gratitude, to pray for him as he requested. Following the example of John Paul II, I also turn to you, kindly asking that you pray for me and for all the Church.

*Blessing in Latin*
