# Letter to Card. Tarcisio Bertone, Secretary of State (2 July 2012) {#b16-let-20120702-cardinale-bertone}
## LETTER OF HIS HOLINESS BENEDICT XVI TO HIS EMINENCE CARDINAL TARCISIO BERTONE, SECRETARY OF STATE

*To my Venerable and Beloved Brother Cardinal Tarcisio Bertone*

On the eve of my departure for Castel Gandolfo for the summer period, I wish to express my deep appreciation of your discreet closeness and of your valuable advice, which I have found particularly helpful in recent months.

Noting with regret the unjust criticisms levelled at you, I wish to express anew my personal confidence in you, which I had already made known once before in a [Letter dated 15 January 2010](https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/letters/2010/documents/hf_ben-xvi_let_20100115_conferma-bertone.html), the contents of which remain unchanged for me.

I entrust your ministry to the maternal intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary, Help of Christians, and of the Holy Apostles Peter and Paul. I am pleased to impart to you, together with my fraternal greeting, the Apostolic Blessing as a pledge of every desired good.

*From the Vatican, 2 July 2012*

## BENEDICTUS PP. XVI
