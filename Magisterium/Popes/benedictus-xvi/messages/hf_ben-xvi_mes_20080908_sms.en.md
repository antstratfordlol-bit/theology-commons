# SMS Message to the young people, fifty days after the celebration of the final Mass on the occasion of World Youth Day 2008 (September 8, 2008) {#b16-mes-20080908-sms}
## SMS MESSAGE BY HIS HOLINESS BENEDICT XVI TO THE YOUNG PEOPLE FIFTY DAYS AFTER THE CELEBRATION OF MASS ON THE OCCASION OF WORLD YOUTH DAY 2008

*Dear Friends,*

Fifty days ago we were together for the celebration of Mass.\
Today I greet you on the birthday of Mary, Mother of the Church.\
Empowered by the Spirit and courageous like Mary your pilgrimage of faith\
fills the Church with life!\
Soon I am to visit France.\
I ask you all to join me in praying for the young people of France.\
May we all be rejuvenated in hope!

*From Castel Gandolfo, 8 September 2008.*

## BENEDICTUS PP. XVI
