# Message to the readers of L’Osservatore Romano on the day of the Beatification of Pope John Paul II (May 1, 2011) {#b16-mes-20110501-osservatore-romano}
## MESSAGE OF HIS HOLINESS BENEDICT XVI TO THE READERS OF L'OSSERVATORE ROMANO ON THE OCCASION OF THE BEATIFICATION OF POPE JOHN PAUL II

I extend to the readers of *L'Osservatore Romano*, to the faithful of Rome and to the pilgrims who have come from all over the world my most cordial welcome on the day of the [Beatification of Pope John Paul II](http://www.vatican.va/special/anniversario_gpii/documents/index_en.htm). May this celebration of faith be a precious opportunity to open the doors to Christ and a pressing invitation to live, with the new Blessed’s generosity, the Gospel of Love.

May everyone receive my Blessing.

*From the Vatican, 1 May 2011*

## BENEDICTUS PP. XVI
