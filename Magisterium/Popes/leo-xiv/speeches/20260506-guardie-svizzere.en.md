# Oath of the Pontifical Swiss Guards (6 May 2026) {#leo14-spe-20260506-guardie-svizzere}
OATH OF THE PONTIFICAL SWISS GUARDS

## GREETING OF POPE LEO XIV

*Audience Hall\
Wednesday, 6 May 2026*

\[[**Multimedia**](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/events/event.dir.html/content/vaticanevents/en/2026/5/6/guardie-svizzere.html)\]

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

At the end of this beautiful and touching ceremony, I would like to extend my warmest greetings to all of you, beginning with the President of the Swiss Confederation and the other civil and military authorities.

With affection and gratitude, I greet the families of the Swiss Guards; thank you for coming!

To you, dear young people who have taken the Oath, I express my esteem and gratitude. The gesture you have made attests to a commitment of fidelity, inspired by youthful enthusiasm and founded on faith in God and love for the Church. I place it under the protection of the Virgin Mary, together with the entire precious service of the Pontifical Swiss Guard.

I wish you all a pleasant evening and a happy celebration!

*Einen schönen Abend und ein schönes Fest!*

*Passez une bonne soirée et une bonne fête ! Merci !*
